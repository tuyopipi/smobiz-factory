PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Bestie LLC (the “Company”), represented by Tsuyoshi Hashimoto, describes below how it handles personal information and user information in connection with Nurevo (the “Service”).

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact information, including email addresses;
  • Business information, including store name, address, telephone number, business hours, business type, location, and price range;
  • Payment-related information processed through Stripe; the Company does not retain card numbers; and
  • Service usage and access information.

2. Purposes of Use

  • To provide, operate, and improve the Service;
  • To publish Business Information as structured data and generate Hosted Pages;
  • To bill and process payments and calculate and pay referral compensation;
  • To respond to inquiries and send important notices; and
  • To improve the Service, analysing in aggregate across sites, AI search appearance measurements (including one baseline measurement taken when a WordPress site is added to the dashboard, before the plugin is connected), records of changes to site settings (for example which outputs were switched on or off and which fields were filled in or cleared, but not the values entered, such as addresses or telephone numbers) and score history, to test which changes the Service can make help businesses appear in AI search (no score is changed automatically as a result).

3. Service Providers and Processors

The Company may share information with the following providers only as necessary to provide the Service:

  • Google (Places API): obtaining business information;
  • Stripe: payment processing and referral payouts;
  • Resend: email delivery;
  • Cloudflare: hosting and data storage; and
  • Perplexity: measuring how businesses appear in AI search (questions that include a business name, category and area are sent);
  • OpenAI: AEO optimization processing for structured data and related AEO improvements.

4. Data Controller

Data controller: Bestie LLC
Address: 3-2-6 Kanda Misakicho, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo, Japan
Email: info@nurevo.jp

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable under data protection law, the Company processes personal information on the legal bases of performing a contract, pursuing legitimate interests, obtaining consent, and complying with legal obligations.

6. Third-Party Disclosure

The Company does not provide personal information to third parties without consent except where required by law or necessary for the providers described above to perform the Service. Business Information may be publicly published as structured data so that AI search engines can read it.

Information entered through a check form or referral page that one of the Company’s sales partners (such as a web agency) places on its own website (for example name, email address and site URL) is handled by that partner as controller; the Company processes and stores it on the partner’s behalf. Please also read that partner’s privacy policy. Email addresses that a partner sets as recipients of monthly reports are used only after the recipient has confirmed them, and only to send those reports and manage unsubscribes.

7. Retention

The Company retains information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes of use and securely deletes it when it is no longer necessary, subject to legal retention obligations.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, and object to processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with your competent data protection supervisory authority.

9. International Data Transfers

Information may be processed by providers or stored on servers outside your country. The Company uses reasonable contractual, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the transfer and applicable law.

10. Cookies

We use essential session cookies only for functions such as maintaining sessions. We do not use third-party analytics.

11. CCPA Notice

For California residents, the categories of personal information we may collect include identifiers and contact information, commercial or transaction information, internet or other electronic network activity information, and professional or business information. Subject to the CCPA, you may request to know the personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of certain uses or disclosures. We do not sell or share personal information.

For privacy inquiries, contact: info@nurevo.jp
