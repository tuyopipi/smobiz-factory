Bing Places for Business setup for Copilot and Bing: importing from Google, which way sync overwrites, and fixing duplicates

Bing Places for Business is Microsoft's free service for managing the business listing shown in Bing and Bing Maps. If you already have a Google Business Profile (GBP), importing it is the fastest route, and once sync is on, GBP overwrites Bing, so the two places you edit are GBP and your website. Microsoft says it is deepening integration with Copilot, but it has not published how today's Copilot answers use Bing Places. That is why the most reliable preparation is keeping your name, address, phone number and hours identical in Bing Places, GBP and your site.

Written November 24, 2026 (JST). Bing Places behaviour was checked that day on Microsoft's official blogs and in answers from Microsoft staff on Microsoft Q&A. Bing Places' own screens and help pages could not be read in our fetch that day, so screen names and steps may have changed. Follow what the screen shows when you sign up.

No listing can guarantee that Copilot or any other AI will show your business or rank it higher. AI answers change from day to day. This guide is about preparing your facts so AI can describe your business correctly.

What Bing Places is, and what the October 2025 rebuild changed

On October 3, 2025, Microsoft announced a rebuilt Bing Places for Business (Bing Search Blog). It is a free service to create and manage the business listings that appear in Bing search results and Bing Maps.

Since the mechanism is not published, betting on any one field makes less sense than keeping your basic facts the same wherever they appear. Copilot also uses web pages as sources for its answers, so your website matters just as much.

Setup: find an existing listing, import, verify

  1. Sign in at bing.com/forbusiness with a Microsoft account.
  2. First search for your business. Bing can create listings automatically from public data, and creating a new one on top of that causes duplicates.
  3. If you use GBP, choose "Import from Google", sign in with the GBP account and pick the location. A Microsoft staff answer on the forum says the import "could take up to two days" (Microsoft Q&A, Nov 2025).
  4. After importing, check the category. A 2023 support answer said categories are not imported and must be set by hand (Microsoft Q&A). This may have changed with the 2025 rebuild, so check on screen.
  5. Without GBP, enter name, address, phone, category, hours and website by hand and verify ownership with the method offered. Which methods appear (phone, email, a mailed postcard and so on) depends on the country and the business details.
  6. Work through the Recommendation Tool's suggestions (photos, hours, social links) and finish by checking that everything matches your site.

The material we could read did not say whether every country's addresses are supported or which verification methods are offered where. In France, one owner reported that the only option was a postcard that never arrived; Microsoft staff suggested trying the Google import and, failing that, contacting support, which is the only team that can publish a listing manually (Microsoft Q&A, contact support).

Limits such as up to 100 photos and up to 10 categories are given in Whitespark's guide (updated 2025-10-30), based on Bing's help pages (Whitespark, secondary source).

Syncing with GBP: decide which way overwrites

A 2023 answer from a Bing Places support specialist spells out the direction of sync (Microsoft Q&A). It predates the rebuild, so details may have changed, but the principle is still useful.

Item2023 support explanationWhat to do
Sync methodManual, or automatic (weekly, biweekly or monthly)Use automatic if your hours change with seasons or holidays
Overwrite directionGBP data overwrites the Bing listing. A Bing edit that conflicts with GBP becomes the GBP value, whichever is newerWhile syncing, edit GBP, not Bing
Bing to GoogleYou cannot update GBP from the Bing dashboardTreat GBP and your site as the two sources of truth
Time to publishThe standard publish cycle normally lasts around ten days, and each verification or edit restarts itBatch urgent changes instead of editing repeatedly
AddressBing requires an address; GBP listings without one (service-area businesses) are flaggedService-area businesses should check how the address is handled

For a business that syncs, Bing Places is a copy of GBP. When you find an error, fix GBP and your site first, then let sync carry it to Bing. Without sync, you must fix GBP, Bing and the site separately, and drift is much more likely.

When you find a duplicate or a listing you did not create

  1. Search Bing Maps by name, phone and address and count how many listings exist for your business. Look for pre-move addresses and old names too.
  2. Microsoft staff say a duplicate was "likely created automatically from a different data source" (Microsoft Q&A, Jun 2026).
  3. Do not verify the duplicate. Owning both makes it harder to tell which one is correct.
  4. Send support the details of both listings (the one to keep, such as the Google-synced one, and the one to remove), proof of ownership, your GBP link and screenshots, and ask for a merge or removal. Staff say you can chat with support after choosing region and language.
  5. Also report the duplicate through "Suggest an edit" or "Feedback" on the Bing Maps listing. Staff describe this as something that "sometimes works", so it is not a guarantee.

What to match: Bing Places, GBP and your site side by side

AI reads several sources. When the same business is described differently in different places, which version it gives can waver. Make the items below identical in all three places.

WordPress steps: match the facts on your site

  1. Write your name, address, phone and hours as text, not an image, on your About or Access page, using exactly the wording you entered in Bing Places and GBP.
  2. Check the name, address and phone your SEO plugin or theme puts into structured data. Free tiers often leave out the address and hours. See SEO plugins and local business structured data.

  4. Make sure Bing can read your site: in Settings > Reading, "Search engine visibility" should be unchecked, and /robots.txt should not block Bingbot.
  5. When hours change, update the site and GBP (and Bing Places too if you do not sync) on the same day and note the date.

How to check after setup

  1. Search your business name on Bing and confirm the Bing Maps listing shows the same name, address, phone and hours as your site.
  2. Ask Copilot questions like "a [business type] in [area] that [condition]" and "What are [business name]'s opening hours?", and record the answer and its sources. Answers change from day to day, so ask the same questions on the same day each month.
  3. In Bing Webmaster Tools' AI Performance, see how often your pages were sources in Microsoft's AI and for which phrases.
  4. In your analytics, look at visits referred by copilot.microsoft.com.
  5. ChatGPT and Perplexity work separately from Bing. Record them with a fixed set of questions, as in how to measure AI visibility. Checklist

  1. Searched bing.com/forbusiness for an existing listing
  2. Imported from GBP, or registered by hand and completed verification
  3. Confirmed the category is set
  4. Decided and noted whether you sync, and that GBP and the site are the sources of truth
  5. Searched Bing Maps for duplicates, old addresses and old names
  6. For any duplicate, asked support to merge or remove it instead of verifying it
  7. Name, address, phone, hours, category and URL match across Bing, GBP and the site
  8. Structured data values match the text on the site
  9. Confirmed robots.txt does not block Bingbot
  10. Recorded Copilot questions and answers with dates

FAQ

Is Bing Places free?

Yes. Microsoft describes Bing Places for Business as a free service to create and manage listings in Bing and Bing Maps.

If I have a Google Business Profile, do I still need Bing Places?

Your GBP does not appear in Bing Places automatically. Use "Import from Google" in Bing Places to create a listing from it. With sync on, later GBP changes also reach Bing.

I fixed something in Bing Places and it changed back.

With GBP sync on, conflicting fields are overwritten with GBP's values, as a Bing support specialist explained in 2023. While syncing, fix the GBP side.

How long do changes take to show?

A 2023 support answer said the publish cycle normally lasts around ten days and restarts with each edit. In 2025, staff said a Google import could take up to two days. Check the screen for current timing.

Does Copilot answer from Bing Places data?

Microsoft has announced deeper integration with Copilot but has not published how today's answers use Bing Places. Keeping your facts identical in Bing Places, GBP and your site is the reliable preparation.

Can businesses in every country register?

The Microsoft material we could read lists neither supported countries nor verification methods by country. Check on the sign-up screen whether your country and address can be selected and which verification methods appear.

Can I delete a duplicate listing myself?

Microsoft forum staff point to Bing Places support. Send details of the listing to keep and the one to remove, plus proof of ownership, and ask for a merge or removal. Do not verify the duplicate.
