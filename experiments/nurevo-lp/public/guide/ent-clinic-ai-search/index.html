AI search for ENT clinics: how to publish pollen-season booking, sublingual immunotherapy and hearing aid information so AI reads it correctly

The single most useful thing an ENT (ear, nose and throat) clinic can do for AI search is to state, in dated text on each service page, when, for whom and under what conditions a service is available. ENT clinics change how they take patients during pollen season, sublingual immunotherapy can only be started at certain times, and hearing aids split the work between the clinic and a shop, so AI tools easily mix in out-of-date notices or another business's details. Then say from what age you see children, follow the medical advertising rules, and publish the same facts in your structured data, llms.txt and Google Business Profile.

Written November 19, 2026 (JST). The examples come from Japan, where Nurevo is based. Immunotherapy start timing (a guideline committee of 日本耳鼻咽喉科免疫アレルギー感染症学会), insurance coverage (Japan Medical Journal), drug approvals and ages (a Torii Pharmaceutical release and Nikkan Yakugyo), supply (Yakuji Nippo), the hearing aid tax deduction (National Tax Agency) and the hearing aid advisor and certified shop schemes (prefectural guides), and the schema.org types (schema-dts 2.1.0 type definitions) were checked that day. The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) medical advertising Q&A items are as checked on November 16, 2026. Outside Japan, insurance, tax and advertising rules differ: check your own country's rules.

AI answers change by day and by how a question is asked. This guide makes your clinic's facts readable to AI. It does not guarantee that you will appear in AI answers or rank higher. It is not legal or medical advice. Check drug details against the package insert.

What patients ask AI about ENT clinics

ENT questions are not only about symptoms. They carry season, age and process. Parents ask about a child's ear pain or runny nose, and adult children ask about a parent's hearing. Six question types come up most.

Question typeExampleWhat AI uses to answer
Area × symptom × time"An ENT near X station that sees children with ear pain on Saturday afternoon"Hours by day, last check-in time, appointments or queue, ages seen
Pollen season"An ENT where I can just get hay fever medicine" "Is it busy in February?"Seasonal check-in rules, online check-in, busy times (as the clinic's own guidance)
Sublingual immunotherapy"When can I start cedar pollen immunotherapy? From what age for kids?"Cedar vs dust mite, when you can start, ages, visit frequency, whether new patients are taken
Hearing and hearing aids"Should I see an ENT before buying a hearing aid? Is it tax deductible?"Hearing tests offered, whether a hearing aid advisor doctor is on staff, the shop relationship, documents issued
Dizziness and tinnitus"An ENT that tests for dizziness" "Which doctor for ringing in the ears?"Names of tests offered, whether booking is needed, how long it takes
Surgery and referral"An ENT that does day surgery on the nose" "Where do I get tonsil surgery?"Which operations are done in-house and which are referred, referral destinations, inpatient or not

In every case, AI can only use pages that state the conditions in text. Hours shown only as an image, a notice that just says "busy during hay fever season", or an immunotherapy page with no start timing are unlikely to be cited.

Write seasonal changes with start and end dates

ENT clinics often switch to online check-in only or close check-in earlier during pollen season. If a notice says "until further notice" and stays up after the season, AI may still answer "online check-in only" the following summer. Always give seasonal changes a period.

What to writeExampleAvoid
The period"From February 1 to April 10, 2027, morning check-in is online only""Online only during pollen season", "until further notice"
What stays the same"Opening hours and closed days are unchanged"Listing only the change, not what stays the same
AfterwardsRemove the notice or mark it "ended" once the period is over, and take it out of llms.txtLeaving ended notices online
Phone enquiriesPhone hours and what staff can answer by phone (such as availability)Only "please call us"

Keep your regular hours on a fixed page as a text table of days and times, separate from notices. Not mixing seasonal exceptions into it helps AI tell the normal hours from the exceptions.

The immunotherapy page: start timing, ages and intake

In Japan, sublingual immunotherapy has been covered by insurance since 2014 for cedar pollen allergy and dust mite allergic rhinitis. Japan Medical Journal (Japanese). The questions AI hears most are "when can I start?" and "from what age for children?". The allergic rhinitis guideline committee of Japan's ENT immunology, allergy and infection society (日本耳鼻咽喉科免疫アレルギー感染症学会) says cedar treatment should be started outside the pollen season, and that dust mite treatment has, in general, no start-time restriction (document as of July 2023). Committee answers (PDF, Japanese).

On ages: the cedar pollen tablet (シダキュア) was approved in September 2017 and announced as Japan's first sublingual tablet usable in both adults and children (the clinical trial enrolled ages 5 to 64). Torii Pharmaceutical release (PDF, Japanese). The dust mite tablet (ミティキュア) received an additional approval for children in February 2018, removing the earlier "12 and over" restriction. Nikkan Yakugyo (Japanese). The age at which a child actually starts depends on the clinic and the examination, so write your own practice: "we start from age X, decided at the consultation".

Supply matters too. Yakuji Nippo reported that the cedar tablet has been on limited shipment since 2023 because of rising demand, with shipments planned to increase in stages from autumn 2025. Yakuji Nippo, February 14, 2025 (Japanese). If you have paused new patients or have a waiting list, say so with a date, and update it when you reopen.

  1. The treatment name and whether you treat cedar, dust mite or both (say so if only one).
  2. When patients can start: for example, cedar outside the season and dust mite at any time, as your clinic's practice.
  3. Ages: from what age you offer it, and whether a parent must attend.
  4. Process: consultation and tests to confirm the allergy, whether the first dose is taken at the clinic, visit frequency.
  5. Length: the treatment length your clinic advises (one hospital's leaflet says three years or more is recommended. Leaflet (PDF, Japanese)).
  6. Cost: that it is insured care; if you give an out-of-pocket estimate, state the conditions (co-payment rate, days of medicine).
  7. Whether you are taking new patients, and the date that was last updated.

Hearing and hearing aids: keep the clinic and the shop apart

Prefectural guides and a regional branch of the national ENT society advise having an ENT examination before buying a hearing aid. In Japan, a hearing aid advisor doctor (補聴器相談医) is commissioned by the Japan Society of Otorhinolaryngology-Head and Neck Surgery, which publishes a list on its site. Sales and fitting are done by certified hearing aid technicians and certified hearing aid shops, certified by the Association for Technical Aids. Shiga Prefecture guide (Japanese), Shimane Prefecture guide (Japanese), Hearing aid advisor doctor list (Japanese).

AI is also often asked about the tax deduction. According to the National Tax Agency (April 16, 2018), when a hearing aid advisor doctor certifies, using the "information document on hearing aid fitting (2018)", that a hearing aid is directly necessary for medical care, its cost qualifies for the medical expense deduction, limited to the part that is not significantly above the usual level. National Tax Agency (Japanese). What a clinic can state is whether it has a hearing aid advisor doctor and can issue this document. The tax office decides whether the deduction applies, so do not write "you will get the deduction".

  1. On the hearing page, list the hearing tests, how long they take and whether booking is needed.
  2. If a hearing aid advisor doctor works at the clinic, give their name and days. If not, do not say so.
  3. State whether you can issue the information document and what it costs (if self-pay).
  4. If you work with a hearing aid shop inside or near the clinic, say whether it is the same business or a separate one. If separate, give its own hours, phone number and prices.
  5. Say that disability benefits for assistive devices are handled by the local municipality, and do not quote amounts.

Children: from what age, and who comes with them

ENT clinics see many children for ear infections and runny noses. When a parent asks AI for "an ENT that sees babies", a page with no age information means AI either drops the clinic or guesses.

  1. From what age you see children ("from birth", "infants by arrangement" and so on).
  2. Whether a parent must attend, and whether older children can come alone (with an age guide).
  3. What to bring, as the clinic's guidance (medical cost subsidies differ by municipality in Japan, so name the scheme and point to the local office).
  4. Whether you offer immunotherapy to children and from what age (the same value as in the section above).

Where common AI advice collides with medical advertising rules

AI search guides often say "get more reviews" and "show results". On a medical website in Japan, follow the MHLW medical advertising guidelines and their Q&A.

Common adviceHow it applies to clinics (MHLW Q&A)What to do instead
Publish patient testimonialsTestimonials about treatment content or effect cannot be published (Q1-18, Q2-9)Explain the facts in detail: process, length, visit frequency, cost conditions
Show results such as "cures hay fever" or "restores hearing"Claims of treatment effect are restricted; before/after photos count as effect claims (Q2-8, Q3-14)Check what you can say in the MHLW case book and with your public health centre
"No. 1 in the area", "the ENT with no wait"False, exaggerated or comparative claims are treated as prohibited examplesState only what you can as fact, such as check-in method and times
Describe self-pay tests and surgery in detailFor self-pay care, the content and cost, main risks and side effects, and a contact point that answers questions must be given (Q6-10 to 6-12)Put the fee and main risks on the same page, meeting those conditions

Sources: MHLW medical advertising guidelines Q&A (PDF, Japanese), MHLW case book (PDF, Japanese). Keep FAQs to factual questions such as "When can I start cedar immunotherapy?" and "Can you issue the hearing aid document?".

Structured data: the Otolaryngologic type, and the shop kept separate

schema.org has a type for ENT practices (checked in the schema-dts 2.1.0 type definitions on November 19, 2026).

Adding medicalSpecialty

Nurevo AEO does not output medicalSpecialty on its own. To add it, use the filter that changes the business structured data (webmcp_canary_business_node) in your theme's functions.php or a small plugin.

add_filter('webmcp_canary_business_node', function ($org, $settings) {
    // Add only specialties your pages describe (drop Pediatric if you do not see children)
    $org['medicalSpecialty'] = array('Otolaryngologic', 'Pediatric');
    return $org;
}, 10, 2);

Pre-publish checklist

  1. Hours by day and last check-in times are on a fixed page as text.
  2. Seasonal check-in changes have a start and end date. Ended notices are removed or marked "ended" and taken out of llms.txt.
  3. The immunotherapy page states cedar, mite or both, when patients can start, ages, and whether new patients are taken, with the update date.
  4. The hearing page lists tests, whether a hearing aid advisor doctor is on staff, and whether you can issue the document and at what cost.
  5. If the hearing aid shop is a separate business, its hours, phone and prices are given separately.
  6. Ages seen and the rule on parents attending are stated.
  7. No testimonials, promised results, superlatives or comparisons in the page text, FAQs or structured data.
  8. The structured data type is Otolaryngologic and prices match the page. Insured out-of-pocket costs are not entered as fixed prices.
  9. Name, address, phone and hours match across structured data, Google Business Profile and the official medical directory. Clinic basics
  10. llms.txt lists the services, immunotherapy, hearing and access pages. What is llms.txt

FAQ

Where should an ENT clinic start with AI search?

Put hours by day and last check-in times on a fixed page as text, and publish seasonal changes as dated notices. Then add the conditions to your immunotherapy, hearing and children pages, and make your name, address, phone and hours match across structured data and Google Business Profile.

How should we describe immunotherapy start timing?

Write cedar and dust mite separately. A guideline committee document in Japan says cedar treatment should start outside the pollen season and dust mite treatment has, in general, no start-time restriction. Then state, with a date, when your clinic takes new patients.

Can we say from what age children can start immunotherapy?

Yes, as your clinic's practice. In Japan the dust mite tablet's "12 and over" restriction was removed in 2018, and the cedar tablet was approved for adults and children. Also say that the starting age is decided at the consultation.

What can a clinic website say about the hearing aid tax deduction?

Whether a hearing aid advisor doctor is on staff, whether you can issue the information document on hearing aid fitting (2018), and its cost. The tax office decides whether the deduction applies, so do not promise it; link to the National Tax Agency's information.

Which structured data type should we use?

schema.org's Otolaryngologic is the type for ENT practices; it is a MedicalBusiness and a LocalBusiness. If you see children, you can add Pediatric as a medicalSpecialty. If the hearing aid shop is a separate business, keep it out of the clinic's details.
