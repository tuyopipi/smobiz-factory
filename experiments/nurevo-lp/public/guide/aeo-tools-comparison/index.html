AEO tools compared: monitoring tools vs implementation tools, and how to choose

AEO tools come in two kinds: monitoring tools that measure whether ChatGPT, Google's AI and others mention you, and implementation tools that publish your business facts in a form AI can read. Measuring a site AI cannot read tells you little, so for most small businesses the practical order is to fix the implementation first (much of it is free), then pick a monitoring tool by how many AI platforms you need to track and your budget.

AI answers also change from day to day. No tool can guarantee that an AI will mention you or rank you anywhere.

How to choose an AEO tool: five questions

  1. Can AI read your site yet? If your structured data, llms.txt and AI crawler access are not in place, implement before you measure. The free AI readability check shows where you stand.
  2. Which AI platforms matter to you? Whether your customers ask ChatGPT or read Google's AI Overviews decides which platforms you need, and platform count drives price.
  3. How many questions do you need to track? The number of prompts, such as "best hair salon in Austin", decides the plan. Start with a few questions that matter and grow from there.
  4. Do you already pay for an SEO suite? If you use Semrush or Ahrefs, their AI visibility features may be the lowest-effort option.
  5. Budget, currency and who uses it. Check the billing currency, who will read the reports, and whether you need to manage many sites at once (agencies).

Reading the numbers from monitoring tools

AI answers vary between days and between runs of the same question. Monitoring figures are samples from a set of questions asked a few times, not a view of every answer. Compare trends measured with the same questions, platform and settings, and do not act on a single run.

A tool also cannot tell you for certain why you are missing from an answer. Keep improving what your site publishes (implementation) and checking whether you appear (monitoring) side by side.

FAQ

Are there free AEO tools?

Yes. Nurevo AEO is free on WordPress.org, and its free plan includes the AEO score, structured data, llms.txt and AI crawler rules. Several monitoring tools offer free trials (for example 14 days for Otterly.AI and 7 days for Profound, as of October 2026). Nurevo AEO on WordPress.org

Is a monitoring tool enough on its own?

A monitoring tool tells you how you appear today, but it does not change your site. If your structured data or llms.txt are missing, you need implementation as well.

How are AEO tools different from SEO tools?

SEO tools deal with rankings in search results; AEO tools deal with whether AI answers cite or mention you. You need both: AEO does not replace SEO. AEO vs SEO vs GEO

Will an AEO tool guarantee that AI shows my business?

No. AI answers change daily and no tool can guarantee a mention or a position. Tools help you make your site easy for AI to read and see how that changes over time.
