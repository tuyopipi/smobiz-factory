AI search for tutoring centers and cram schools: get grades, courses, fees and trial lessons read correctly

AI search optimization (AEO) for a tutoring center comes down to publishing the grades you teach, your courses, the class format (group, one-to-one, online), the full cost, the timetable, how to book a trial lesson and where each location is, accurately and consistently, in your page text and in structured data. That gives ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI features what they need to answer a parent's question such as "a tutor near the station who can help my 8th grader with math one-to-one" without guessing. Pass rates and "No. 1" claims are not a lever for AI; they are facts to state accurately, with their basis.

Written November 12, 2026 (JST). Information from Google, schema.org and Japanese regulators was checked on their official pages on that date.

AI answers vary from day to day and with how a question is asked. The steps here make your center's facts easy for AI to read correctly; they do not guarantee that you will appear in AI answers or rank higher. Legal points are general explanations, not legal advice; check the rules that apply where you operate.

What parents and students ask AI

Questions about tutoring combine place, grade, subject, goal, format, time and price: "a tutoring center near the station for an 8th grader who struggles with math, one-to-one", "group cram school versus private tutoring for high school entrance exams, and what each costs", "somewhere my child can go at 8 pm after club practice", "online test prep for an English exam". ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews read pages on the web and put answers together from them.

AI can use only facts it can read as text and that do not contradict each other. If your fee table is only an image, if the only price information is "contact us", if class times differ between location pages, or if there is no link to the trial-lesson form, AI has little to work with, and anything it does say about you is more likely to be wrong. In areas where AI answers lean on large chains, an independent center has all the more reason to state its own facts clearly.

Structured data types: EducationalOrganization and Course

schema.org has no type specifically for tutoring centers. The closest fit for the center itself is EducationalOrganization. It sits under Organization and under CivicStructure, not under LocalBusiness, which hair salons and dental clinics use. You can still give an address, phone and opening hours, but don't assume it is treated as a local business listing. Some centers that want to present themselves as a local business declare both LocalBusiness and EducationalOrganization; either way, the markup has to match the facts shown on the page.

Courses are described with Course: a name, description, provider, educationalLevel and offers for the price. Days, times and whether a course is in person or online belong to a CourseInstance, linked with hasCourseInstance, through courseMode, courseSchedule and courseWorkload.

How Google displays this has changed. In June 2025 Google announced it would phase out seven search features, including Course Info, which showed course details. Google Search Central blog, June 12, 2025. The documentation for the course list, which shows three or more courses side by side, is still published as of November 12, 2026 (Google: Course list), but nothing guarantees it will be shown. It is safest to treat structured data not as decoration for search results but as a way to hand your facts to AI and search engines without ambiguity.

Your fees page and consumer law: what you write is what AI repeats

Rules on tutoring contracts differ by country. In Japan, a tutoring-school contract that runs longer than two months and costs more than ¥50,000 in total (enrollment fee, tuition, materials and so on) is a "specified continuous service" under the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions. The school must give a summary document before the contract and a contract document after it; the customer can cool off within eight days of receiving the contract document; and if the customer cancels midway, the most the school can charge is ¥11,000 before lessons start, or, after they start, the fee for lessons already given plus the lower of ¥20,000 or one month's tuition. Home tutoring is a separate category with different caps. Consumer Affairs Agency: specified continuous services (Japanese)

When AI is asked about price and picks up only the monthly tuition, parents feel misled later. List everything a year costs on your fees page, including enrollment, materials, seasonal courses and mock exams, and give a worked total by grade and course. If you have a page on cancellation and cooling-off, link to it from the fees page so AI can answer "can we stop partway?" with facts.

Be careful with wording that makes prices look lower. On March 2, 2023, a Japanese order under the Act against Unjustifiable Premiums and Misleading Representations was issued over an individual-tutoring school's display of hourly tuition that made the price look more favorable than it was. Japan Fair Trade Commission press release, March 2, 2023 (Japanese). AI summarizes what you publish, so a misleading display spreads through AI answers too.

Pass rates and "No. 1" claims: state the basis

Parents often ask about results, but a number without a counting method can't be compared, by parents or by AI. In 2011, orders were issued in Japan over university pass records that included students from other schools and students who had not taken the lessons. Consumer Commission material, November 24, 2011 (Japanese). On your results page, always add who is counted, the year, and how, for example "of N students enrolled in year X; students who took only seasonal courses are not included".

Take care with claims such as "No. 1 for satisfaction in the area" as well. In a report of September 26, 2024, Japan's Consumer Affairs Agency said that a No. 1 claim needs an appropriate choice of what is compared, of who answers, of survey method, and a match between the claim and the results, and that claims based on surveys asking people who had never used a service for their impression of a website may be a problem. Tutoring and cram-school advertisers were among those interviewed. Consumer Affairs Agency: report on No. 1 claims (Japanese). AI leans on facts it can check rather than strong words, so leave claims you can't support out of both your text and your markup.

Checklist before you publish

  1. Grades, courses, class format, subjects and lesson length are written as text, not only shown in images.
  2. Prices are shown as local rules require, with a worked yearly total that includes enrollment, materials and seasonal courses.
  3. If your contracts fall under consumer rules like Japan's specified continuous services, there is a page on documents, cooling-off and cancellation, linked from the fees page.
  4. The timetable, closed days, address and nearest station match across your site, Google Business Profile and directories.
  5. Trial-lesson conditions (free or not, sessions, length) and a link to the booking form are on the page.
  6. Results state the year, who is counted and how, and there are no "No. 1" claims you can't support.
  7. Types, prices and times in structured data match what the page shows. Google: general structured data guidelines

Checking whether you appear in AI answers

Once the facts are in place, ask AI the questions parents would ask (area plus grade plus subject or goal plus format) and see whether your center comes up by name or URL, and whether fees and class times are described correctly. Answers change between runs, so ask the same questions several times on different days and keep a record. Checking before busy periods, such as the new school year, summer courses or exam season, leaves time to fix things. FAQ

Where should a tutoring center start with AI search?

Write the grades you teach, your courses, class format, full cost, timetable, trial lesson and location correctly as text on your pages, not in images. Then publish the same facts as structured data and in llms.txt, and make sure they match your Google Business Profile and directory listings.

Which schema.org type should a tutoring center use?

There is no tutoring-specific type, so EducationalOrganization for the center and Course for each course are the closest fit. Note that EducationalOrganization is not a subtype of LocalBusiness. Whatever type you choose, it must match what the page shows.

Is it fine to say "contact us for prices"?

AI can't state a price you haven't published, so you are more likely to be treated as a center whose fees are unknown. Publish monthly tuition, enrollment, materials and seasonal course fees, and give a worked yearly total by grade and course.

Do pass rates help in AI search?

Results are a common question, but a number without a counting method can't be compared. State the year, who is counted and how. In Japan, displays that counted students of other schools have led to regulatory orders.

Will this guarantee my center appears in ChatGPT?

No. AI answers change, and no tool or technique can guarantee a mention or a position. What you can do is make your facts easy for AI to read and keep checking whether you appear.
