AI search for fertility clinics: how to publish coverage conditions, add-on treatments and outcome figures so AI reads them correctly

The most useful thing a fertility clinic can do for AI search is to state, in dated text on its own site and in the same words as the official rules, who is covered by insurance (age and number of attempts), which add-on and self-pay treatments the clinic offers with their costs and risks, and how its outcome figures are counted. People considering treatment ask AI questions like "Can I still use insurance for IVF at 43?" or "Which clinics in X offer time-lapse embryo culture?" before choosing a clinic. If the clinic's site does not say this, AI answers from explainers about old rules or from comparison sites. Then make the same facts match in structured data, Google Business Profile and public or professional listings.

Written November 20, 2026 (JST). The examples come from Japan, where Nurevo is based. Insurance age and count conditions (Tochigi Prefecture and Sapporo City guidance), the list of advanced medical care (先進医療) and local subsidies (Hyogo Prefecture as of March 1, 2026; Sapporo as of April 1, 2026), the high-cost medical expense benefit (Tokai City leaflet, April 2026 revision), how attempts are counted (Kagoshima Prefecture Q&A), the 2022 Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) documents, the PGT-A evaluation (MHLW expert panel, December 15, 2025), the MHLW medical advertising Q&A (March 2026 revision) and the schema.org types (schema-dts 2.1.0) were checked that day. Outside Japan, coverage, outcome reporting and advertising rules differ: check your own country's rules.

AI answers change by day and by how a question is asked. This guide makes your clinic's facts readable to AI. It does not guarantee that you will appear in AI answers or rank higher. It is not legal or medical advice.

What people considering treatment ask AI

Fertility questions are less about symptoms and more about conditions, cost and how easy the clinic is to attend. Because the insurance conditions depend on age, many people include their age in the question, and couples often research together. Six question types come up most.

Insurance conditions"If I start IVF at 41, how many attempts does insurance cover?"The age and count conditions, at what point age is judged, whether the clinic provides insured treatment

In every case, AI can only use pages that state the conditions in text. If conditions exist only in an image table or inside a PDF, AI may not read them. The basics of writing prices and conditions are in how to write a pricing page.

Insurance conditions: use the same words as official guidance

Since April 2022, general fertility treatment such as intrauterine insemination and assisted reproduction such as IVF and ICSI have been covered by health insurance in Japan. For IVF and ICSI, the woman must be under 43 when treatment starts. Insurance covers up to six attempts per child if she is under 40, and up to three if she is 40 to under 43. Couples in a de facto marriage are also covered, and patients pay 30% at the counter. Tochigi Prefecture (Japanese), Sapporo City (Japanese).

Attempts are counted as embryo transfers. A 2022 MHLW document says that for the first transfer, age is judged on the day the treatment plan for that egg retrieval was made. Kagoshima Prefecture's Q&A says only transfers done under insurance are counted. MHLW 2022 revision overview, fertility (PDF, Japanese), Kagoshima Prefecture (Japanese). Treatment using donor sperm or eggs is not covered.

AI is asked "can I still use insurance at age X?" directly. Write the rule and your clinic's handling separately, as below.

What to writeExampleAvoid
Age and countA table: "Under 40: up to 6 per child / 40 to under 43: up to 3 / 43 and over: not covered"Only "age limits apply"
When age is judged"For the first transfer, age is judged on the day the treatment plan is made"Saying nothing
How attempts are counted"Attempts are embryo transfers. If you have had insured transfers elsewhere, tell us at your first visit"Explanations that differ from the rules, such as "attempts are egg retrievals"

The high-cost medical expense benefit is also worth covering. Tokai City's subsidy leaflet (April 2026 revision) calculates its subsidy after deducting what is refunded through the high-cost medical expense benefit and additional benefits. Tokai City leaflet (PDF, Japanese). On your site, name the scheme and where to ask, for example "Insured costs are eligible for the high-cost medical expense benefit; please check with your health insurer", rather than quoting caps that will go out of date.

Add-on treatments: write what you offer, assuming the list changes

Treatments designated as advanced medical care can be combined with insured treatment, with only the advanced part paid by the patient. The designated techniques are reviewed. For example, Hyogo Prefecture's subsidy page (as of March 1, 2026) lists eleven techniques as category A, including time-lapse embryo culture, PICSI, SEET, ERA, endometrial scratching, two-step embryo transfer, IMSI and ERPeak, and lists preimplantation genetic testing for aneuploidy (PGT-A) as category B. Sapporo City's page (as of April 1, 2026) also lists tacrolimus for repeated implantation failure and sperm sorting using microfluidics. Hyogo Prefecture (Japanese), Sapporo City (Japanese).

Write about PGT-A with particular care. An MHLW expert panel evaluation (December 15, 2025) of one clinical study's final report found that the primary endpoint (ongoing pregnancy rate) did not exceed the threshold set, and said the technique needs to be verified through comparative trials. Evaluation document (PDF, Japanese). Avoid wording such as "PGT-A raises pregnancy rates".

  1. Use formal names and add the common short names (time-lapse, ERA and so on). AI is asked with both.
  2. Say clearly whether your clinic offers each technique. Techniques that require notification can only be provided by approved facilities.
  3. Give the cost with a date, such as "X yen (self-pay, as of month/year)".
  4. Local subsidies for advanced care differ by municipality (Hyogo pays a flat JPY 30,000 per cycle; Sapporo pays 70% of the patient's cost up to JPY 35,000). Say "check your local subsidy" and link to the main municipalities' pages.
  5. When the list is reviewed, update technique names and costs the same day. Remove notices for techniques that are no longer designated, or say so.

Self-pay treatment and costs: meet the disclosure conditions on the same page

The patient's share of advanced care, egg freezing and treatment outside the insurance conditions are self-pay. The MHLW medical advertising Q&A sets conditions for publishing information beyond the permitted advertising items on a website: for self-pay care, the usual treatment content and cost and the main risks and side effects; a contact point that answers questions (a booking-only phone number is not enough); and text that is not hard to read (Q6-10 to 6-12). MHLW medical advertising Q&A (PDF, Japanese).

These are also the facts AI can use when asked "how much is self-pay treatment and what are the risks?". Keep cost and risks in text on the same page, not on separate pages or in images. Insured costs change when fee schedules are revised, so explain the principle ("you pay 30% of insured costs") and add a date if you quote amounts.

Outcome figures and pregnancy rates: if you publish them, add definitions and periods

AI search guides often say "show your results in numbers". Fertility pregnancy rates overlap with medical advertising rules, so decide how to handle them first.

What you want to publishHow the MHLW Q&A treats itHow to write it
Treatment effect such as pregnancy ratesTreatment effect cannot be advertised (Q2-16). A website can state it if the disclosure conditions are met, but must be able to show evidence and demonstrate it objectively on request (Q2-17)Only figures you can back up, on a page that meets the conditions
Number of proceduresA facility's number of operations can be stated with the period. Annual totals over several years are preferred (Q3-17)Egg retrievals and transfers per year, over several years
An individual doctor's numbersCannot be advertised (Q3-16)Use facility-level numbers
Patient testimonialsTestimonials about treatment content or effect cannot be published (Q1-18, Q2-9)Explain the facts in detail: the treatment flow, number of visits, cost conditions

The Q&A does not say how a rate must be calculated, but without its meaning, both AI and people misread a number. We recommend putting at least four things next to any pregnancy rate: the denominator (per transfer or per retrieval), the definition of pregnancy (such as a confirmed gestational sac), the age groups, and the period counted. A single rate across all ages does not apply to anyone of a different age.

A 2022 Central Social Insurance Medical Council document set out, as facility standards for insured assisted reproduction, registration with the Japan Society of Obstetrics and Gynecology as an IVF-ET facility and entering case data into its ART online registry. MHLW: support for fertility treatment (PDF, Japanese). Being a registered facility is a fact you can state on your site.

Visit burden and male infertility: who can get what, and when

  1. Besides hours by day, state what happens on retrieval, transfer and insemination days (including Sundays and holidays). Give days and times, not "easy to combine with work".
  2. State how to book a first visit, whether a referral is needed, which visits need the partner, and information session dates and format (online or not).
  3. Say whether you offer male testing or a male clinic, and if not, which urology clinic you work with. Say whether semen collection is on site or can be brought from home.
  4. Describe the typical number of visits per IVF cycle from your actual process, without promising a number.
  5. Give doctors' qualifications with the exact certifying body and title. Update doctors' days the same day they change.

Structured data when there is no fertility specialty

schema.org's medical specialty values (MedicalSpecialty) have no value for fertility or reproductive medicine (checked in schema-dts 2.1.0 on November 20, 2026). Use this combination instead.

MedicalClinicA clinic. A MedicalBusiness and a LocalBusinessThe clinic's type. It can carry availableService and medicalSpecialty
medicalSpecialtyThe clinical specialty. Values include Gynecologic and UrologicAdd Gynecologic, and Urologic if you run a male clinic
availableServiceCare provided (MedicalProcedure and others)Names of treatments your pages describe, such as insemination, IVF and ICSI
Service and offersA service you provide and its priceRegister only items whose price appears on the page, such as self-pay treatment

Google says no special structured data is needed for AI Overviews and similar features, and that structured data should match the visible content of the page. Google: AI features and your website. Do not put insured costs or pregnancy rates in structured data. Turning a number that depends on conditions into a single value invites wrong answers.

Adding medicalSpecialty and availableService

Nurevo AIO does not output medicalSpecialty or availableService on its own. To add them, use the filter that changes the business structured data (webmcp_canary_business_node) in your theme's functions.php or a small plugin, with the type set to MedicalClinic.

add_filter('webmcp_canary_business_node', function ($org, $settings) {
    // Add only specialties and treatments your pages describe (drop Urologic without a male clinic)
    $org['medicalSpecialty'] = array('Gynecologic', 'Urologic');
    $org['availableService'] = array(
        array('@type' => 'MedicalProcedure', 'name' => 'Intrauterine insemination'),
        array('@type' => 'MedicalProcedure', 'name' => 'In vitro fertilization'),
        array('@type' => 'MedicalProcedure', 'name' => 'ICSI'),
    );
    return $org;
}, 10, 2);

Pre-publish checklist

  1. The insurance age and count conditions are in a table, with when age is judged and that attempts are counted as embryo transfers.
  2. It says de facto couples are covered and whether your clinic provides insured treatment.
  3. Rule explanations are dated and rewritten when the rules change.
  4. Advanced techniques you offer are listed by formal and short name, with dated costs. No notices remain for techniques no longer offered.
  5. Nothing promises an effect for PGT-A or other techniques.
  6. Self-pay treatment pages give content, cost, main risks and side effects, and a contact point that answers questions, on the same page.
  7. Any pregnancy rate has its denominator, definition, age groups and period beside it, and can be backed up. Procedure numbers are given per year over several years.
  8. No testimonials, promised results, superlatives or comparisons in the page text, FAQs or structured data.
  9. The structured data type is MedicalClinic, prices match the page, and there are no insured costs or pregnancy rates in it.
  10. Name, address, phone and hours match across structured data, Google Business Profile, the official medical directory and professional or municipal listings, and llms.txt lists the insurance, advanced care, cost and access pages. Clinic basics, What is llms.txt

FAQ

Where should a fertility clinic start with AI search?

Put the insurance age and count conditions in a table, with when age is judged, how attempts are counted and whether your clinic provides insured treatment. Then list the advanced and self-pay treatments you offer, with cost, risks and dates. Finally, make your name, address and hours match across structured data, Google Business Profile and professional or municipal listings.

How should we write the insurance age and count conditions?

In Japan, IVF and ICSI require the woman to be under 43 when treatment starts; insurance covers up to six attempts per child under 40 and up to three from 40 to under 43, counted as embryo transfers. Put this in a table with an "as of month/year" date.

Can we publish pregnancy rates on our site?

Under the MHLW Q&A, treatment effect cannot be advertised; a website can state it if the disclosure conditions are met, but must be able to show evidence and demonstrate it objectively on request. If you publish a rate, we recommend giving the denominator, the definition of pregnancy, the age groups and the period beside it. Check individual cases with your local health authority.

How should we write about advanced treatments?

List the techniques you offer by formal name and short name, with dated costs. The official list is reviewed, so update the same day it changes. Local subsidies differ by municipality, so do not state amounts as fixed; link to the main municipalities' pages.

Which structured data type should we use?

schema.org has no fertility specialty, so use MedicalClinic as the type, Gynecologic as the medicalSpecialty (plus Urologic if you run a male clinic) and treatment names such as insemination and IVF in availableService. Do not include insured costs or pregnancy rates.
