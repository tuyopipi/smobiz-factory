AI search for local SEO agencies: align Business Profile and website facts, then add it to your contract

The fastest way for a local SEO agency to add AI search optimization (AEO, also called GEO or LLMO) is not a new set of tactics. It is making the Google Business Profile facts you already manage match the client's website and main directory listings, then adding structured data, llms.txt and AI crawler access on the website. ChatGPT and Perplexity read the website and review sites as well as the Business Profile, so when the facts disagree outside the profile, AI can repeat the old ones or name someone else.

Written November 11, 2026 (JST). The outside studies were read that day; the Google, OpenAI and Japanese Consumer Affairs Agency information was checked on their official pages on the dates given in the text (the official pages could not be fetched again for this update, so the earlier check dates are shown).

AI answers change by day and by how the question is asked. The steps here make a business's facts readable for AI. They do not guarantee that a business appears in an AI answer or ranks higher.

What local SEO and AI search optimization share, and where they differ

Local SEO (called MEO in Japan) improves how a business shows up in Google Maps and local results, mostly by managing its Google Business Profile. AI search optimization makes sure ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode can read the business's facts correctly when someone asks, say, "which dentist near the station is open on Saturday?" Both come down to keeping a business's facts accurate and current, so most of an agency's existing routines carry over.

The difference is how many places get read. BrightLocal ran 20 searches across 10 industries on Google AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity and ChatGPT Search. AI Mode used the Business Profile as a primary source, but across every tool most sources were business websites, and directories such as Yelp were used in 33% of searches (BrightLocal, published July 2025, updated October 2025; a small US sample). A perfect profile does not help much if the website still shows last year's hours or a directory lists the wrong phone number: AI reads those too.

Google says there are no additional requirements and no special schema.org markup needed to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, and that the usual basics still matter, such as structured data that matches the visible text and an up-to-date Business Profile (checked November 1, 2026). Google: AI features and your website. The habit of keeping facts right that local SEO teams already have is the foundation for AI search as well.

Step one: a fact sheet for every location

The step that matters most, and gets skipped most, is checking that each fact has the same value everywhere. Insites looked at 10,000 businesses on ChatGPT and Perplexity: 93% were known by at least one AI platform, but only 55% were described with accurate information (Insites, May 2026; a correlation study whose query set is not published). Fixing "known, but described wrongly" is exactly the kind of work local SEO agencies are good at.

Keep one fact sheet per client location (a spreadsheet is enough) and decide the correct value once. Then bring every listing in line with it.

FieldCorrect value on the sheetWhere it must match
Business nameThe official name as on the sign, with one rule for branch namesProfile, website header and about page, structured data name, directories
AddressStreet, number, building and floorProfile, website directions page, structured data address, directories
PhoneOne main number (decide how any tracking number is handled)Profile, website text, structured data telephone, directories
Hours and closed daysHours per weekday, plus public holidays and temporary closuresProfile (special hours), website text, structured data, llms.txt
Business type and servicesWhat the business is and its main services, brieflyProfile categories and services, website service pages, structured data type
Booking and contactBooking page URL, hours the phone is answeredProfile booking link, website, structured data

Add a "last checked" column. It stops the classic mistake of updating the hours on the profile while the website and directories keep the old ones.

Three things to add on the website: structured data, llms.txt, AI crawler access

Even if your agency does not build or maintain the client's website, these three are quick to set up and easy to add to a monthly local SEO plan.

  1. Structured data (schema.org JSON-LD): describe the business as a LocalBusiness or a more specific type such as Dentist, HairSalon or Restaurant, with the same name, address, phone and hours as the fact sheet. Google's local business structured data requires name and address and recommends openingHoursSpecification, telephone and url, among others (checked October 29, 2026). Google: local business structured data
  2. llms.txt: a plain-text summary of the site at /llms.txt (what the business is, where it is, the main pages) that AI can read easily. There is no solid data yet showing it moves results, so treat it as one more readability step, not a switch that makes a business appear. How to write llms.txt
  3. AI crawler access: check that robots.txt does not block AI search crawlers. OpenAI says sites that opt out of OAI-SearchBot will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers (checked October 31, 2026). OpenAI: crawlers

In fairness: the Insites study found no meaningful correlation between structured data or llms.txt and AI visibility, while businesses in the Local Pack were recommended 3.5 times more often (Insites). So structured data is not a ranking lever on its own. Think of it as the part that publishes the fact sheet in a machine-readable form and removes contradictions. Your core local SEO work (a complete profile, reviews, the Local Pack) still matters for AI search.

Reviews: don't carry every local SEO habit over

Reviews are a strong input when AI picks a business. In the Insites study, businesses found by both ChatGPT and Perplexity averaged 133 Google reviews, while those found by neither averaged 11 (Insites, correlation only). But there are legal and policy lines on how reviews are gathered.

In Japan, stealth marketing (advertising that hides that it is advertising) has been regulated as a misleading representation under the Act against Unjustifiable Premiums and Misleading Representations since October 1, 2023 (checked November 1, 2026). Consumer Affairs Agency: stealth marketing. Avoid rewards for reviews, reviews from staff or related parties, and steering only happy customers to post; ask customers for their honest opinion instead. And marking up a business's own reviews on its website does not help with Google: self-serving reviews about a LocalBusiness or Organization are not eligible for review rich results (Google Search Central, September 16, 2019).

Measurement: add one page on AI visibility to the rank report

Add AI visibility to the local rank report you already send. Pick a few questions a client's prospects are likely to ask (business type + area + a condition, such as "a dentist in [city] open on Sundays"), ask them under the same conditions every month, and record whether the business was named, whether its website was cited, and who appeared instead. Don't judge from one run; look at the trend for the same questions. How to measure AI visibility

Checklist for local SEO agencies

  1. Every client location has a fact sheet (name, address, phone, hours, business type, booking URL) with last-checked dates.
  2. The profile, website and main directories match the fact sheet.
  3. The website states hours, address and phone as text, not only in images.
  4. The structured data type fits the business (LocalBusiness or a more specific type) and its values match the page text.
  5. robots.txt does not block AI search crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot.
  6. There is an llms.txt with the main pages and the key facts.
  7. Review requests carry no rewards and no filtering, in line with stealth marketing rules and Google's policies.
  8. A fixed set of AI questions is recorded every month under the same conditions.
  9. No contract guarantees appearances or positions.

FAQ

If a client already has local SEO, do they need AI search optimization?

The foundation is shared, but it may not be enough. AI reads the website and directories as well as the Business Profile, so if information outside the profile is out of date, AI can repeat it. The added work is matching facts everywhere and adding structured data and llms.txt to the website.

How do local SEO, AEO, GEO and LLMO differ?

Local SEO targets Google Maps and local results. AEO, GEO, LLMO and AIO are different names for making sure AI answers such as ChatGPT's describe a business correctly. See AEO vs SEO vs GEO.

What about clients with no website?

Most AI sources for local questions are business websites, so start by proposing a site that states the business's facts as text, even a single page.

How long until we see results?

No fixed timeline can be given. AI answers vary by day and by how the question is asked, and each AI picks up changes on its own schedule. Record the same questions monthly and judge by the trend.
