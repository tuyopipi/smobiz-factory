WordPress SEO plugins and AI search readiness: 12,376 web agency websites (November 2026)

We examined the home pages of 12,376 WordPress websites of web design agencies in Japan and other countries. The most common SEO plugin in Japan was All in One SEO (35.2%); outside Japan it was Yoast SEO (53.1%). In Japan, 19.3–22.5% of sites with a detected SEO plugin had an llms.txt, against 7.7% of sites with none detected.

Published 2026-11-14. Data aggregated 2026-11-12. Research by Nurevo AEO (Bestie.合同会社). Nurevo sells AI search optimization (AEO) tools and a WordPress plugin, and so has an interest in this topic.

Key findings

  1. Among 2,295 WordPress sites of Japanese web agencies, we detected All in One SEO on 35.2% (807), Yoast SEO on 14.4% (331) and Rank Math on 1.7% (40); none of the plugins was detected on 48.6% (1,115). Among 10,081 WordPress agency sites outside Japan: Yoast SEO 53.1%, Rank Math 22.2%, All in One SEO 6.1%.
  2. llms.txt adoption in Japan was 19.3–22.5% across the three detected-plugin groups and 7.7% with no plugin detected. Outside Japan: All in One SEO 51.1%, Rank Math 40.8%, Yoast SEO 30.8%, none detected 24.2%.
  3. Few sites in any plugin group blocked AI bots from the home page in robots.txt (Japan 0.0–1.1%, outside Japan 3.8–9.2%). Outside Japan, non-WordPress sites blocked more often (15.9%).

Method

  1. Population: web design agencies' own websites — 3,352 in Japan (confirmed as agencies) and 20,335 in other countries, 23,687 in total. Not businesses in general.
  2. Reached: 15,838 home pages could be fetched (Japan 2,486, outside Japan 13,352). This report focuses on the 12,376 identified as WordPress (Japan 2,295, outside Japan 10,081).
  3. Fetched: each site's home page, /robots.txt, /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt, once each (User-Agent: NurevoSurvey/1.0), early November to 12 November 2026; aggregated 2026-11-12.
  4. Classification: WordPress = the home page HTML contains wp-content/ or wp-includes/, or a WordPress generator tag. SEO plugin = the first of the strings yoast, rank-math (rankmath), all in one seo (aioseo), seopress and slim seo found in that HTML, checked in that order. llms.txt = HTTP 200, not HTML, more than 20 characters. AI bot blocking = the robots.txt rules that apply to any of GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot or OAI-SearchBot disallow the home page (/) (longest match); the base is sites with a robots.txt.
  5. Country is the country in our own collection list. Groups under 30 sites are not shown (country shares only for countries with 100+ WordPress sites).

Results: plugin share

The ranking flips between Japan and elsewhere. In Japan All in One SEO appears about 2.4 times as often as Yoast SEO (807 vs 331 sites) and Rank Math is at 1.7%. Outside Japan Yoast SEO is on more than half of WordPress sites, followed by Rank Math at just over a fifth. About half of Japanese WordPress sites showed none of the five plugins (see Limitations).

Home page items by plugin

Where an SEO plugin was detected, JSON-LD, Open Graph and canonical links were present on more than 90% of sites. The differences are in llms.txt and in BreadcrumbList, which rarely appears on the home page in the Rank Math and SEOPress groups — most likely a difference in what each plugin outputs on the front page by default. We did not look at inner pages.

The six items are: (1) has an llms.txt; (2) has JSON-LD; (3) has an Organization or LocalBusiness type; (4) has a robots.txt that blocks none of GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and OAI-SearchBot; (5) has a meta description; (6) has a canonical link. This is a simple count made for this survey, not Nurevo's 0–100 product score.

Limitations

  1. Plugins are identified from strings left in the home page HTML. Settings that hide the plugin name, or caching and minification that strip it, lead to "none detected"; sites with traces of more than one are counted under the first checked (Yoast SEO, Rank Math, All in One SEO, SEOPress, Slim SEO).
  2. Only five plugins were checked. Others (such as SEO SIMPLE PACK, used in Japan) and SEO features built into themes are counted as "none detected", which likely explains part of the high Japanese figure.
  3. Only the home page was examined. robots.txt was judged for / only; partial blocking and blocking by firewalls or CDNs were not counted.
  4. The population is web design agencies' own websites, not businesses in general. The plugin–item relationships are co-occurrences, not evidence that the plugin causes them (agencies more invested in SEO may both install a plugin and do other work).
  5. One fetch per site; unreachable sites are excluded. For llms.txt we checked only whether it exists, not its quality.

Our reading (opinion)

What follows is our opinion, not a finding. In every SEO plugin group, basic items such as structured data, canonical links and Open Graph were largely in place; the differences between plugins show up in newer items such as llms.txt and in what structured data is output on the home page. WordPress sites without a detected SEO plugin, and non-WordPress sites, more often miss the basics, so we think that is the first thing to check. None of this guarantees appearing in AI answers (in another of our studies, on-site signals showed no relationship with appearance).

FAQ

Which SEO plugin do Japanese web agencies use most?

In this survey, All in One SEO: 807 of 2,295 Japanese web agency WordPress sites (35.2%). Yoast SEO was on 331 (14.4%) and Rank Math on 40 (1.7%) (November 2026).

What about other countries?

Across 10,081 WordPress agency sites outside Japan: Yoast SEO 53.1%, Rank Math 22.2%, All in One SEO 6.1%, SEOPress 0.8%. Yoast SEO leads in all 13 countries with 100+ WordPress sites.

Does an SEO plugin improve llms.txt or AI readiness?

The survey shows co-occurrence, not cause. In Japan llms.txt was on 19.3–22.5% of sites with a detected plugin and 7.7% without, and none of this guarantees appearing in AI answers.
