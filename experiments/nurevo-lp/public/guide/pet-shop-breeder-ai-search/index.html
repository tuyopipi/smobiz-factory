AI search for pet shops and breeders: get your licence details, puppy facts and visit-first process right in AI answers

For a pet shop or breeder, AI search optimisation (AEO) means that when someone asks an AI "Which Shiba Inu breeders are near me?" or "Can I buy a puppy online?", your own site supplies the correct facts. Put your licence or registration details, breed facts and per-puppy facts, the total price, the hand-over age and how viewing works into plain text on the page and into structured data. In Japan a sale must follow an in-person viewing, England sets rules on how puppies are advertised and shown, and in the United States sight-unseen sellers need a federal licence, so it also matters that AI does not describe you as selling puppies online when you do not.

Written November 28, 2026 (JST). Legal and regulatory facts come from the government pages linked in the text (Japan's Ministry of the Environment and the Tokyo and Hiroshima governments, GOV.UK and legislation.gov.uk) and, for the United States, from the AVMA's report on the 2013 USDA rule. This article is not legal advice; check the details with your licensing authority.

AI answers change by the day and by how a question is asked. The steps below make your facts readable to AI. They do not guarantee that you will appear in an answer or rank higher. Grooming and boarding are covered in AI search for pet grooming salons, and training in AI search for dog trainers.

What people ask AI about pet shops and breeders

People looking for a puppy or kitten ask about four things: which animals are available, what it costs, when they can take one home, and whether the seller can be trusted. If your site does not state these facts, AI either cannot answer or guesses from listing sites and old social posts.

The example questions illustrate the point. They are not measured question frequencies.

Write these details as text. A photo of your licence certificate may not be readable as text to AI or search engines. When your licence is renewed and the number or expiry date changes, update your site, social profiles and listing sites on the same day.

Wording that stops AI saying "buy online"

AI summarises the words it finds. Phrases such as "buy online", "nationwide delivery" or "video viewing available" can lead it to tell people you sell puppies online — which may be untrue and, depending on where you are, may also conflict with the rules above. Replace them with wording that states the process.

If your WordPress site runs WooCommerce, do not list puppies or kittens as products with an add-to-cart button. A cart and checkout read as an online sale, and WooCommerce products also appear in the llms.txt that Nurevo AEO builds, which makes the message even stronger for AI. On the site, take bookings for visits only.

Puppy pages go out of date fast. When a puppy is reserved, mark it as "reserved" or unpublish it and update the date on the listing page. If you keep the page, use Nurevo AEO's per-page setting to leave it out of llms.txt so AI's reading list does not include animals that are gone. Because availability changes within days, it is safer not to mark up individual puppies as Product or Offer; that avoids an old "in stock" being used in an answer.

State the total price and what it includes

AI tends to answer "Shiba puppies cost X" with a single number. Some sellers add vaccinations, microchipping, a vet check or pedigree papers on top of the puppy's price. State the total you charge and list what is included and what costs extra, for example: "Total price [amount] including tax. Includes first vaccination, microchip and registration, vet check. Extra: pedigree papers [amount], if you want them." In Japan, prices shown to consumers must include consumption tax. When you change a price, change it on listing sites the same day.

Structured data: PetStore for the business, Service for visits

schema.org's PetStore ("A pet store") is a LocalBusiness subtype. There is no separate type for breeders, so both shops and breeders can use PetStore and say what they do in the description: breeds, visit-first sales, hand-over age and licence number.

Publish the visit as a Service, even if it is free, with a ReserveAction pointing to your booking page. AI can then answer "How do I book a visit?". Values below are examples.

Setting it up on a WordPress pet shop or breeder site

  2. In the setup wizard choose "Retail shop", then enter PetStore in the business type (schema.org type) field. Add your address, phone and visiting days and hours.
  3. In the business description, name your breeds and write "sold after a visit", your hand-over age and your licence or registration number.
  4. Add a service "Puppy visit (by appointment)" with price 0, your currency, the booking URL and a description of how long it takes and what you will see. You can add up to 20 services; each also gets a line in llms.txt.
  5. Add FAQs (can I buy online, how old are puppies at hand-over, visiting from far away, deposits, microchip registration, guarantee terms) and place the Nurevo FAQ block (or the [nurevo_faq] shortcode) on your visit and buying page.

  7. Open /llms.txt and check that breed pages, the buying process, your business details and the visit booking page are listed and reserved puppies are not. Run the diagnosis on the AEO score screen and fix what is missing.

Keep listing sites, social media and Google Business Profile in step

Puppies are often found through breeder directories, pet classifieds and Instagram, and AI reads those too. If a puppy is reserved on your site but still listed elsewhere, or the price or licence number differs, AI may answer from the older source.

Keep licence details, breeds, total price and visiting hours the same on your site, listing sites, Google Business Profile and Instagram, and update all of them on the day a puppy is reserved or your licence is renewed. For how to correct listings, see fixing your business details on listing sites and maps.

Checklist before you publish

  1. Licence or registration details are in text on every page or on a business details page linked from every page.
  2. No "buy online", "nationwide delivery" or "video viewing" wording remains on the site or social profiles (unless it is accurate and lawful where you are).
  3. The site says any sale follows a visit and states the hand-over age (8 weeks in England, after 56 days in Japan).
  4. Breed pages cover adult size, lifespan, housing, feeding, exercise, health and neutering cost.
  5. Puppy pages show sex, date of birth, vaccinations, breeder registration, a real photo and age, and an updated date.
  6. The price is a total, with what is included and what costs extra.
  7. Microchipping and how registration passes to the new owner are explained.
  8. Structured data uses PetStore and a Service with a booking URL; individual puppies are not marked up as Product or Offer.
  9. Reserved puppies are unpublished or left out of llms.txt.
  10. Licence details, prices and visiting hours match across your site, listing sites, Google Business Profile and Instagram.

Check whether AI shows you, and whether it is right

Once the facts are in place, ask AI the questions buyers ask. Try area-plus-breed questions such as "Shiba Inu breeder in [county]" to see whether you appear, then ask with your name: "Can I buy a puppy online from [name]?", "How old are their puppies at hand-over?", "Are they licensed?". Record whether the answers match your site. If AI says you sell online or deliver nationwide, look for old wording on your site, listing sites or old posts.

AI answers vary between runs, so ask the same questions several times over a few weeks. FAQ

Where should a pet shop or breeder start with AI search optimisation?

Put your licence or registration details in text on every page and write in your business description that sales follow a visit and what age puppies go home. Then split breed pages from puppy pages, state the total price, and publish the same facts as PetStore and Service structured data and in llms.txt.

Do breeders need a licence?

In England, you need one if you breed 3 or more litters in any 12-month period and sell any of the puppies, or run a business that breeds and advertises dogs for sale, and every advert must show the licence number and issuing council. In Japan, breeding for sale counts as a registered sales business. In the US, federal USDA licensing applies to sight-unseen sales, and states add their own rules. Check with your local authority.

Can I sell puppies online?

It depends on where you are. In Japan a contract made only online is prohibited and the animal must be seen in person. In England a puppy may only be shown to a buyer with its biological mother. In the US, selling sight unseen requires a USDA licence, and state law may add more. In all cases, write your actual process clearly so AI does not describe it wrongly.

Should I add structured data for each puppy?

We do not recommend it. Puppies are reserved within days, and an old Product or Offer marked in stock can be used by AI after the puppy is gone. Use PetStore for the business and Service for visits, and keep puppy facts in the page text with an updated date.

What must a breeder's advert show in England?

Under Schedule 6 of SI 2018/486, any advert for the sale of a dog must include the licence number, the local authority that issued the licence, a recognisable photograph of the dog and the dog's age. The statutory guidance also asks for the licence holder's name and number to be shown clearly on any website used for the activity.
