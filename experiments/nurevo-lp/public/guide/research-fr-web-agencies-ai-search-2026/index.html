AI search readiness of French web design agencies: 405 agency websites (November 2026)

We fetched the home page, robots.txt and llms.txt of 405 French web design agencies' own websites. They averaged 4.71 of six AI-search items and 90.1% had JSON-LD structured data — both the highest among the 17 countries with 100+ sites in our survey. 26.4% (107 sites) had an llms.txt, exactly the same as all other overseas agencies (26.4%) and 2.0 times Japan (13.4%). Only 1.9% (7 sites) blocked an AI bot in robots.txt, the second-lowest rate after Japan (1.3%), and none blocked a search crawler.

Published 23 November 2026. Data aggregated 12 November 2026. Research and writing: Nurevo AEO (Bestie.合同会社, Japan). Nurevo sells AI search optimisation (AEO) tools and a WordPress plugin, so we have an interest in this topic. This is the sixth of our country reports (after the US, the UK, Australia, India and Canada). A French version is also available.

Key findings

  1. French web agency websites (405 reached) averaged 4.71 of six AI-search items, 74.3% met five or more, and 90.1% had JSON-LD. The average and the JSON-LD share were the highest of the 17 countries with 100+ reached sites (UK: 3.61, 55.1%, 68.8%; Japan: 4.05, 52.2%, 69.4%).
  2. 26.4% (107 sites) had an llms.txt — the same as all other overseas agencies (26.4%), with no statistically significant difference from the UK (21.5%, p=0.06) or Spain (31.9%, p=0.16), and 2.0 times Japan (13.4%, p<0.001). Of the 204 sites meeting five items, 196 (96.1%) were missing only the llms.txt.
  3. Of the 378 sites with a robots.txt, 1.9% (7) blocked an AI bot — second-lowest of the 17 countries after Japan (1.3%). All 5 sites blocking GPTBot still allowed OAI-SearchBot, and no site blocked any of the four bots used for search or for fetching pages when a user asks.

Methodology

  1. Population: web design agencies' own websites. Of 821 agency sites listed as France in our collection list, 405 home pages were reachable. This is not a sample of all French businesses. For comparison we use the UK (942), Spain (248), Italy (110), Japan (2,486) and all other overseas sites excluding France (12,947) from the same survey.
  2. What we fetched: each site's home page, /robots.txt, /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt, once each (User-Agent: NurevoSurvey/1.0), between early November and 12 November 2026; aggregated on 12 November.
  3. How we judged: llms.txt counts if it returned status 200 with non-HTML text longer than 20 characters. JSON-LD means at least one block on the home page; "broken" means at least one block that does not parse as JSON. Organization-type means an @type containing Organization, LocalBusiness, Corporation or ProfessionalService. WordPress means wp-content/, wp-includes/ or a WordPress generator tag in the home page HTML. SEO plugins were identified from strings in the home page HTML.
  4. AI bot blocking: whether the robots.txt group that applies to each bot disallows the home page (/), using longest match. The base is sites with a robots.txt. "Blocks an AI bot" means blocking any of GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot or OAI-SearchBot.
  5. The "six items" are: (1) has an llms.txt, (2) has JSON-LD, (3) has an Organization or LocalBusiness type, (4) has a robots.txt that blocks none of GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and OAI-SearchBot, (5) has a meta description, (6) has a canonical link. It is a simple count (0–6) for this survey, not Nurevo's 0–100 score.
  6. HTTP 202: only 7 French home pages answered with status 202 (1.7% of reached), far fewer than the UK (14.9%), Italy (22.7%) or other overseas sites (11.4%). A 202 page scores "no" on almost everything, which affects country averages. Among the 398 French sites that answered 200, llms.txt was 26.9%, all six items 24.4% and the average 4.80 — no significant difference from US sites that answered 200 (27.2%, 22.3%; p=0.96, p=0.37).
  7. Statistics: differences were tested with chi-square tests, and AI bot blocking (small counts) with Fisher's exact test. Groups with fewer than 30 sites are not shown.

Results: France vs the UK, Spain, Italy and Japan

France was 15–20 points higher than other overseas sites, and 19–22 points higher than the UK, on JSON-LD (90.1%), Organization-type markup (81.2%), meta descriptions (90.4%), Open Graph (90.9%), canonical links (93.6%) and robots.txt (93.3%). On llms.txt (26.4%) there was no significant difference from the UK, Spain or Italy, and it equalled other overseas sites (p=1.00). The share meeting all six items (24.0%) was higher than the UK (17.7%, p=0.01) and not different from Spain (27.4%, p=0.37). Compared with Japan, the WordPress share (92.3%) and meta descriptions (90.4% vs 90.7%, p=0.92) were almost identical, while llms.txt was 2.0 times higher, JSON-LD 90.1% vs 69.4%, and all six items 2.3 times higher.

France's high figures go with a high WordPress share (92.3% vs 75.0% for other overseas sites) and few 202 responses (1.7%). Comparing WordPress sites only, France's llms.txt share (27.5%) did not differ from the US (30.1%, p=0.34) and was a little lower than other overseas WordPress sites (33.3%, p=0.023); all six items did not differ (25.9% vs 28.1%, p=0.39).

France among 17 countries

The 17 countries (Japan included) with 100 or more reached sites, ranked by llms.txt. France was 7th on llms.txt and 7th on all six items, behind Singapore, India, Spain, the Philippines, Brazil and South Africa. Its six-item average (4.71) and JSON-LD share (90.1%) were the highest of all 17, and its AI bot blocking rate (1.9%) the second-lowest after Japan (1.3%). Within Europe, Spain had a higher llms.txt share (31.9%), while Italy — where many sites answered 202 — had the lowest average (2.70). Country counts reflect our collection list, not the distribution of agencies in each country.

Blocking by bot

Share of the 378 French sites with a robots.txt that block each bot from the home page. Bots described as training crawlers (CCBot, GPTBot, Google-Extended, Bytespider, Applebot-Extended, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai) were blocked by 1.1–1.9%, lower than the UK (3.9–4.7%), Spain (3.9–5.6%) and Italy (4.8–9.5%) and close to Japan (1.0–1.2%). No French site blocked OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot or PerplexityBot — the bots used for search and for fetching pages when a user asks — nor Googlebot or Bingbot. France's AI bot blocking rate (1.9%) was lower than the UK, Spain, Italy and other overseas sites (p=0.018, 0.031, 0.005, 0.002) and not significantly different from Japan (p=0.34).

What the blocking looks like (facts)

  1. All 5 sites that blocked GPTBot did not block OAI-SearchBot.
  2. Of the 7 sites blocking any of the 11 AI bots above, 4 used the same set of seven: GPTBot, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, Google-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider and Applebot-Extended. The others blocked CCBot only, GPTBot and CCBot, or Google-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider and Applebot-Extended (one site each).
  3. 21 sites (5.6% of those with a robots.txt) named an AI bot in robots.txt without blocking the home page for any of them.
  4. 3 of the 7 sites blocking an AI bot also had an llms.txt.
  5. 88.1% of robots.txt files included a Sitemap line.

Structured data types

The most common JSON-LD types on French agency home pages were WebSite (87.7%), Organization (77.3%), WebPage (74.1%), SearchAction (74.1%) and ImageObject (73.8%), each higher than in the UK, Spain and Italy. These are types WordPress SEO plugins commonly output, consistent with the high WordPress share. Compared with Japan, Person (26.4% vs 10.2%), PostalAddress (32.3% vs 16.4%), GeoCoordinates (13.6% vs 3.7%), FAQPage (13.1% vs 3.9%) and AggregateRating (6.2% vs 0.2%) were higher. 1.1% of French sites with JSON-LD (4 sites) had a block that did not parse.

WordPress and SEO plugins

92.3% of French agency sites (374) ran WordPress — the same share as Japan (92.3%) and higher than the UK (73.4%), Spain (67.3%) and Italy (51.8%). WordPress sites had llms.txt 27.5%, JSON-LD 94.1% and a six-item average of 4.91; the 31 non-WordPress sites (7 of which answered 202) had 4 llms.txt files, 13 with JSON-LD and an average of 2.29.

Yoast SEO was detected on 55.9% and Rank Math on 20.9% of French WordPress sites, a mix similar to the UK (58.0%, 20.7%). AIOSEO (11 sites) and the French-developed SEOPress (4 sites) are below 30 sites and not shown. llms.txt was present on 37.2% of Rank Math sites, 25.8% of Yoast SEO sites and 18.1% of sites with no plugin detected. We did not check whether an llms.txt was generated by a plugin or placed by hand.

.fr vs .com

We split French agency sites by the last label of the host name: of 405 reached, 209 were .fr, 143 .com and 53 other. .com sites were more likely to have an llms.txt (33.6% vs 21.5%) and to meet all six items (32.2% vs 18.2%), both statistically significant (p=0.017, p=0.004). JSON-LD (91.4% vs 86.7%, p=0.22) and the six-item average (4.72 vs 4.66) were about the same. This survey cannot tell why (client base, how agencies build sites, or something else).

Distribution of the six items

Limitations

  1. The population is web design agencies' own websites, not all businesses. Country comes from our collection list, which is not a representative sample of French agencies; the high WordPress share (92.3%) may partly reflect how the list was collected. We did not analyse regions. Some counts (for example per bot) are small.
  2. Only home pages were checked. robots.txt was judged for the home page (/), so partial-directory blocks and firewall or CDN blocking are not counted. We checked whether an llms.txt exists, not its quality.
  3. Each site was fetched once; unreachable sites (416) are excluded. The 7 sites that answered 202 may have shown us something other than their real home page.
  4. SEO plugins were identified from HTML strings, so sites that hide traces or run several plugins may be misclassified. Structured data added later by JavaScript is not counted.
  5. Figures are from the 12 November 2026 aggregation. We re-run the survey weekly, so later figures may differ.

How to read this (opinion)

This section is our interpretation. French agency sites have the basics — structured data, meta descriptions, canonical links, robots.txt — in place unusually often, while llms.txt adoption is at the overseas average, and almost every five-item site lacked only an llms.txt. The spread of WordPress and SEO plugins may be part of the reason, but this survey does not test cause. AI bot blocking is rare and limited to training crawlers; which bots to block is each site owner's decision. None of this guarantees appearing in AI answers (in another of our studies, on-site signals showed no relationship with appearance).

FAQ

Are French agencies ahead on AI search readiness?

On the basics, yes: 90.1% had JSON-LD and the six-item average was 4.71, both the highest of 17 countries. llms.txt (26.4%) was exactly the overseas average (November 2026, agencies' own websites).

How does France compare with Japan?

The WordPress share (92.3%) and meta descriptions (90.4% vs 90.7%) were almost identical. France had 2.0 times Japan's llms.txt share (26.4% vs 13.4%), more JSON-LD (90.1% vs 69.4%) and 2.3 times the share meeting all six items (24.0% vs 10.4%).

Do many French sites block AI crawlers?

Very few: 1.9% of sites with a robots.txt (7 sites), the second-lowest after Japan. All targeted training crawlers; none blocked OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot or PerplexityBot.
