AI search for auto repair shops: getting AI to read your certifications, price breakdown, turnaround and loaner cars correctly

AI search optimization (AEO) for an auto repair shop, inspection station or body shop comes down to publishing the facts drivers ask about — what you are certified or licensed to do, how a price breaks down, how long a job takes and whether there's a loaner car, which vehicles and jobs you handle, and your hours and booking — accurately and consistently, in the text of your pages and in structured data (schema.org's AutoRepair). Two things set shops apart in AI answers: separating the total from its parts when you publish prices, and stating in words which jobs and vehicles you do and don't take.

Written November 13, 2026 (JST). Facts about schema.org, Google, OpenAI and Japan's Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT) were checked on the dates given in the text. Licensing rules differ by country; check your own regulator's current rules.

AI answers vary by day and by how a question is phrased. The steps here make your shop's facts easy for AI to read correctly. They cannot guarantee that you appear in an AI answer or rank in any position.

What drivers ask AI

When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews to find a mechanic, the AI reads what it can find on the web and answers with a few shop names. The questions are rarely just "mechanic near me". They look like "a shop in [town] that can finish an inspection the same day", "which garage gives you a free loaner", "who repairs European cars near me", "where can I get my windshield camera recalibrated" or "a shop open on Saturday". Time, cost, vehicle type and the job are all in the question.

Price is where shop information goes wrong most often. Many jobs combine fixed costs that are the same everywhere (fees, taxes, mandatory charges) with labour and parts that differ by shop. If a site says only "inspections from $X", AI can't tell whether that is the total or one part of it, and may line your shop up against others using the wrong number.

Google says there are no extra requirements or special structured data for appearing in AI Overviews or AI Mode, and that the usual SEO basics apply: structured data should match the visible text, and your Business Profile should be up to date (checked November 12, 2026). Google: AI features and your website. The work is not a trick; it is publishing your shop's facts correctly, in a form that can be compared.

Certifications are facts AI needs too: Japan as an example

In most countries, what a shop is allowed or certified to do decides which questions it can answer. Japan makes this especially clear, so it's a useful example even if you work elsewhere.

According to MLIT's regional bureaus, a business that does work involving removing brakes, steering and other safety-critical parts (now called "specified maintenance", tokutei seibi) needs certification from the regional transport bureau for each workshop. When a certified shop (ninsho kojo) handles the statutory inspection (shaken), the car is taken to a government inspection station. A designated shop (shitei kojo), a certified shop that also has inspection equipment and an authorised inspector, can issue a certificate of conformity, so the car never has to go to the government station (checked November 13, 2026). Tohoku bureau (Japanese) / Shikoku bureau (Japanese)

Since April 1, 2020, removing or adjusting the forward cameras and radar used by automatic emergency braking and lane keeping (what the trade calls ADAS calibration or aiming) also needs certification. Kinki bureau (Japanese). The transition period for shops already doing this work ended on March 31, 2024. Shikoku bureau leaflet, PDF (Japanese). An on-board diagnostics (OBD) check was added to the inspection from October 1, 2024 (imported cars from October 1, 2025), for new models introduced from October 2021 (imports from October 2022). MLIT: OBD inspection (Japanese)

So the answers to "same-day inspection", "inspection while you wait" or "who can recalibrate my ADAS camera" depend on certifications and equipment. Wherever you are, write your certifications, licence numbers and the equipment-dependent jobs you can do as text on the page, so AI has something to cite.

Write each fact as a sentence an AI could lift straight into an answer. "Cheap inspections, just ask!" helps less than "As a designated station we finish while-you-wait inspections in about 60 minutes. A compact car costs $X in total, including $Y in mandatory fees." Leave out "cheapest" or "best in town" unless you can back them up. AI uses amounts and times far more than claims it can't check.

Structured data type: AutoRepair and AutoBodyShop

schema.org has AutomotiveBusiness for car-related businesses, with subtypes including AutoRepair for repair and servicing, AutoBodyShop for body and paint, AutoDealer and AutoWash. AutomotiveBusiness is itself a kind of LocalBusiness, so address and opening hours sit inside the type's definition (checked November 13, 2026 against the type definitions in schema-dts 2.1.0, which is generated from the schema.org vocabulary).

Google's local business structured data documentation asks you to use the most specific subtype you can (checked October 29, 2026). Google: Local business structured data. Use AutoRepair if servicing and inspections are your main work, AutoBodyShop if it's bodywork. A shop that does both picks its main line as the type and lists the other as a service. schema.org has no type or property for a statutory inspection or a designated station, so those go in the description and text.

Checklist before you publish

  1. Your certifications and licence numbers, including for ADAS calibration if you do it, are written as text.
  2. Prices separate mandatory costs from your own charges and say whether tax is included.
  3. Turnaround (while you wait, same day, drop-off) and loaner car conditions are stated.
  4. Vehicles and jobs you handle, and those you don't, are stated.
  5. Hours and closed days are text, not an image, and match the hours in structured data.
  6. Shop name, address, phone and hours match across your site, Google Business Profile and booking sites.
  7. The structured data type is AutoRepair (or AutoBodyShop) rather than a leftover LocalBusiness or ProfessionalService.
  8. No claims like "cheapest" or "best in town" that you can't support, in the text or in structured data.
  9. llms.txt lists your overview, prices, services, booking, directions and FAQ pages. What is llms.txt

Checking whether you appear in AI answers

Once the facts are in place, ask AI the questions drivers would ask (area + vehicle + job + a condition such as time or a loaner car), without your shop's name, and see whether your shop comes up by name or URL. Then ask by name — "Can [shop] do an inspection in a day? Do they lend cars?" — and check the answer against today's prices and terms. If it's wrong, look at which page the AI cited and fix that page or the outside listing. AI answers change from one run to the next, so ask the same questions several times, spaced out, and keep a record. FAQ

Where should an auto repair shop start with AI search?

Write your certifications, prices with their breakdown, turnaround and loaner cars, the vehicles you service, and your hours and booking as text on your pages. Then publish the same facts in structured data and llms.txt, and make them match your Google Business Profile and booking sites.

Which structured data type fits a repair shop?

schema.org's AutoRepair for servicing and inspections, or AutoBodyShop for bodywork. Both sit under AutomotiveBusiness, which is a LocalBusiness type, so address and hours fit naturally. There is no property for statutory inspections or licences, so put those in the description and text.

Isn't "inspections from $X" enough?

AI can't tell whether that is the total or one part, and may compare you with other shops using the wrong number. Give an example per vehicle class that separates mandatory costs from your labour and fees, and say whether tax is included.

Should we mention certifications such as ADAS calibration?

Yes. Questions like "who can recalibrate my windshield camera" or "same-day inspection" depend on what you are certified and equipped to do. Writing the certification and number as text gives AI something to cite.

Can we add "cheapest in town" for AI?

Avoid it unless you can prove it. Advertising rules in many countries restrict unsupported comparative claims, and AI uses checkable amounts and times far more than slogans.

Is Nurevo AEO free?

Yes. Business type (AutoRepair), address, hours, services and FAQ structured data, llms.txt, AI crawler rules and the AEO score are on the free plan. The paid plans add an always-current ruleset (Standard, $20/month) and AI visibility measurement in beta (Pro, from $99/month). Nurevo AEO on WordPress.org

Will this guarantee my shop appears in ChatGPT?

No. AI answers change, and no tool or technique can guarantee a mention or a position. What you can do is make your facts easy for AI to read and keep checking whether you appear.
