What Is llms.txt? How to Write One, What We Know About Whether It Works, and How to Add It on WordPress

llms.txt is a Markdown text file at the root of your domain (/llms.txt) that sums up your site and links to its most important pages. It was proposed in 2024 and is not an official standard. Google says its AI features don't need it, and we found no public statement from ChatGPT or Perplexity that they use other sites' llms.txt files in answers. It takes about 30 minutes to write, so the sensible way to treat it is as a short, accurate summary you add after the facts on your pages are right.

Written 16 November 2026 (JST). This is a full rewrite of our 23 October 2026 page, based on a fresh reading of the top-ranking guides and the primary sources. It is for business owners deciding whether to add llms.txt, and for web agencies deciding whether to add it to client sites.

AI answers change from day to day and with how a question is asked. The steps here make the information AI reads more accurate; they cannot guarantee that you will appear in an AI answer.

What llms.txt is for

llms.txt is a proposal published by Jeremy Howard of Answer.AI on 3 September 2024 (Answer.AI announcement; spec at llmstxt.org). The idea: instead of making an AI (a large language model) crawl HTML pages one by one, hand it one file with a summary of the site and a list of the pages worth reading. It was first aimed at software documentation that people want AI coding tools to read.

It is easy to confuse with other files at the root of a site, so here is how they differ.

FileJobStatusRelation to AI
robots.txtTells crawlers what they may fetchStandard (RFC 9309)AI companies say their crawlers follow it. This is where you block or allow AI
sitemap.xmlGives search engines a list of your URLsFormat supported by the search enginesHelps discovery. Describes nothing
Structured data (JSON-LD)States facts per page (address, hours, prices) in machine-readable formschema.org vocabulary; Google and others document their useUsed for search result features. How AI answers use it is not documented in detail by any provider
llms.txtSums up the site and links its key pages in one Markdown fileProposal, not an official standardNo AI search service we know of has said it uses it (as of 16 November 2026)

llms.txt does not replace robots.txt. Whether AI crawlers may visit is decided in robots.txt; llms.txt says "if you read this site, start here".

Does it work? What is known and what is not

You will often read that adding llms.txt gets you cited by AI. Very little of that can be checked. Here is what the public record supports as of 16 November 2026.

QuestionWhat is knownSource
Do Google AI Overviews and AI Mode use it?Google says you don't need to create new AI text files or special markup to appear in its AI features, and that normal SEO best practices applyGoogle Search Central: AI features and your website
Do ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude use it?We found no public documentation saying they use other sites' llms.txt files in answers. Nor do they say they don'tProviders' public documentation, as far as we could check when writing
Do AI bots actually request it?A Japanese company published 28 days of its own server logs: of 72,100 bot requests, 4 were for llms.txt (all Amazonbot); ChatGPT-User made 18,553 requests and none for llms.txt. One site onlyStellagent's AI Search Dock blog (Japanese)
How many sites have one?13.4% of 2,486 Japanese and 26.4% of 13,352 overseas web agency sites (November 2026)Nurevo study
Are sites with one recommended more by AI?Across 62 local businesses in Japan, site-side signals including llms.txt showed no relationship with appearing in Perplexity answersNurevo study

In short: there is no evidence so far that llms.txt makes you appear more in AI search. There is no official statement that it hurts either. Because it costs little, the fair position is "fine to add, but don't treat it as the job done". What AI answers usually draw on is page text and structured data, so get those facts right first.

Should you add one? A decision by type of site

With no proven effect, the deciding factors are the effort and the risk of the file going stale. A hand-written llms.txt that nobody updates keeps publishing old prices or hours that no longer match your pages.

SiteRecommendationWhy
Developer docs, API referencesAdd oneThe original use case; AI coding tools are pointed at these files
Small local business, clinic, law or accounting firmAdd one if it can be generated automaticallyFew pages, easy to summarise. Hand-written means someone must update hours and prices
B2B and SaaSAdd one, linking pricing, case studies and the About pagePuts what comparison questions need in one place
Online shopAdd one if it can be generated automaticallyToo many products and price changes to keep up by hand
Site nobody maintainsSkip it, or generate it automatically onlyPublishing stale facts does more harm than having no file

How to write it: the format

The proposed format is Markdown, in this order (llmstxt.org). Only the first heading is required; everything else is optional.

  1. One heading starting with "# ": the name of the site, company or business (required)
  2. A blockquote starting with "> ": what the site is, in one to three sentences (optional, but include it)
  3. Plain text without headings: facts you want in the summary, such as location, who you serve and opening hours (optional)
  4. Lists of links under "## " headings, each line as "- [Page name](URL): one-line note" (optional)
  5. A list under a heading named "## Optional": pages that can be skipped when a shorter context is needed (optional)

Name the file llms.txt and put it at the root of your domain (https://example.com/llms.txt). Save it as UTF-8 and write it in the language of your site. The proposal also suggests a Markdown copy of each page at its URL with .md appended, and some sites publish a second file with all their text (often called llms-full.txt). Both are optional; for a small business site one llms.txt is enough.

Example: llms.txt for a B2B SaaS company

A made-up company. In one screen you can see what it does, who it is for and what each linked page contains.

# Example Cloud Inc.

> Example Cloud makes "Example Time", a cloud time-tracking app for companies with 10 to 300 employees. Monthly pricing, with a 14-day free trial.

Head office in Osaka, founded in 2018. Support by phone and email on weekdays, 9:00 to 18:00 JST.

## Product and pricing

- [Example Time features](https://example.com/features/): clock-in, shifts, paid leave, export to payroll software
- [Pricing](https://example.com/pricing/): monthly price per plan, and whether there is a setup fee
- [Case studies](https://example.com/cases/): customers by industry and company size

## Company and support

- [About us](https://example.com/company/): legal name, address, founding year, CEO
- [FAQ](https://example.com/faq/): cancelling, exporting your data, security

## Optional

- [News](https://example.com/news/): feature releases and price changes

Three habits help. First, state only facts that are also on your pages: if a price is only in llms.txt and the page changes, nobody can tell which is right. Second, give every link a short note saying what the page answers. Third, leave out marketing claims ("industry-leading", "best in class"). What gets quoted in AI answers is checkable fact.

Three ways to add llms.txt on WordPress

WordPress has no built-in llms.txt. You have three options.

  1. Write it and upload it: create llms.txt in a text editor and upload it by FTP or your host's file manager to the WordPress root (the folder with wp-config.php). WordPress's standard rewrite rules serve real files as they are, so a hand-uploaded file usually wins over any plugin. Updates are manual too
  2. Use an llms.txt plugin: WordPress.org lists several plugins that generate llms.txt. Before installing, check which pages it lists, whether it updates itself, and that it doesn't overlap with another plugin

How to check it after publishing

  1. Open https://your-domain/llms.txt in a browser: you should see Markdown text, not HTML, with no garbled characters
  2. Run curl -I https://your-domain/llms.txt: expect 200, no redirect to another URL, and a Content-Type of text/plain (or text/markdown)
  3. Check that the name, address, phone, hours and prices match your page text and structured data exactly
  4. Count requests for /llms.txt and their user agents in your server logs to see which bots fetch it (none is not a fault)
  5. Run Nurevo's free check to see llms.txt alongside your structured data and AI crawler access

Checklist before you publish

  1. The first line is a "# " heading with your name
  2. The "> " summary says what you are, where, and what you offer to whom
  3. Every fact matches your page text, structured data and Google Business Profile
  4. Links are limited to key pages, each with a note
  5. No noindex pages, no pages blocked in robots.txt, no broken links
  6. No marketing claims and no instructions to AI
  7. https://your-domain/llms.txt returns 200 and shows text, not HTML
  8. Exactly one source serves the file (hand-made or one plugin)
  9. Someone is responsible for updating it when prices or hours change (not needed if generated)
  10. robots.txt does not block the AI crawlers you want to read your site

FAQ

Is llms.txt an official standard?

No. It is a proposal published in 2024 by Jeremy Howard of Answer.AI, not a specification from a standards body. As of 16 November 2026 we found no public statement from an AI search service that it uses other sites' llms.txt files.

Does llms.txt affect Google rankings or AI Overviews?

Google says you don't need new AI text files to appear in its AI features. There is no official statement that the file raises or lowers rankings. For Google, page text and structured data come first.

Do I need llms-full.txt too?

No. It is a file with all of a site's text, sometimes used by large documentation sites. A business or company site is fine with llms.txt alone. Nurevo AEO doesn't create llms-full.txt.

If robots.txt blocks AI crawlers, will they still read llms.txt?

llms.txt doesn't change robots.txt. If a crawler is blocked, assume it fetches nothing, llms.txt included. If you want AI to read your site, first check that robots.txt isn't blocking the AI search crawlers.

Can I write it in my own language?

Yes. The proposal doesn't fix a language; write it in the language your customers use. Nurevo AEO uses fixed English headings and keeps the content in your site's language.

How many pages should I list?

There is no rule. Keep to pages that answer the questions people ask AI about you: about, pricing, services, FAQ, location. Nurevo AEO lists the 30 most recently modified pages automatically, and you can leave out any page.

How often should I update it?

As soon as a fact in it changes. If you maintain it by hand, add "update llms.txt" to your routine for price and opening-hours changes. Nurevo AEO rebuilds it whenever you save a page or the settings.
