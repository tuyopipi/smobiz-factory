Structured data adoption, types and errors: 15,838 web design agency websites (November 2026)

We examined the home pages of 15,838 websites of web design agencies in Japan and elsewhere. 69.4% of Japanese agency sites (1,726 of 2,486) and 72.0% of agency sites outside Japan had structured data (JSON-LD). FAQPage appeared on 3.9% and 9.4%, LocalBusiness on 5.4% and 12.3%. Among sites with JSON-LD, 1.7% (Japan) and 1.8% (elsewhere) included JSON-LD that cannot be parsed.

Published 2026-11-13. Data aggregated 2026-11-12. Research by Nurevo AEO (Bestie.合同会社). Nurevo sells AI search optimization (AEO) tools and so has an interest in this topic.

Key findings

  1. JSON-LD structured data is on the home page of 69.4% of Japanese agency websites (1,726 / 2,486) and 72.0% of those outside Japan (9,615 / 13,352). The gap is small, but FAQPage is on 3.9% vs 9.4%, LocalBusiness on 5.4% vs 12.3%, and PostalAddress on 16.4% vs 28.0%.
  2. Of sites with JSON-LD, 1.7% in Japan (29) and 1.8% elsewhere (177) include a block that is not valid JSON. On 9 Japanese and 10 non-Japanese sites, every JSON-LD block was broken.
  3. Among Japanese WordPress sites, 92.5% to 99.1% of those with a detected SEO plugin have JSON-LD, against 48.3% of the 1,115 where no plugin was detected. Sites not built on WordPress: 28.3% in Japan, 9.0% elsewhere.

About the survey

  1. Population: the company websites of web design agencies: 3,352 in Japan (confirmed as agencies) and 20,335 elsewhere, 23,687 in total. This is not a sample of all business websites.
  2. Reached: the home page could be fetched for 15,838 (2,486 in Japan, 13,352 elsewhere). All results refer to these sites.
  3. Fetched: each site's home page (plus /robots.txt, /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt), once each (User-Agent: NurevoSurvey/1.0), between early November and 2026-11-12, aggregated on 2026-11-12.
  4. How things were judged: every <script type="application/ld+json"> on the home page was parsed as JSON and its @type values counted, nested ones included. A site counts as having broken JSON-LD if any block fails to parse. Organization-type means an @type containing Organization, LocalBusiness, Corporation or ProfessionalService. Microdata means an itemtype with a schema.org URL. WordPress and SEO plugins were identified from strings in the home page HTML (wp-content, yoast, rank-math, aioseo and so on).
  5. Country is the agency's country in our collection list. Groups under 30 sites are not shown.

Broken JSON-LD

Counted per block, 34 of 2,352 JSON-LD blocks in Japan (1.4%) and 193 of 15,609 elsewhere (1.2%) could not be parsed as JSON. Per site, the rates are 1.7% and 1.8%. Sites where every JSON-LD block was broken, so that no structured data could be read at all, numbered 9 in Japan (0.5% of sites with JSON-LD) and 10 elsewhere (0.1%). Most sites with a broken block (20 of 29 in Japan, 167 of 177 elsewhere) also had at least one block that parsed correctly.

WordPress sites are much more likely to have JSON-LD: 92.5% outside Japan. Among sites not on WordPress, the share is 28.3% in Japan and 9.0% elsewhere.

Limitations

  1. Only the home page was examined. Structured data on inner pages (about, FAQ and so on) is not counted.
  2. Structured data injected by JavaScript after load is not in the fetched HTML and is not counted. Up to 40 @type values were recorded per site.
  3. "Broken" means only that the block is not valid JSON. We did not check required properties, conformance with schema.org, or Google's rich result requirements.
  4. SEO plugins were identified by their names in the HTML. Plugins configured not to show their name, and structured data from other plugins or themes, cannot be told apart.
  5. The population is web design agencies' own websites, not businesses in general. The agency list is our own and not exhaustive. One fetch per site; unreachable sites are excluded.

Our reading (opinion)

What follows is our opinion, not a finding. Whether a site has JSON-LD at all differs little between Japan and elsewhere, and much of it appears to be generated automatically by SEO plugins. The gap is in types that need company-specific content, such as address, opening hours and FAQs. Structured data does not guarantee appearing in AI answers (in another of our studies, on-site signals showed no relationship with appearance), but we think it is worth checking on your own and your clients' sites as a basis for machines reading a company's facts correctly.
