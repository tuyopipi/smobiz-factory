AI search for used car dealers: getting AI to read your total prices, your stock and your licence details correctly

AI search optimization (AEO) for a used car dealer comes down to making your own site able to answer what buyers ask AI, such as "Which dealers in [city] have a small car under $8,000 all-in?" or "Does this dealer's price include fees?" Show each price as the total payable amount with its breakdown and conditions, write mileage, warranty, servicing and accident history for every car as text, mark sold cars as sold the same day, show your dealer licence details, and keep the same facts on your listing portals and Google Business Profile.

Written November 24, 2026 (JST). The display rules described here are Japanese: the used-car advertising code of the Japan Fair Trade Council for Automobiles (AFTC) and the Antique Dealings Act as explained by the Tokyo Metropolitan Police, as cited in the text. Rules differ by country and state, so check the ones that apply where you sell. This article is not legal advice.

AI answers change from day to day and with how a question is phrased. The steps here make your facts readable to AI. They do not guarantee that an AI will mention you or rank you anywhere.

What buyers ask AI about used cars

Used car buyers usually start from a model and a budget, then narrow down by where to buy, whether the advertised price is what they will actually pay, and whether the car is in good shape. AI answers these questions by combining dealer sites, portal listings, map data and reviews. If your site does not state the conditions in text, AI leans on other sources or answers vaguely, without the conditions.

Show the total payable price, with the breakdown and conditions in text

Since October 1, 2023, Japan's used-car advertising code has required dealers to show a "total payable amount" (支払総額) whenever they show a price. It is the vehicle price plus fees, shown with the breakdown and with conditions such as when and where the car is registered. Only insurance (compulsory), taxes and registration-related charges (paperwork for inspection, registration and parking certificates) count as fees. AFTC: the total payable price rule (PDF, Japanese)

Preparation charges, pre-delivery inspection and servicing, sales commission and advertising costs cannot be added as separate fees; they belong in the vehicle price. If a warranty or service plan is a condition of sale, its cost goes into the vehicle price too. The AFTC materials do not allow "fees: 0" or names such as "all-in price" in place of the term "total payable amount". AFTC pamphlet (PDF) / AFTC advertising checklist (PDF)

Even outside Japan, this is exactly what makes a price usable by AI. A bare number, or a price that exists only inside an image, gives AI no way to tell whether it is the total or the car alone. Put the total, the breakdown and the conditions in one sentence of text.

The AFTC materials ask dealers to update the total promptly when it changes, and say that writing "as of [month]" alone does not solve the problem.

The checklist lists describing a registered but unused car as "unused, 0 km" as a possible misleading claim. For AI too, plain numbers and the registration status lead to more accurate, conditional mentions than strong wording.

Handling sold cars and stock pages

Every used car is a single item. If a sold car's page still says it is for sale, AI may tell buyers you have it, and the enquiry ends in disappointment. Out-of-date source information is one of the common reasons AI gets a business wrong.

  1. On the day a sale is agreed, mark the car's page as sold and remove the price or show "sold" beside it. Update the portal listings the same day.
  2. If you keep sold pages for a while, write "Sold" as text near the heading and link to similar cars in stock. If you do not keep them, delete them and remove them from the stock list.
  3. Put the date of the last update on the stock list, with a sentence such as "Stock shown as of [date]".
  4. If you use structured data, set availability to SoldOut or remove it together with the page. Do not leave a sold page with InStock data.
  5. On your home or About page, say that stock changes daily and where the current stock list is. That makes it easier for AI to tell buyers to confirm by phone.

Causes of drift and how to fix them are covered in why AI visibility drops or goes wrong.

Licence details on the site (Japan: the second-hand dealer display)

In Japan, buying and selling used cars is a second-hand dealing business. Licensed second-hand dealers must show on their website their name, the name of the Public Safety Commission that granted the licence, and the licence number (Antique Dealings Act, Article 12(2), in force from April 1, 2024). Businesses with five or fewer regular employees are exempt, but dealers registered for internet transactions must show these details regardless of size. The display must be clear and easy for consumers to see. Tokyo Metropolitan Police: 2024 amendment (Japanese)

Dealers who trade online also register their website URL with the Public Safety Commission, and prefectural police publish the list of names, URLs and licence numbers. Shizuoka Prefectural Police (Japanese). If you change your site's URL in a redesign or domain move, check whether the registration needs updating.

This is a legal display, but for AI it is also a concrete signal that the dealer exists and is licensed. Put it as text, not an image, in the footer or on your company or About page. Outside Japan, show whatever dealer licence or registration your jurisdiction requires in the same way.

Business structured data: AutoDealer

schema.org has AutoDealer for dealerships. It is a kind of LocalBusiness (under AutomotiveBusiness). Publish your business with this type, using the same name, address, phone and hours as the page text (checked in schema-dts 2.1.0, November 24, 2026).

{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "AutoDealer",
  "@id": "https://example.com/#organization",
  "name": "Example Motors",
  "description": "Used car dealer in Example City. Mostly compact cars and minivans, with around 30 cars on the lot. Every price is shown as the total payable amount, with the fees itemised.",
  "address": { "@type": "PostalAddress", "streetAddress": "1-2-3 Example St, Example City" },
  "telephone": "+1-000-000-0000",
  "url": "https://example.com/",
  "openingHours": "Mo,Tu,Th,Fr,Sa,Su 10:00-19:00"
}

Each car as Car markup, and what changed at Google

Each car in stock can be described with Car, a kind of Vehicle and Product. It has properties for the first registration date (dateVehicleFirstRegistered), mileage (mileageFromOdometer), fuel, transmission, colour and a text description of known damages, repaired and unrepaired (knownVehicleDamages). The price goes in an Offer. In the example below, the price is the total payable amount, and the breakdown, conditions, warranty and servicing are in the description, in the same words as the page.

{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Car",
  "name": "Example Make Example Wagon G 2WD",
  "url": "https://example.com/stock/1234/",
  "dateVehicleFirstRegistered": "2021-04",
  "mileageFromOdometer": { "@type": "QuantitativeValue", "value": 31000, "unitCode": "KMT" },
  "fuelType": "Petrol",
  "vehicleTransmission": "CVT",
  "color": "Pearl white",
  "knownVehicleDamages": "No accident repair history",
  "offers": {
    "@type": "Offer",
    "price": "8900",
    "priceCurrency": "USD",
    "itemCondition": "https://schema.org/UsedCondition",
    "availability": "https://schema.org/InStock",
    "description": "Total payable $8,900 (vehicle $8,100 + fees $800), as of November 2026, for registration in this state and collection at our lot. 12-month warranty, no mileage limit. Service before handover included in the vehicle price.",
    "seller": { "@id": "https://example.com/#organization" }
  }
}

One caution. Google launched vehicle listing structured data for dealers in October 2023, but on September 9, 2025 it removed the documentation, saying the type is no longer shown in Google Search results. Google: Search Central updates / 2023 announcement. So do not expect Car markup to earn a special search feature. Google also says its AI features need no special markup. Google: AI features and your website. Treat Car markup as a machine-readable copy of the facts on the page: include only what is visible there, and never let it drift from the page.

Keep portals, Google Business Profile and Bing in step

A dealer's stock often appears on used-car portals before, or instead of, the dealer's own site. AI reads those listings too, so if the site and a portal disagree on the total price, mileage or warranty, AI cannot tell which is right and may use the older one. In Japan, the AFTC also asked dealers to change both their own websites and their used-car portal listings when the total-price rule came in.

Update in this order: (1) the source data in your inventory system, (2) your own site, (3) the portals, (4) Google Business Profile (hours, closed days, phone, website URL). Enter the same business details in Bing Places as well; see AI search and Bing Places.

Checklist before you publish

  1. Every price is a total payable amount, with the breakdown (vehicle + fees) and conditions (as of [month], where registered, how handed over) in text.
  2. No "plus fees", "fees: 0" or "all-in price" wording remains.
  3. Each car page states mileage, warranty (length and mileage), servicing before handover, accident repair history and (in Japan) the last three digits of the chassis number as text.
  4. No fact exists only inside a price-board photo or listing image.
  5. Sold cars are marked sold the same day, and their structured data is not left as InStock.
  6. The stock list shows an "as of [date]" update date.
  7. Licence details are in the footer or on the company page as text.
  8. The business structured data type is AutoDealer (or LocalBusiness), and name, address, phone and hours match the page.
  9. Prices, stock and hours match across your site, the portals, Google Business Profile and Bing Places.
  10. llms.txt lists the stock list, About page, warranty and servicing pages and company page. What is llms.txt?

Check whether AI mentions you, and whether it is right

Once your pages are in order, put the questions buyers would ask to AI. Check whether your name comes up for area-plus-condition questions such as "used small car under $8,000 all-in in [city]" or "dealers near [station] with no accident history", and also ask with your name, such as "Does this dealer's price include fees?" or "Do they deliver out of state?", to see whether the answers match your site. Check that AI does not say you still have cars you have sold.

FAQ

Where should a used car dealer start with AI search?

First, rewrite each car's price as a total payable amount with its breakdown and conditions, and write mileage, warranty, servicing and accident history for every car as text. Then set a rule to mark sold cars the same day and put your licence details in the footer. After that, publish your business as AutoDealer structured data and in llms.txt, and align your portals and Google Business Profile.

Does the total-price rule in Japan apply to a dealer's own website?

The AFTC materials include a website advert example and ask dealers to change both their own websites and their used-car portal listings to the total payable amount. For details, such as how the rule applies to non-members, ask the AFTC.

Where on the site should licence details go?

Japan's Antique Dealings Act requires a clear display that is easy for consumers to see. Putting the name, the granting Public Safety Commission and the licence number as text in the footer of every page, or on the company page, is the safest choice.

Does every car need structured data?

No. Car markup can carry mileage and first registration date, but Google removed its vehicle listing documentation in September 2025, so do not expect a special search feature. Writing the facts as text on the car page comes first. If you use markup, keep it identical to the page and update it when the car sells.

Can Nurevo AEO publish my stock?

No. Nurevo AEO publishes your business (such as AutoDealer), services, FAQs and llms.txt, not per-car data. Leave car pages to your inventory system or theme, and add dealer services such as servicing, warranty plans, trade-ins and finance advice to Nurevo AEO.

Is Nurevo AEO free?

Yes. Business type, address, hours, services and FAQ structured data, llms.txt, AI crawler rules, the AEO score and the monthly free AI check are on the free plan. Will this guarantee ChatGPT recommends my dealership?

No. AI answers change, and no tool or technique can guarantee a mention or a position. What you can do is make your price and stock facts easy for AI to read, and keep checking whether you appear and whether the answers are correct.
