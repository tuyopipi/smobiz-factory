AI search readiness by prefecture: 2,098 websites of Japanese web design agencies (November 2026)

We grouped 2,098 websites of Japanese web design agencies by prefecture. Across the 23 prefectures with at least 30 reachable sites, the average of six AI-search-related items ranged from 3.69 (Kyoto) to 4.38 (Hiroshima), and llms.txt adoption from 5.9% (Niigata) to 22.5% (Hiroshima). With so few sites per prefecture, these differences are not statistically clear (chi-square p = 0.36). By region, llms.txt adoption stayed between 11.8% and 15.0%.

Published 2026-11-16. Data aggregated 2026-11-12. Research by Nurevo AEO (Bestie.合同会社). Nurevo sells AI search optimization (AEO) tools and a WordPress plugin, and so has an interest in this topic.

Key findings

  1. Among the 23 prefectures with 30+ reachable sites, the highest 6-item average was Hiroshima (4.38, 40 sites), followed by Gifu (4.36, 50) and Kanagawa (4.35, 80); the lowest was Kyoto (3.69, 39). The national average was 4.05.
  2. llms.txt adoption was highest in Hiroshima (22.5%, 9 of 40), Hokkaido (20.9%, 27 of 129) and Gifu (18.0%, 9 of 50), and lowest in Niigata (5.9%, 2 of 34), Okinawa (6.1%, 2 of 33) and Fukuoka (7.0%, 4 of 57).
  3. The differences between prefectures are not statistically clear (across 23 prefectures: llms.txt p = 0.36, share with 5+ items p = 0.56, JSON-LD p = 0.79). Across six regions, llms.txt ranged 11.8–15.0% (p = 0.90), and Tokyo (16.4%) differed from the rest of Japan (12.8%) by only a few points.

Method

  1. Population: web design agencies' own websites. In Japan, 3,352 sites confirmed as agencies, of which 2,486 home pages could be reached. Of these, 2,098 have a known prefecture in our collection list and are grouped by prefecture (388 have no location). Not businesses in general.
  2. Fetched: each site's home page, /robots.txt, /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt, once each (User-Agent: NurevoSurvey/1.0), early November to 12 November 2026; aggregated 2026-11-12.
  3. Classification: llms.txt = HTTP 200, not HTML, more than 20 characters. JSON-LD = present on the home page; Organization-type = an @type of Organization, LocalBusiness, Corporation or ProfessionalService. WordPress = the home page HTML contains wp-content/ or wp-includes/, or a WordPress generator tag. AI bot blocking = the robots.txt rules that apply to any of GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot or OAI-SearchBot disallow the home page (/) (longest match); the base is sites with a robots.txt.
  4. The six items: (1) has an llms.txt; (2) has JSON-LD; (3) has an Organization or LocalBusiness type; (4) has a robots.txt that blocks none of GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and OAI-SearchBot; (5) has a meta description; (6) has a canonical link. The count (0–6) is a simple measure made for this survey, not Nurevo's 0–100 product score.
  5. Prefecture is the location in our collection list; entries that include a city were assigned to their prefecture. The 24 prefectures with fewer than 30 reachable sites are not shown individually but are included in the regional figures. Regions: Hokkaido & Tohoku (7 prefectures), Kanto (7), Chubu (Niigata, Toyama, Ishikawa, Fukui, Yamanashi, Nagano, Gifu, Shizuoka, Aichi), Kinki (7, including Mie), Chugoku & Shikoku (9), Kyushu & Okinawa (8).

How solid are the differences?

Prefectures have 30 to 299 sites each; with 40 sites, one site moves a share by 2.5 points. We used chi-square tests to see whether the differences go beyond chance. Across the 23 prefectures, llms.txt gave p = 0.36, the share with 5+ items p = 0.56 and JSON-LD p = 0.79; across the six regions, llms.txt gave p = 0.90. None of these supports a real difference.

In other words, this data cannot tell which prefecture's agencies are ahead on AI search readiness. Read the ranking as a rough guide, not as a measure of regional capability.

Other observations (facts)

  1. Every prefecture had between 0 and 3 sites blocking an AI bot in robots.txt (29 of 2,281 sites with a robots.txt nationwide, 1.3%).
  2. Kyoto (76.9%) and Miyagi (79.4%), both lower on the 6-item average, also had lower WordPress shares. Nationally, WordPress sites average 4.2 on the six items and non-WordPress sites 2.1 (score distribution study). The WordPress mix may show up in prefecture figures, but we did not test this further.
  3. Tokyo (299 sites) vs the rest of Japan (1,799): llms.txt 16.4% vs 12.8%, 6-item average 4.16 vs 4.02, 5+ items 55.5% vs 51.0%.

Limitations

  1. The population is web design agencies' own websites, not businesses in general. Counts per prefecture reflect our collection list, not the number or distribution of agencies in each prefecture (Tokyo's 299 sites, for instance, do not match its real share of businesses).
  2. Location comes from our list; companies whose head office and studio differ, or listing errors, are possible. The 388 sites without a location are not in the prefecture figures.
  3. Only the home page was examined. robots.txt was judged for / only; partial blocking and blocking by firewalls or CDNs were not counted. For llms.txt we checked only whether it exists, not its quality.
  4. One fetch per site; unreachable sites are excluded. Part of this aggregation reuses fetches from the 5 November run.
  5. With 30 to 299 sites per prefecture, ranks change with a few sites, and statistical tests did not show clear differences.

Our reading (opinion)

What follows is our opinion, not a finding. In this data, AI search readiness seems to depend far more on what each site does than on where the agency is. Checking whether your own or your clients' sites are missing llms.txt, structured data or canonical links, one by one, is likely more useful than comparing prefecture averages. None of this guarantees appearing in AI answers (in another of our studies, on-site signals showed no relationship with appearance).

FAQ

Which prefectures are furthest ahead?

Among the 23 prefectures with 30+ sites, Hiroshima (4.38), Gifu (4.36) and Kanagawa (4.35) had the highest 6-item averages. With so few sites per prefecture, the differences are not statistically clear (November 2026).

Are Tokyo agencies ahead?

Slightly: llms.txt 16.4% in Tokyo vs 12.8% elsewhere, 6-item average 4.16 vs 4.02.

Why are some prefectures missing?

The 24 prefectures with fewer than 30 reachable sites are not shown, because a few sites would swing their figures. They are included in the regional totals.
