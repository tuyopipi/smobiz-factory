AI search optimization for SWELL and Lightning sites: keep the theme's structured data, add the missing facts, avoid duplicates

SWELL and Lightning (with VK All in One Expansion Unit) publish structured data that describes the site and its articles, such as WebSite and Article. In what we checked, they do not publish the address, telephone, opening hours and services an AI uses to describe a business, nor llms.txt, nor rules that let AI search crawlers in. SEO SIMPLE PACK publishes no structured data at all. The working method is to keep the theme's output, add only the missing facts as part of the same business, and then check each page type once for duplicates.

Written November 11, 2026 (JST). What SWELL publishes is taken from a Japanese write-up that tested SWELL 2.19.0 on September 30, 2026 (linked below). SWELL's own site could not be read for this guide, and the output can differ between theme versions, so use the checks later in this guide on your own site.

No setting guarantees that ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI will mention you, or at what position. AI answers change from day to day. The goal here is narrower: make sure AI reads your facts correctly.

In every setup, the site and its articles are already described to some degree. What is missing is the facts about the business and the entry points made for AI (llms.txt and crawler access). Filling those two gaps is most of the work on a themed site.

Why the theme's structured data is not enough

To answer "which osteopath near me is open on Saturday?" or "which web agency in Osaka is good with WordPress?", an AI needs facts: the kind of business, where it is, the telephone number, the opening hours, the services and a rough idea of price. A theme's Article and WebSite nodes describe something else: who wrote this page and when. They contain none of those facts.

An AI can of course read an address or opening hours from the page text. But facts spread across a footer, a banner image and a contact page are easy to misread. Stating the same facts plainly in structured data and in llms.txt gives the AI more material to cross-check against the page. What matters most is that the page text, the structured data and your Google Business Profile all say the same thing.

First, check for duplicates: a five-minute test

  1. Pick three pages: the front page, an ordinary blog post, and a page that has an FAQ block (if you have one).
  2. Run each one through Google's Rich Results Test or the Schema Markup Validator and write down the types (@type) it finds.
  3. Look for any type that appears twice. Watch four cases in particular: two business nodes (Organization or LocalBusiness) under different @id values, two WebSite nodes, an Article plus a separate BlogPosting, and two FAQPage nodes.
  4. Open the page source in your browser and search for "application/ld+json". This shows how many blocks there are and helps you tell which come from the theme and which from a plugin.
  5. Where there is a duplicate, decide which one to keep and switch off the other (next section). Then test the same three pages again.

A duplicate is not always an immediate problem. But a page that seems to describe two companies, or two articles, makes AI systems and search engines do extra guessing. Once you have checked, the result holds until you update the theme or a plugin.

SWELL with SEO SIMPLE PACK

This is the most common pairing. Keep SEO SIMPLE PACK for meta and OGP tags, keep SWELL's structured data, and add what is missing. With Nurevo AEO (version 0.9.1) it works like this:

  1. In Nurevo AEO's settings, enter the business type (dentist, hair salon and so on), address, telephone, opening hours, email, services and FAQ. Blank fields are filled where your SEO plugin or WordPress settings already hold the value. Values you typed are never overwritten.
  2. With SEO SIMPLE PACK active, Nurevo AEO reads your own front page (on activation, when settings are saved, when the set of plugins changes, and daily) and records which types other code already publishes. If it sees SWELL's WebSite, it leaves its own WebSite out.
  3. If SWELL's Organization has no address or telephone, Nurevo AEO publishes the business facts under the same @id, so the two are read as one business. If SWELL's Organization has no @id, Nurevo AEO uses its own.
  4. Watch out: that check reads only the front page. SWELL prints no Article there, so on blog posts you may see both SWELL's Article and Nurevo AEO's BlogPosting. Test a post with the steps above and, if both appear, keep one.
  5. How to keep one: to keep SWELL's Article, remove the BlogPosting and WebPage nodes from core with Nurevo AEO's developer filter webmcp_canary_schema_graph. To hand everything to Nurevo AEO instead, turn off "JSON-LDを自動生成する" (generate JSON-LD automatically) under SWELL settings > 構造化データ (structured data). Note that this also stops SWELL's BreadcrumbList, and Nurevo AEO does not publish breadcrumbs.
  6. Use one FAQ source per page. The structured data from SWELL's FAQ block is not stopped by the general JSON-LD switch. If you also place the Nurevo FAQ block (or [nurevo_faq]) on the same page, there will be two FAQPage nodes, so choose one.

The SWELL setting name, and the fact that the FAQ block runs separately from it, come from a write-up on turning off SWELL's JSON-LD (in Japanese), tested on SWELL 2.19.0. If you run SWELL with no SEO plugin at all, Nurevo AEO 0.9.1 does not stand down for anything and publishes its full set, so WebSite and similar nodes are more likely to overlap. Run the same check in that case too.

Lightning with VK ExUnit

On Lightning, structured data is mostly ExUnit's job. It comes from two ExUnit functions, "Structured data - WebSite" and "Structured data - Article", each of which can be switched on or off in ExUnit's activation settings. Nurevo AEO 0.9.1 adapts to ExUnit as follows:

  1. On pages where ExUnit prints its WebSite or Article, Nurevo AEO leaves its own WebSite and article nodes out.
  2. ExUnit's Article gets only what it lacks (a publisher pointing at your business, the description and the language), through ExUnit's veu_article_structure_data filter. Nothing ExUnit sets, the author included, is changed.
  3. ExUnit publishes no business node, so the one with your address, telephone and opening hours comes only from Nurevo AEO.
  4. If ExUnit's structured data is turned off, Nurevo AEO publishes its own WebSite and article nodes instead.
  5. Lightning's breadcrumb uses microdata and Nurevo AEO publishes no breadcrumbs, so there is no overlap there.

Lightning itself is still being updated; version 15.42.0 shipped on September 9, 2026 (changelog). Moving the breadcrumb is a Lightning G3 Pro Unit feature, not part of the free theme (Lightning features). After updating the theme or ExUnit, run the duplicate check again.

llms.txt and AI crawler rules are separate from the theme

In the material checked for this guide, none of SWELL, Lightning, SEO SIMPLE PACK or ExUnit lists llms.txt output or a setting that allows AI search crawlers by name. This part does not change when you change theme, so set it up with a plugin or by hand.

Even when robots.txt allows a crawler, a bot-protection setting at your host or CDN can still block it. If your hosting control panel has bot protection, check that it is not stopping the AI search crawlers.

Checklist

  1. On the front page, a post and a page with an FAQ, there is exactly one business node, one WebSite, one article node and at most one FAQPage
  2. The business node has the business type, address, telephone and opening hours, written the same way as on the page and in your Google Business Profile
  3. Each page uses either SWELL's FAQ block or Nurevo's, never both
  4. https://(your domain)/llms.txt opens and lists your main pages: pricing, services, company information and directions
  5. robots.txt allows the AI search crawlers, and you have decided yourself what to do with the training crawlers
  6. After updating the theme, SEO SIMPLE PACK or ExUnit, you repeat the three-page duplicate check

FAQ

If I use SWELL, is my structured data enough for AI search?

SWELL describes your site and articles (WebSite, Article, breadcrumbs and so on). In what we checked, it does not include a business node with address, telephone and opening hours, llms.txt or AI crawler rules. For a shop or company site, adding those makes your facts easier for AI to confirm.

Does SEO SIMPLE PACK output structured data?

No. It prints meta tags, OGP tags and verification codes (checked in version 3.7.0's code). Paired with SWELL, your structured data therefore comes mainly from SWELL.

Should I turn off SWELL's JSON-LD?

Usually there is no need. Add the missing business facts and check that blog posts do not end up with two article nodes. If you do turn it off, remember that SWELL's breadcrumb structured data stops too.

Will Nurevo AEO duplicate ExUnit's structured data on Lightning?

Nurevo AEO 0.9.1 leaves out its own WebSite and article nodes on pages where ExUnit prints them, and adds only what ExUnit's Article lacks. ExUnit publishes no business node, so there is a single business node.

Will adding llms.txt get me into ChatGPT's answers?

Nothing guarantees that. llms.txt is a helper that hands AI a readable summary of your site. Use it together with consistent facts in your page text, structured data and Google Business Profile.

If I switch from SWELL to Lightning, what do I need to redo?

The duplicate check. Each theme publishes different structured data, so test the front page, a post and a page with an FAQ again after switching. llms.txt and robots.txt do not depend on the theme, so if a plugin serves them they carry over.
