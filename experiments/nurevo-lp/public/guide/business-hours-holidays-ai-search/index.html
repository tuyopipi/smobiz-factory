Opening Hours, Closing Days, Holidays and Temporary Closures: How to Make AI Answer Correctly

To get AI to state your hours correctly, write your regular hours, your closing days and how you handle public holidays as HTML text in one place on your site. Give every holiday or temporary closure exact dates ("closed from December 24 to December 26" or the changed hours) and update your site, Google Business Profile and structured data on the same day. If you will be closed for seven days or more, or don't know when you'll reopen, use "Temporarily closed" in Google Business Profile rather than special hours. Once a closure has passed, delete the notice or mark it as ended so old dates don't linger.

Written November 20, 2026 (JST). How to write prices is covered in How to write a pricing page AI reads correctly. This guide is for every business people visit, such as shops, clinics, studios and offices, and focuses on one thing: telling AI whether you are open and until when. It is written so you can act on it before the holiday season.

AI answers change from day to day, between phrasings and between services. These steps make your hours harder for AI to misread and show you how to check the answers. They don't guarantee that you'll appear in an answer or that it will be correct.

Wrong hours in AI answers are common. Search Engine Journal (July 20, 2026) reported a test by the AI visibility company Searchable in which 64% of UK high-street retailers had at least one false fact in AI answers, and singled out wrong hours and a branch "reported as closed" as errors to fix first (Search Engine Journal; a vendor test as reported by the article, covering the UK).

A correct Google Business Profile doesn't rule out old sources. On a local search forum, a business owner reported that their profile and Google Search showed the right hours while Google's AI Overview quoted old hours from spreadsheet files dating back to 2023 (Local Search Forum, October 2026; no fix was posted). Old documents on your own site and elsewhere are part of what you need to clean up.

On your site: six things the hours block needs

When asked "Is it open on Sunday?" or "Can I go now?", AI pulls the hours section out of your page. If the exceptions sit somewhere else, they tend to be dropped. Put these six things together on your location page (your contact or about page).

What to writeExampleWhat happens if it's missing
Hours for each dayMon, Wed–Fri 10:00–19:00; Sat–Sun 9:00–18:00Weekday hours get applied to weekends
Closing daysClosed TuesdaysAI says you're open on your day off
Public holidaysOpen on public holidays (if a holiday falls on a Tuesday we open and close the next day)AI guesses
Last entry or last orderLast appointment 18:00People are told they can arrive just before closing
Breaks and split hoursClosed 13:00–14:00AI says you're open during your break
Hours past midnightFri–Sat 18:00–02:00 (next day)Read as opening at 2 a.m.

Bad: "Open from 10, closed irregularly." Good: "Hours: Mon and Wed–Fri 10:00–19:00, Sat–Sun 9:00–18:00 (last appointment one hour before closing). Closed Tuesdays. Open on public holidays; if a holiday falls on a Tuesday we open and close on the Wednesday. Temporary closures are posted with dates under News on this page."

If you close irregularly, say how it is decided: "Twice a month, announced on this page by the 25th of the previous month." Telling people where to look also helps AI point them to your site. If your hours are shown as an image, add the same content as text.

How to write holiday and temporary closure notices

For a closure with a start and an end, write the dates, the weekdays, and whether you are closed or on changed hours, one line each. "We'll be closed over the holidays" leaves AI unable to say when.

  1. Where: next to the hours block on your location page, and in a news post. Not only as a banner image on the home page
  2. How: "Closed Thursday December 24 to Saturday December 26, 2026. Open as usual from Sunday December 27. Closing at 17:00 on Wednesday December 23."
  3. Include the year: "12/24–12/26" alone can't be told apart from last year's notice
  4. When it's over: delete the notice that day, or add "This closure has ended" at the top. Remove any line you added next to the hours block too
  5. Sudden closures: a short reason is enough, but give the date and the reopening date. If you don't know yet, say "We'll announce the reopening date on this page"
  6. Long closures or moves: give the period, the new address and how to reach you. Don't leave a page with the old address up after a move

Rewriting the same "Holiday opening hours" page every year leaves fewer stale pages than creating a new post each year. If you do create a new one, mark last year's post with "This notice is from 2025" and link to the current one.

Google Business Profile: special hours or "Temporarily closed"

How Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode use Google Business Profile isn't published. But the profile feeds the business details in Google Maps and Search directly, so update it the same day as your site. Google's help pages draw the line like this (Special hours, Mark your business as closed, checked November 20, 2026).

SituationSettingWhat the help pages note
Changed hours or a closure for a holiday or event, up to six days in a rowSpecial hoursGoogle suggests confirming hours for official holidays even if they're the same as usual
Closed for seven days or more, for the off-season, or with no reopening dateTemporarily closedFor broad searches, temporarily closed businesses can rank after open ones
Special hours past midnightSplit across two dates (19:00–24:00 and 00:00– the next day)The next day's regular hours are then ignored, so re-enter them
ReopeningSwitch back to "Open with main hours"The temporarily closed label disappears

The two help pages word the seven-day line slightly differently: the special hours page says seven days or more means "Temporarily closed", the closure page says more than seven days. For a closure of exactly seven days, setting special hours and posting a dated notice on your site covers both readings.

Special hours step by step: at business.google.com choose Edit profile, then Hours, then edit Special hours. Review a suggested date or add a date, tick Closed or enter the hours, and save. Special hours only show if the profile is set to "Open with main hours". Google's list of holidays shows, country by country, the days on which a profile displays a note that hours may differ.

For several locations, Search Engine Roundtable reported a feature that copies special hours from one location to others; it overwrites and copies old special hours as well (Search Engine Roundtable, 2025-01-28). See AI search for multi-location businesses for managing locations.

Structured data: closing days, past-midnight hours and holiday closures

Structured data restates the hours on your page in a machine-readable form. It doesn't replace the page text and must match it. In Google's LocalBusiness structured data documentation (updated September 2026), openingHoursSpecification is a recommended property, with these patterns in its examples.

<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "HairSalon",
  "@id": "https://example.com/#business",
  "name": "Example Hair Studio",
  "openingHoursSpecification": [
    {
      "@type": "OpeningHoursSpecification",
      "dayOfWeek": ["Monday", "Wednesday", "Thursday", "Friday"],
      "opens": "10:00",
      "closes": "19:00"
    },
    {
      "@type": "OpeningHoursSpecification",
      "dayOfWeek": ["Saturday", "Sunday"],
      "opens": "09:00",
      "closes": "18:00"
    },
    {
      "@type": "OpeningHoursSpecification",
      "opens": "00:00",
      "closes": "00:00",
      "validFrom": "2026-12-24",
      "validThrough": "2026-12-26"
    }
  ]
}
</script>
<!-- Tuesday is left out = closed. The third entry is a holiday closure, written the way
     Google's own example writes it. Use the same dates as the notice on the page and
     delete it once the dates have passed -->
What you want to sayHow (from Google's examples)
Regular hoursDays in dayOfWeek, times in opens and closes (10:00); one entry per group of days with the same hours
Closing dayLeave the day out, or give it opens and closes of 00:00
Open 24 hoursopens 00:00, closes 23:59
Past midnightOne entry: Saturday, opens 18:00, closes 03:00 means Saturday 6 p.m. to Sunday 3 a.m.
Holiday closureDates in validFrom and validThrough, opens and closes 00:00

schema.org also has specialOpeningHoursSpecification, meant to override regular hours (schema.org). Google's documentation doesn't mention it, and Google's own holiday example uses validFrom and validThrough as above. schema.org also describes a place as "open if the opens property is specified, and closed otherwise", which is worded differently from Google's 00:00–00:00 for closed (OpeningHoursSpecification). When in doubt, follow Google's examples.

The day values include PublicHolidays, but schema.org doesn't say which country's holidays it means, and Google's examples don't use it. If your holiday hours differ, give each holiday its own dated entry or say it in the page text. Check the syntax with the Rich Results Test or the Schema Markup Validator. In our research we found no official statement that ChatGPT or Perplexity use opening-hours structured data.

WordPress steps (with Nurevo AIO)

In Nurevo AIO (free, formerly listed as Nurevo AEO), what you type in the Opening hours setting is published in your business's structured data and on the "Hours" line of llms.txt. Work in this order.

  1. Fix the page text first, using the six things above (page text before structured data)
  2. In Opening hours, enter only your weekly pattern and closing days ("Mon, Wed-Fri 10:00-19:00 / Sat-Sun 9:00-18:00, closed Tuesdays"). Common English and Japanese forms are published as day-by-day openingHoursSpecification
  3. If you add public holidays, "second Tuesday" or last-order notes, the plugin doesn't risk a misreading: it publishes the text exactly as typed (as an openingHours string) and never invents hours. Keep exceptions in the page text and the field in weekly form to get the day-by-day output
  4. "Until 24:00" is published as 23:59 and "18:00-02:00" as past-midnight hours. An ambiguous range such as "9-5" with no a.m./p.m. is published as typed; write "9:00-17:00" or "9am-5pm" instead
  5. To put a holiday closure into structured data, the developer filter webmcp_canary_business_node can add one entry in Google's format (code below). Delete it when the dates have passed
  6. If your SEO plugin or theme already publishes your business's name, address, phone and hours, Nurevo doesn't publish a second business entity, so the filter has no effect; change the hours in that plugin or theme instead. Nurevo works without duplicates alongside Yoast SEO, Rank Math, All in One SEO and Slim SEO, and since 0.9.1 alongside SEO SIMPLE PACK and VK All in One Expansion Unit
  7. Sites set up as an online shop or B2B publish no hours in structured data or llms.txt, since there is no place to visit
add_filter('webmcp_canary_business_node', function ($org, $settings) {
    // Holiday closure. Same dates as the notice on the page; delete once they have passed
    if (!empty($org['openingHoursSpecification']) && is_array($org['openingHoursSpecification'])) {
        $org['openingHoursSpecification'][] = array(
            '@type'        => 'OpeningHoursSpecification',
            'opens'        => '00:00',
            'closes'       => '00:00',
            'validFrom'    => '2026-12-24',
            'validThrough' => '2026-12-26',
        );
    }
    return $org;
}, 10, 2);

Add it to your child theme's functions.php or a code snippets plugin. Then check the page source or the Rich Results Test to confirm the closure entry appears on your business. The llms.txt "Hours" line repeats the Opening hours field as typed, so if you added a holiday note to the field, remove it there too once it has passed.

How to check: fix your questions and keep a record

Ask the same questions to the same AI on different days and record the answers. Don't judge from a single answer.

"What time does (business) close?"Hours per day and last entry are right
"Is (business) open on public holidays?"It repeats what you wrote rather than guessing
"What are (business)'s holiday hours?"It gives this year's dates, not last year's

FAQ

If I fix Google Business Profile, will ChatGPT and Perplexity be fixed too?

Not necessarily. How ChatGPT and Perplexity use Google Business Profile isn't published. Update your site, directories and social profiles the same day and check the pages cited as sources.

How do I mark a closing day in structured data?

Leave that day out, or give it opens and closes of 00:00. Google's documentation shows both. Say "Closed Tuesdays" in the page text too.

Our hours differ on public holidays. Can structured data say that?

There is a PublicHolidays day value, but schema.org doesn't say which country's holidays it covers and Google's examples don't use it. Give each holiday a dated entry with validFrom and validThrough, or say it in the page text. In Google Business Profile, set special hours for each holiday.

Does "Temporarily closed" hurt us in search?

Google's help says temporarily closed businesses can rank after open ones for broad searches. For closures of up to six days use special hours, and switch back to "Open with main hours" as soon as you reopen.

AI keeps quoting last year's holiday closure.

Check the source URL it shows. If it's last year's notice, delete it or mark it as ended, and add the year if it was missing. How long updates take isn't published, so ask the same question again a few days to a few weeks later.

Our hours are shown as an image. Is that a problem?

Whether an image can be read differs by AI service and isn't published. Keep the image if you like, but add the same content as HTML text.

Can I put public holidays in Nurevo's Opening hours field?

You can, but then the field is published exactly as typed rather than day by day, to avoid a misreading. Keep the field to your weekly hours and closing days and put holidays and closures in the page text to get the day-by-day output.
