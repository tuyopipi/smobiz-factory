How Perplexity cites websites, and how to check whether it cites yours

Perplexity searches the web when it gets a question and lists the pages it read as numbered sources. What a site owner can do is (1) make sure robots.txt and the firewall (WAF) do not block Perplexity's two crawlers, PerplexityBot for search and Perplexity-User for fetching pages when someone asks; (2) state answers and facts in the page text; and (3) record, again and again, whether unbranded questions actually cite you. Perplexity has not published how it chooses which pages to cite.

Written 18 November 2026 (JST). For a ChatGPT-focused walkthrough see How to appear in ChatGPT; for measuring AI visibility in general see How to measure AI visibility. This guide covers Perplexity only: what can be confirmed from Perplexity's own documentation, and the checks you can run on your site.

AI answers change with the day and the wording. These steps make your site readable by Perplexity and help you measure citations correctly; they do not guarantee an appearance or a position in any answer.

What Perplexity has published, and what it hasn't

Perplexity answers usually carry numbers in the text and a list of source links, so you can see which page was used where. That makes "were we cited?" easier to check than on most AI products. What is not public is how it gathers candidate pages, how it ranks them and how many it cites.

Top-ranking articles quote specific figures, such as "5-10 candidates, 3-4 cited" or "20-50 candidates". They contradict each other, and none could be traced to Perplexity's documentation. This guide treats only what Perplexity documents as fact.

Two crawlers: PerplexityBot and Perplexity-User

The two crawlers in Perplexity's documentation have different jobs and a different relationship with robots.txt. If you allow only one, you can misread what is happening.

User-agent strings and IP ranges are in Perplexity's crawler page and its PerplexityBot guide. The two pages show the user agent differently and host the IP files on slightly different domains, so check the current page when you add WAF rules.

robots.txt and WAF rules: letting Perplexity in, or keeping it out

If you want Perplexity to cite your site, the starting point is not blocking either crawler. If you give them their own robots.txt groups, repeat the rules you apply site-wide (for example, keeping /wp-admin/ out) inside those groups: a crawler that finds a group with its own name follows only that group.

# Allow the crawlers Perplexity uses for search and answers
User-agent: PerplexityBot
Allow: /
Disallow: /wp-admin/

User-agent: Perplexity-User
Allow: /
Disallow: /wp-admin/

Even with robots.txt fixed, a server or CDN rule can refuse the request first: a WAF, a hosting provider's foreign-IP restriction, or a security plugin. Perplexity's documentation recommends WAF rules that match both the user agent and the IP range, because a rule based on the name alone also lets in anything that copies the name.

If you do not want Perplexity to read your site, disallow PerplexityBot in robots.txt and block Perplexity-User in the WAF. Perplexity can still build answers about you from other sites. Either way, Perplexity says changes can take up to 24 hours to apply.

Cloudflare's 2025 report: what it means for you

On 4 August 2025, Cloudflare reported that Perplexity had used undeclared crawlers to access test domains that disallowed crawling, and said it had removed Perplexity from its list of verified bots (Cloudflare blog). Perplexity disputed the report (PC Gamer, 5 August 2025).

For site owners this means two things. First, if you use Cloudflare or another CDN, a setting that blocks AI bots may also be blocking Perplexity. If you want to be cited, check the bot settings in your dashboard. Second, if you want to keep Perplexity out, consider a WAF rule rather than relying on robots.txt alone. This guide does not judge which side of the dispute is right.

What you can do on the page to be a usable source

Perplexity does not publish its selection criteria, but a page without usable facts will not be cited. The points below make a page easier for any AI to use as evidence, not just Perplexity. For writing in detail, see How to write articles AI cites.

  1. Answer the question in the first one or two sentences under the heading (for "Which dentists in X are open on Saturday?", state the days and hours).
  2. Put the facts that answer questions (prices, hours, service area, who it is for) in HTML text or tables, not only in images or PDFs.
  3. Give every number a date ("revised November 2026") and, for other people's numbers, a source link.
  4. Keep each page to a narrow set of questions. Split services or locations into their own pages, and keep facts consistent between pages.
  5. Keep your name, address and contact details the same across your about page, structured data and outside profiles such as Google Business Profile.
  6. If the main text only appears after JavaScript runs, render it on the server (whether the crawlers run JavaScript is not published).

In Nurevo's study of 62 local businesses in Japan, our on-site AEO score showed no relationship with how often Perplexity named or cited a business (study details). Structured data and llms.txt are groundwork that makes a site readable; it is safer not to expect them to raise citations on their own.

Steps in WordPress

When there is no robots.txt file in the site root, WordPress generates robots.txt itself (a virtual robots.txt). Rules added by plugins go into that virtual file. If a real robots.txt file exists on the server, it takes priority and plugin settings do not show up.

  1. Open https://your-domain/robots.txt in a browser. Check for PerplexityBot and Perplexity-User lines, and that User-agent: * does not disallow the whole site.
  2. With Nurevo AEO (free), the setting that allows AI search crawlers in the virtual robots.txt adds lines allowing PerplexityBot and Perplexity-User (and the ChatGPT and Claude search crawlers). Your existing rules, such as /wp-admin/, are repeated inside those groups. AI training crawlers are left as they are by default.
  3. If a robots.txt file exists on the server, add the lines from the example above to it, or remove the file to use the virtual one (copy its contents first).
  4. Check that your security plugin, hosting WAF or CDN is not blocking bots or foreign IPs. If it is, add Perplexity's published IP ranges to the allow list.
  5. Content: write the facts that answer questions as text on your pages. Nurevo AEO publishes your business, company and service facts as structured data and in llms.txt, but it does not add facts your pages don't state.
  6. Run the commands below to check robots.txt and the response to a request with the PerplexityBot user agent.
# 1) Does robots.txt have lines for both crawlers?
curl -s https://example.com/robots.txt | grep -i -A3 perplexity

# 2) Does a request with the PerplexityBot user agent get a 200? (403, 503 or a challenge page is a problem)
curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}\n" \
  -A "Mozilla/5.0 AppleWebKit/537.36 (KHTML, like Gecko; compatible; PerplexityBot/1.0; +https://perplexity.ai/perplexitybot)" \
  https://example.com/

# 3) Are they actually visiting? (the log location depends on your server)
grep -E "PerplexityBot|Perplexity-User" access.log | tail -20

The second command only checks whether you block by name. It does not test rules based on IP ranges, so use the access log (the third command) to see whether the crawlers really visit. If PerplexityBot shows up with 403 or 503 responses, a WAF or similar rule is stopping it.

How to check whether Perplexity cites you

Perplexity answers change from one ask to the next, so don't judge from a single try. Combine these three methods.

MethodWhat it showsWhat it misses, and cautions
Ask by hand and recordFor unbranded questions ("Saturday dentist in X"), whether your name appears, whether your URL is in the source list, and whereOne try is mostly chance. Ask the same questions on different days, with the same login state and location settings
Analytics referrersVisits from links in answers (when a referrer such as perplexity.ai is kept)Citations nobody clicked and name-only mentions are invisible. Some apps do not pass a referrer
Server access logsWhether PerplexityBot and Perplexity-User visit, which pages they read, and whether they get errorsBeing read is not the same as being used in an answer

Use branded questions ("What are X Dental's Saturday hours?") to check whether facts are read correctly, and leave them out of the appearance rate. A branded question almost always returns you, and it is not what a new customer asks.

Checklist

  1. robots.txt has no line blocking PerplexityBot or Perplexity-User (including User-agent: * with Disallow: /)
  2. If they have their own groups, site-wide rules such as /wp-admin/ are repeated in them
  3. The WAF, CDN and security plugins do not block Perplexity's published IP ranges
  4. A request with the PerplexityBot user agent gets a 200
  5. The access log shows PerplexityBot visits without errors
  6. The facts that answer questions (prices, hours, area, who it is for) are in the page text
  7. Numbers have a date, and other people's numbers have a source
  8. Name, address and contact details match on your site and outside profiles
  9. You have a fixed set of unbranded questions and ask them several times under the same conditions
  10. You note the date of each setting change and check no earlier than 24 hours later

FAQ

If I allow PerplexityBot, will Perplexity cite me?

Not necessarily. Allowing it is what lets Perplexity read your pages; Perplexity decides which pages to cite, and its criteria are not public.

If I disallow Perplexity-User in robots.txt, will it stop reading my site?

Perplexity's documentation says Perplexity-User generally ignores robots.txt rules because a user requested the fetch. To block it, consider a WAF rule that matches its user agent and IP ranges.

Does Perplexity use pages it reads to train AI models?

Perplexity's documentation says neither PerplexityBot nor Perplexity-User is used to collect content for training AI foundation models.

Do robots.txt changes apply immediately?

Perplexity says changes can take up to 24 hours to apply, so check the next day. Whether you then appear in answers is a separate question.

Do paid Perplexity plans cite different sources?

Plans and modes may build answers differently, but differences in how sources are chosen are not published. When you record results, keep the plan, mode and login state the same.

Does an llms.txt file help with Perplexity citations?

We found no official statement that Perplexity uses a site's llms.txt to choose sources. If you publish one, treat it as an entry point to your main pages and a summary of facts. See the llms.txt guide.

Perplexity says something wrong about us. What can we do?

Trace the sources in the answer. Fix outdated text on your own site, and ask other publishers to update theirs. You cannot edit AI answers directly, and there is no set time for changes to show.
