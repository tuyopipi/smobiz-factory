How to read Bing Webmaster Tools' AI Performance report: turn Copilot citations, grounding queries and citation share into page fixes

Bing Webmaster Tools' "AI Performance" report shows how often pages from your site were shown as sources in Microsoft Copilot and Bing's AI-generated summaries. It reports citation counts, which pages were cited and the phrases the AI used to find content (grounding queries). It does not report clicks or where you appeared inside an answer. The practical use is to see which pages are cited for which phrases, then fix the important pages that are not cited.

Written November 23, 2026. Bing's features were checked that day on Microsoft's own blogs (Bing Webmaster Blog, Bing Search Blog and the Microsoft Advertising blog). Parts of AI Performance are in preview or beta, so names and contents can change. Check the signed-in screen and Bing's help pages too.

This report only covers citations inside Microsoft's AI experiences. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews are not included. And nothing you do can guarantee that an AI answer will cite you or rank you anywhere. AI answers change from day to day.

What AI Performance counts

Bing launched AI Performance in Bing Webmaster Tools as a public preview on February 10, 2026 (Bing Webmaster Blog). It covers Microsoft Copilot, AI-generated summaries in Bing and select partner AI experiences. Bing does not say which partners.

What it counts is how often a page from your site was displayed as a source inside an AI answer. That is a different thing from the impressions, clicks and positions in the normal search report.

Based on Bing Webmaster Blog (2026-02-10).
MetricMeaningCaution
Total CitationsTotal number of times your pages were shown as sources in AI answers in the selected periodDoes not show where in the answer you appeared
Average Cited PagesNumber of distinct pages from your site shown as sources per day, averaged over the periodNot a measure of page importance or ranking
Grounding queriesShort phrases the AI used when retrieving the content it citedNot the user's original prompt; a sample of overall citation activity
Page-level citationsCitations per URLA count, not a page score or rank
Visibility trendsCitations over time, by daySudden jumps can come from data backfill or changes on the AI side (see below)

The report has no click, traffic or revenue data (Search Engine Land). Look at visits in your analytics separately.

What was added in 2026: page mapping, intents, topics, citation share, compare

Since launch, Bing has added the following, one after another. All are preview or beta, so labels and counting can change.

Bing says Citation Share does not expose competitor domains, is not traffic share and is not a content quality score. For Topics Portfolio, Bing itself says to treat the numbers as directional signals for planning, not a precise ledger. Neither is a ranking table.

Topics with too little site presence or activity are not shown in Topics Portfolio, and Bing notes that branded queries (your own business name) can show up as opportunities (Bing Webmaster Blog).

Getting started: adding the site and opening the report

  1. Sign in to Bing Webmaster Tools with a Microsoft account, add your site and verify ownership. If the site is already in Google Search Console, you can import it from there (Practical Ecommerce describes choosing "Import your sites from GSC" and waiting about 24 hours for data: Practical Ecommerce).
  2. Open "AI Performance" from the left menu. Choose a period and switch between the "Citations & Pages" and Topics Portfolio tabs (tab names may change over time).
  3. Click a phrase in the grounding queries table to see the pages cited for it. Click a page URL to see the phrases it was cited for.
  4. The tables can be exported to a spreadsheet (Practical Ecommerce). Preview features can change, so export on the same day each month to keep comparable records.

If there is no data or very little, your pages may simply not be cited in Microsoft's AI yet, or Bing may not have indexed them. Run the checks in the WordPress section below, then watch over months rather than days.

When the numbers jump or drop

From around June 1, 2026, many accounts reported large jumps in citations. PinMeTo reports that a Microsoft product manager replied on X that this was a data backfill, not more citations (PinMeTo, secondhand). Note such changes in your records as a data change.

Bing says citation patterns shift with user behavior, evolving models, freshness and partner refresh cycles (Bing Search Blog). Do not judge from one month's change. Use Compare to line up periods of the same length and read the trend over several months.

Turning what you see into page fixes

Reports tend to end at "we looked at it". Decide in advance what you will do for each finding, as in this table.

What you seeLikely reasonWhat to do
Pricing, services or directions pages are never citedThe page is not indexed, or the answer is not written as textCheck the page is indexed in Bing. Write prices, hours and location in body text and a table. Keep structured data values identical
Only old blog posts are citedOld posts answer the question more directly than the current official pageAnswer the same question on the official page, and update old posts or link them to it
For your own business name, other sites take most citations (low citation share)Comparison or review sites are used firstStrengthen your company and location pages, and bring outside listings up to date
You are missing for area + service phrases (e.g. "dentist open Saturday Shibuya")Area or conditions are not written on your pagesState service area, opening days and weekend availability in plain words. Check Bing Places too
Several similar pages of yours are cited for one phrasePages overlapPick one main page and link the others to it, or separate their content

None of these is a special trick for AI. The core is writing the facts customers need, accurately and clearly. Record the date of each fix and compare before and after one to three months later. You cannot prove the fix caused a change, so record it as "after this fix, this changed".

Setting up in WordPress

  1. Let Bing read the site: in WordPress, go to Settings > Reading and make sure "Search engine visibility" is unchecked. When it is checked, WordPress asks search engines not to index the site.
  2. Check that robots.txt does not block Bingbot: open /robots.txt in a browser and look for Disallow: / under Bingbot or under all agents (User-agent: *). Bing says it respects robots.txt, so a blocked site will not be cited either.
  3. Verify ownership: many SEO plugins have a field for webmaster tools verification codes (meta tags). If yours does not, use another method Bing offers (file upload or DNS) or import from Search Console.
  4. Submit the sitemap: add the sitemap URL your SEO plugin outputs (for example /sitemap.xml or /sitemap_index.xml) in Bing Webmaster Tools.
  5. Tell Bing about updates quickly: Bing recommended IndexNow in its launch post (Bing Webmaster Blog). If your SEO plugin or another plugin offers IndexNow, turn it on.
  6. For local businesses, Bing Places too: Bing also recommends Bing Places for business listings. Keep address, hours and phone number identical to your site.

FAQ

Where do I find AI Performance?

Add your site to Bing Webmaster Tools, verify ownership, then open "AI Performance" from the menu. It started as a public preview on February 10, 2026, and later features were added as previews or beta.

Does it show citations in ChatGPT or Perplexity?

No. It covers Microsoft Copilot, Bing's AI-generated summaries and select partner AI experiences. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews are not included, so check them separately, for example with Search Console and a fixed set of questions asked regularly.

Are grounding queries what users typed?

No. They are short phrases the AI used to retrieve the content it cited, not the user's original prompt. Bing says the data shown is a sample of overall citation activity.

Can I see competitors in Citation Share?

No. Citation Share is your share of all citations shown across all sites for one phrase. Bing says it does not expose competitor domains and is not traffic share or a quality score.

Can I see clicks or visits from AI?

AI Performance has no click or traffic data. Look at visits from AI in your analytics referrers, such as copilot.microsoft.com.

Why did citations jump in June 2026?

For the jumps seen from around June 1, 2026, a Microsoft product manager reportedly said it was a data backfill (secondhand report). Note it as a data change and compare periods of the same length.

Is there a setting that increases citations?

What Bing points to is letting Bing read your site (robots.txt and other controls), telling it about updates with IndexNow, and keeping Bing Places listings accurate for local businesses. Nothing guarantees citations. The core is writing the facts customers need, accurately and clearly.
