Add local business schema to Yoast SEO, Rank Math or AIOSEO, free

Keep your SEO plugin. Nurevo AEO, a free WordPress plugin, adds the facts AI needs to describe a local business (address, telephone, opening hours, business type, services and FAQ) into the structured data Yoast SEO, Rank Math or All in One SEO already publishes, without duplicating it.

Last checked: October 2026. SEO plugin plans change, so check each plugin's own pages for its current features.

What the free tiers of Yoast SEO, Rank Math and AIOSEO publish

When an AI answers a question like "which dentist near me is open on Saturday", it needs facts it can read without guessing: what kind of business you are, where you are, how to call you and when you are open. On WordPress, those facts usually come from your SEO plugin's JSON-LD.

The free tiers publish your site as an Organization with a name, a URL and a logo. Most of the local facts sit in paid local SEO add-ons or plans. As of October 2026:

How Nurevo AEO fills the gaps without duplicate schema

Nurevo AEO does not print a second set of structured data beside your SEO plugin's. It hooks the filter each plugin uses to build its JSON-LD graph (wpseo_schema_graph in Yoast SEO, rank_math/schema/validated_data in Rank Math, aioseo_schema_output in AIOSEO) and adds only what is missing:

  1. Address, telephone, opening hours, description and email go onto your existing business node, under the same @id.
  2. A value your SEO plugin already sets is never overwritten. If you filled in Rank Math's Local SEO settings, those values stay.
  3. Services you enter are added and linked to your business.
  4. FAQ questions you enter are added to the page's FAQPage only on the page that shows the Nurevo FAQ block or the [nurevo_faq] shortcode, skipping questions it already answers.
  5. WebSite, WebPage, Article and breadcrumbs stay exactly as your SEO plugin publishes them.

The page ends up with one graph describing one business, with no duplicates. With any other setup (SEOPress, The SEO Framework, a theme's own schema, or no SEO plugin), Nurevo AEO prints its own graph and puts the business facts under the existing business @id, so the two still describe one business.

Set it up in five steps

  1. Install Nurevo AEO from WordPress.org (Plugins > Add New, search for "Nurevo AEO") and activate it. Keep your SEO plugin active.
  2. The setup wizard opens once. Choose the kind of site (local business, online shop or B2B), confirm the name, address, telephone, opening hours and description, and choose what to publish for AI.
  3. Check the facts the plugin filled in from your existing settings. It only fills blank fields, so nothing you typed is replaced, and the wizard reads your opening hours back to you as they will be published.
  4. Enter your services and questions under Nurevo AEO > Settings, and place the Nurevo FAQ block (or [nurevo_faq]) on the page where the FAQ should appear.
  5. Open Nurevo AEO > AEO Score, read what the diagnosis sends, tick the consent box and press Run diagnosis. Then apply the one-click fixes the checklist offers.

To see what AI reads from the outside, run the free AI readability check on your home page. More on the WordPress side: AEO for WordPress.

What structured data can and cannot do

Structured data helps AI read your facts correctly: the right hours, the right phone number, the right kind of business. It does not decide what an AI says. No tool can guarantee that an AI will mention you, and Nurevo does not promise that.

FAQ

Does Nurevo AEO duplicate my SEO plugin's schema?

No. With Yoast SEO, Rank Math or All in One SEO it adds only the missing facts into the graph your SEO plugin already publishes, under the same business @id, and never overwrites a value the plugin sets. The page keeps one graph describing one business.

Do I need to remove Yoast SEO, Rank Math or AIOSEO?

No. Keep your SEO plugin for search ranking. Nurevo AEO adds the facts AI needs on top of it.

Is Nurevo AEO free?

Yes. The business facts, services, FAQ, AEO score, one-click fixes, JSON-LD, llms.txt and AI crawler rules are all on the free plan. The paid plans only add an always-current ruleset and AI visibility measurement (beta).
