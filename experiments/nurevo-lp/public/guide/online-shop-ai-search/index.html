AI search for online shops: how to get AI to read your products, prices, stock and return terms correctly

AI search optimization (AEO) for an online shop comes down to publishing your product names, prices, stock, shipping costs, return terms and who runs the shop accurately and consistently, both in your product page text and in schema.org Product, Offer and OnlineStore structured data. That gives ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI what they need to answer "where can I buy X online?" or "which shop sells X with free returns?" without guessing. It is not about adding special tags for AI.

Written November 2, 2026 (JST). Information from Google, OpenAI and Japan's Consumer Affairs Agency was checked on each official page on that date.

AI answers change from day to day and with how a question is phrased. The steps here make your shop's and products' facts readable to AI. They do not guarantee that an AI will mention your shop or rank it anywhere.

What shoppers ask AI, and what AI reads

Shopping questions put to AI mix product, price, stock, shipping and returns: "where can I buy a sunscreen for sensitive skin online?", "a shop that sells wooden cutting boards made in Japan", "is X in stock, and how much is shipping?", "an online shoe store with easy returns". To answer them, an AI has to be able to read, as text, which products you sell at what price, whether they can be bought now, what shipping and returns cost, and who is selling.

Google says there are no additional requirements and no special schema.org structured data needed to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, and that the usual basics still matter, such as making structured data match the visible text and keeping your Merchant Center and Business Profile information up to date. Google: AI features and your website

For ChatGPT, OpenAI says product results in search are selected independently by ChatGPT and are not ads; that it considers structured metadata such as price and product description from first- and third-party providers; and that merchants are ranked on factors such as availability, price, quality and whether they are the maker or primary seller. Shopify merchants' product data is already integrated through Shopify Catalog, and other merchants can apply to provide a product feed directly (checked November 2, 2026). The facts an online shop should publish

Google's organization documentation has no required properties, asks you to use the most specific subtype, and recommends OnlineStore rather than OnlineBusiness for an e-commerce site. Google: Organization structured data. For products, Google's merchant listing structured data requires name, image and offers on the Product, and price (greater than zero) and priceCurrency on the Offer; availability is recommended. Merchant listing markup is for pages where a shopper can buy the product, and Google recommends adding it to product pages rather than pages that list products. Returns and shipping are best described once, as shop-wide policies on your Organization, rather than on every product. Google: Return policy / Google: Shipping policy

In Google, return and shipping settings in Merchant Center or Search Console take precedence over the structured data on your site. Changing a value in only one place leaves them in conflict, so when a price, shipping cost or return term changes, update your product pages, structured data, Merchant Center, marketplace store pages and llms.txt together.

Facts the law may already require on your pages

Rules for online selling differ by country, so check the ones that apply to you. In Japan, for example, the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions requires mail-order advertising to show the price including consumption tax, shipping costs, any other costs the buyer pays, when and how to pay, when the goods are delivered, terms for cancellation and returns, and the seller's name, address and phone number. Consumer Affairs Agency: mail-order advertising (Japanese). These are exactly the facts an AI needs to answer "how much is shipping?" or "can I return it?".

Under the same law, if no return terms are displayed, the buyer may cancel within eight days of receiving the goods and pays the cost of returning them, the Consumer Affairs Agency explains. Consumer Affairs Agency: mail-order return case (Japanese). Whatever your policy is, state it plainly in text and keep the returns page you link from structured data saying the same.

Show ratings and reviews only from real customers. In Japan, stealth marketing has been a violation of the Act against Unjustifiable Premiums and Misleading Representations since October 1, 2023; posts a business asks or instructs third parties to make count as its advertising, review posts included. Consumer Affairs Agency: stealth marketing (Japanese)

Setting it up on a WordPress (WooCommerce) shop

  1. Install Nurevo AEO (free) from WordPress.org and activate it. Keep Yoast SEO, Rank Math, All in One SEO or Slim SEO if you use one.
  2. Under Settings (or in the setup wizard), set the site type to "Online shop". It is suggested when WooCommerce is active, but nothing changes until you save. The shop is published as an OnlineStore, and no address or opening hours are needed (an address or phone you entered is still published).
  3. Enter the URLs of your return and refund policy page and your shipping policy page. The returns page is published as the shop's MerchantReturnPolicy (a link to that page), and both pages are listed in llms.txt. Shipping is not turned into structured data, because its conditions (costs and times) are not known to the plugin; the page is only listed in llms.txt.
  4. Product pages keep the Product markup WooCommerce prints; Nurevo AEO does not repeat it. On the front page it publishes up to the 30 most recently published products as a catalogue (OfferCatalog), with price, currency and stock where known. A rating is added only for products with real reviews. Products are also listed in llms.txt.
  5. Enter your FAQ (shipping costs, delivery times, gift wrapping, how to return) and place the Nurevo FAQ block or the [nurevo_faq] shortcode on the page where you want it shown.
  6. Run the diagnosis on the AEO Score screen. For an online shop it checks for products with price and stock, a returns page, a shipping page and contact details, and lists what is missing.
  7. See what AI reads from the outside with the free AI readability check, and confirm that prices, shipping and return terms match Merchant Center and your marketplace store pages.

On shops not built with WordPress, such as Shopify, the same idea applies: publish the facts in the table above in your product page text and in structured data, and list your shop overview, returns, shipping and main product pages in llms.txt. What is llms.txt?

Checklist before you publish

  1. Each product page shows the product name, price and stock status as text, not only in an image.
  2. Prices and stock in the structured data match the values visible on the page, and sold-out products are not marked as in stock.
  3. Shipping costs, delivery times and return terms have their own pages, linked from product pages or the footer.
  4. The seller's name, address, phone number and other legally required details are published.
  5. The shop's structured data type fits the business, such as OnlineStore.
  6. Rating and review markup comes only from real customer reviews.
  7. Prices, shipping and return terms match across your site, Merchant Center, marketplaces and social shop pages.

Checking whether you appear in AI answers

Once the facts are in place, ask AI the questions your customers would ask ("best online shop for X", "where to buy X with free shipping") and see whether your shop or products come up by name or URL. Answers change between runs, so ask the same questions a few times, spread over time.

FAQ

Where should an online shop start with AI search?

Write each product's name, price and stock status correctly as text on its product page, and put shipping, returns and seller details on their own pages. Then publish the same facts as Product, Offer and OnlineStore structured data and in llms.txt, and make sure they match Merchant Center and your marketplace listings.

Do I have to pay to get my products into ChatGPT?

OpenAI says product results in ChatGPT search are selected independently and are not ads. Shopify merchants are already integrated through Shopify Catalog, and other merchants can apply to provide a product feed directly. In either case, appearing is not guaranteed.

If I sell on marketplaces such as Amazon, do I still need to fix my own site?

Marketplace pages can be used as sources by AI, but your own site is where you can publish product descriptions, return terms and FAQ in the most detail and most accurately. Keeping prices, shipping and return terms in agreement in both places is the safest approach.

Is Nurevo AEO free?

Yes. The online shop type (OnlineStore), product catalogue, return policy and FAQ structured data, llms.txt, AI crawler rules and the AEO score are on the free plan. Will this guarantee my products appear in AI answers?

No. AI answers change, and no tool or technique can guarantee a mention or a position. What you can do is make your products' and shop's facts easy for AI to read and keep checking whether you appear.
