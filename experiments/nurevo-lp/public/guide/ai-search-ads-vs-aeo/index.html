ChatGPT ads, AI Overviews ads and AEO: what agencies should explain to clients and set up on the site

A ChatGPT ad buys a labeled slot below the answer. It does not buy the answer or its sources: OpenAI states that ads do not influence the answers ChatGPT gives. AI search optimization (AEO) is the work of getting a business named or cited inside the answer itself. They are separate jobs, so agencies should quote, prepare, measure and report them separately.

Written November 22, 2026 (JST). This is for ad agencies running search and social campaigns, and for web agencies and SEO firms that offer AI search optimization. The goal is an explanation you can use as-is when a client asks "If we can advertise in ChatGPT, do we still need AEO?" or "Will ads make the AI recommend us?"

AI answers and ad delivery both vary by day, wording, the user's plan and country. This is not a way to guarantee placement. It is a way to explain the difference accurately and keep the preparation and measurement apart. Availability below was checked against official pages on November 22, 2026 and will change.

OpenAI writes that "Ads do not influence the answers ChatGPT gives you" and that conversations are kept private from advertisers (OpenAI). So you cannot tell a client that more ad spend will earn more recommendations or citations inside the answer. We also found no public, independent test of ad spend versus citations, so the accurate wording is "that is how OpenAI describes it."

Three client questions and how to answer them

  1. "If we advertise in ChatGPT, will it recommend us?" No. The ad appears in a separate, labeled slot below the answer. OpenAI says whether the answer recommends you is decided apart from ads.
  2. "If we can run ads, do we still need AEO?" They do different jobs. Ads reach only Free and Go users, and only while the campaign runs. A name or citation in the answer also reaches paid-plan users and AI tools without ads (such as Perplexity), and does not disappear the day you stop paying.
  3. "Which should we start with?" First check that the site's facts (name, address, prices, hours, services) are accurate and consistent, because both the ad landing page and the AI answer read the same site. Then use ads for products you need leads for now, and AEO for the facts you want the AI to get right when someone asks about you.

The answer changes by industry. In healthcare (dentists, clinics), finance and alcohol, ads on AI answer screens are restricted or excluded, so whether the business appears in the answer depends on AEO alone. Saying so when you pitch ads avoids misunderstandings later.

Site setup: OAI-AdsBot and OAI-SearchBot are different bots

OpenAI splits its crawlers by purpose (OpenAI bots documentation). OAI-SearchBot surfaces sites in ChatGPT's search answers; sites that opt out of it are not shown in ChatGPT search answers. OAI-AdsBot only visits pages submitted as ads, checks them against policy and helps decide when the ad is relevant. OpenAI says the data OAI-AdsBot collects is not used to train its foundation models.

OpenAI's advertiser guidance says you must allow OAI-AdsBot and recommends allowing OAI-SearchBot too. It also warns that a robots.txt rule alone may not be enough: firewall, CDN and bot-protection rules can block it as well (OpenAI Help). Fixing access does not guarantee the ad will be approved.

# Does robots.txt block OAI-AdsBot or OAI-SearchBot?
curl -s https://example.com/robots.txt | grep -i -A3 "oai-\|user-agent: \*"

# Does bot protection turn the landing page away (403 or a challenge page)?
curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}\n" \
  -A "Mozilla/5.0 AppleWebKit/537.36 (KHTML, like Gecko); compatible; OAI-AdsBot/1.0; +https://openai.com/adsbot" \
  https://example.com/lp/

These commands are examples: replace example.com and /lp/ with the client's domain and landing page. Bot-protection services often check IP addresses as well as the user agent, so a 200 here does not prove the real bot gets through. Check the bot-protection logs too.

WordPress settings with Nurevo AEO

Nurevo AEO (a free plugin on WordPress.org) allows the AI search crawlers in robots.txt: OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, PerplexityBot and Perplexity-User. The client's existing rules, such as /wp-admin/, keep applying to them. Training crawlers (GPTBot and others) are left to the client's robots.txt, with a one-click allow or block. Blocking training crawlers does not affect AI search or the AEO score.

OAI-AdsBot is not in the default list: it only reads pages submitted as ads, so it is irrelevant to sites that don't advertise. On a site that runs ChatGPT ads, you can add it with a filter if you want it named in robots.txt.

// Example for a child theme's functions.php or an mu-plugin
// (only on sites that run ChatGPT ads)
add_filter('webmcp_canary_robots_search_agents', function ($agents) {
    $agents[] = 'OAI-AdsBot';
    return $agents;
});

Afterwards, open https://your-domain/robots.txt and check that OAI-AdsBot is in the allow group. If the site has a physical robots.txt file on the server, WordPress's generated robots.txt is not used, so edit that file directly.

OpenAI also uses landing page content to decide when an ad is relevant. Check that the prices and terms on the landing page match the site's structured data and pricing page. The AI answer and the ad read the same site, so consistent facts help both.

Measure them separately: don't mix ad numbers with answer appearances

  1. Measure ad results in the ads manager and with tags and URL parameters on the landing page. Keep them out of the AEO report.
  2. Measure answer appearances by asking fixed questions on fixed dates and recording whether the name or URL appears in the answer text or sources. The steps in measuring AI visibility work for this.
  3. If you check on ChatGPT's Free plan, an ad may appear below the answer. Do not count an ad as an appearance; note "ad shown" separately.
  4. Google says ads within AI Overviews are reported as Top ads, and advertisers cannot target the AI Overviews placement on its own (Google Ads Help). Don't report an "AI Overviews ads" result as if it were a separate number.
  5. Perplexity is reported to have stepped away from ads, so appearing there has nothing to do with ad spend. Nurevo's Pro AI visibility measurement currently covers Perplexity (ChatGPT once configured), so you can treat it as a number unaffected by ads.

Splitting quotes and reports

When you handle both, split the quote into media spend (paid to the platform), ad management fees and AEO work. Split the reports too: don't put ad impressions and clicks on the same chart as AI answer appearances. Scoping AEO as an ongoing service is covered in offering AEO as a monthly service.

In proposals and contracts, don't write "ads will get you recommended by AI" or "you'll rank at the top of AI answers." OpenAI itself says ads don't influence answers, and AI answers change. What you can write is fact: ads appear in an ad slot below the answer, and AEO makes the site's facts accurate and readable by AI.

FAQ

Will ChatGPT ads make the answer recommend us more often?

OpenAI says ads do not influence ChatGPT's answers. The ad is shown separately below the answer and labeled, apart from whether the answer recommends you. We found no public, independent test of ad spend versus answers.

Can we run ChatGPT ads in Japan?

Yes. OpenAI's August 11, 2026 update says ChatGPT ads launched in Japan. They show to logged-in adult users on Free and Go, not on Plus, Pro, Business or Enterprise.

Can we run ads inside Google AI Overviews?

As of November 22, 2026, Google Ads Help lists ads within AI Overviews in English in 12 countries, including the US, Canada, Australia and India. Japan is not listed. Regular search ads above and below AI Overviews are eligible in all 200+ AI Overviews markets.

Can a dental practice or clinic advertise in ChatGPT?

OpenAI says ads are not eligible near sensitive topics such as health, mental health or politics. Expect ads to be hard to show next to medical answers, and start with AEO that publishes accurate services and hours.

If we allow OAI-AdsBot, is our content used for training?

According to OpenAI, OAI-AdsBot only visits pages submitted as ads and its data is not used to train foundation models. Training is GPTBot, which you can handle separately.

How should ads be handled when measuring AI visibility?

Don't count an ad as an appearance; record "ad shown" separately. Measure ad results in the ads manager and keep them out of the AEO report.

With a small budget, ads or AEO?

Start by making the site's facts accurate and consistent: both the ad landing page and the AI answer read the same site. Then use ads for products that need leads now, and AEO for facts you want the AI to get right. AI answers change either way, so record results and decide from them.
