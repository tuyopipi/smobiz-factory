Ending or handing over AI search work: what agencies give the client and what they switch off

When an AI search optimization contract ends, work in this order: agree with the client whether the AI-facing output (structured data, llms.txt, robots.txt rules) is kept or stopped; hand over the settings, per-page settings, your own code and the measurement records; remove your accounts, reports and billing links; and finally check the output on the live site. Plenty of guides cover domains and hosting, but AI-facing output lives inside plugins and code, and removing it the wrong way silently drops the business address and opening hours from the structured data.

Written November 16, 2026 (JST). This is for web agencies, SEO firms, local SEO firms and WordPress care companies that have run AI search optimization (also called AEO, GEO or LLMO) on a client's site. Starting is covered in the first audit and proposal and running it in offering AEO as a monthly service. This guide is about ending it.

AI answers change from day to day and with the wording of the question. These steps are there so that facts do not disappear or conflict during a handover. They do not guarantee that the business keeps appearing in AI answers afterwards.

What the popular handover guides leave out

We read twelve of the Japanese and English articles that rank for queries like "switch web agency" and "client offboarding checklist". They cover domain registration, the hosting contract, WordPress admin accounts, copyright in the code and the handover email well. Almost none of them cover the AI search items below.

Google's "Do you need an SEO?" page suggests that site owners ask an SEO whether they will share all the changes they make to the site (Google Search Central). The handover document is where you keep that promise one last time.

Decide first: keep, stop or move the AI-facing output

When the client gives notice, agree on one of these three. Removing your accounts or code before deciding turns the answer into "stop" by accident.

ChoiceWhen it fitsWhat to do
Keep and hand to the clientThe client (or the next agency) carries on with the same setupLeave the plugin in place. Hand over admin, settings and records, and remove only your own involvement
StopThe client does not want AI-facing output, or the site is closing or being rebuiltSwitch the output off, then remove the plugin or code. Export the settings and hand them over first
Move to another setupThe next agency outputs it with another plugin or its own codeHand over the list of current output and set a switch-over date. Turn the old output off on the same day so there is no gap and no duplicate

Whichever you choose, you first need a list of what is output, from where, on which pages. The table in the next section is page one of the handover document.

Your own code is the easiest item to miss. If your agency keeps code that changes the structured data or llms.txt in a small plugin of its own or in mu-plugins, removing that plugin removes the changes too. If the client is keeping them, hand over the files or move the code into the client's child theme before you remove it.

What disappears when it is removed: the Nurevo AEO case

Here is how our own product (the free plugin on WordPress.org) actually behaves. Check other plugins along the same lines.

  1. The free output (structured data, llms.txt, AI search crawler access) is built by the plugin on the client's own server. It works without a connection to the Nurevo service or a key, so disconnecting the site from your agency's account does not stop it.
  2. Alongside Yoast SEO, Rank Math, All in One SEO or Slim SEO, Nurevo AEO adds the missing address, phone, opening hours, business type, FAQ and services inside their structured data. Deactivate the plugin and the SEO plugin's structured data remains, but those added properties are gone.
  3. The robots.txt rules are added to the robots.txt WordPress generates. Deactivate the plugin and those lines go. On sites with a physical robots.txt file on the server, that file is what gets served.
  4. /llms.txt is served by the plugin, so it is no longer served once the plugin is off.
  5. Deleting (uninstalling) the plugin removes everything it stored: settings, FAQ, services, cached diagnosis data and per-page settings, on every site of a multisite network. Do the export in the next section before deleting.
  6. When a site leaves Standard, the plugin keeps running on the ruleset it shipped with; existing output does not stop or degrade. When it leaves Pro, the AI visibility measurement screen (beta, currently Perplexity) is no longer available. Paid plans can be cancelled at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period and that period's fee is not refunded (Terms of Service, article 5).

Hand over: settings, per-page settings and records

In Nurevo AEO, Settings > Tools in the Nurevo admin menu, or WP-CLI, exports the settings as a JSON file: the output switches, the business details (name, description, address, phone, hours, type, URL, email), the site type and policy page URLs, the FAQ and the services. Keys, tokens, the plan and the connection are never included, and a file containing them is refused on import.

Per-page settings (pages left out of llms.txt or structured data, page-specific FAQs) are not in that file. List them with WP-CLI and paste the list into the handover document.

# 1. Settings, FAQ and services (no keys, tokens or plan)
wp nurevo export --file=nurevo-handover.json

# 2. Per-page settings are not in that file: list them
wp post list --post_type=any --meta_key=_nurevo_exclude_llms --fields=ID,post_title,post_type
wp post list --post_type=any --meta_key=_nurevo_exclude_schema --fields=ID,post_title,post_type
wp post list --post_type=any --meta_key=_nurevo_faq --fields=ID,post_title,post_type

# 3. What the site publishes right now (schema / llms.txt / AI crawler rules)
wp nurevo status

Hand over the measurement records too: the questions asked each month, the date, AI and number of runs, and whether the business was named or the site cited. Branded monthly reports can be printed to PDF, so give the client the whole contract period in one set, and the next team can carry on under the same conditions. How to measure is covered in measuring AI visibility.

The exported file contains the client's business details, such as address, phone and email. Don't stop at attaching it to an email: confirm the client has received it, and delete your own copy as your contract says.

How to verify after the handover

Finally, check the output on the live site and record the result on the last page of the handover document. Write the date of the work and the date of the check separately.

  1. Run the home page, the company page and the FAQ page through the Rich Results Test. Check that the business is described once, and that address, phone and hours are still there (or gone, if you chose to stop).
  2. Open /llms.txt and /robots.txt in a browser and confirm they match what was agreed.
  3. If the plugin stays, run wp nurevo status and confirm the structured data, llms.txt and AI crawler output are as agreed.
  4. If the output moved to another setup, check that the same business is not output in two sets of structured data.
  5. Ask an AI a few questions that include the business name and record whether address, hours and the like now conflict with what they were before (answers vary, so hand the results over as a record).

For the next contract: what to put in the first agreement

  1. The plugins and code used for AI-facing output, and their settings, belong to the client and are exported and handed over at the end.
  2. Top-level permission in WordPress, Search Console, Google Business Profile and similar stays with the client's accounts; the agency is added as an extra user.
  3. Whether training crawlers are allowed or blocked in robots.txt is the client's decision.
  4. Monthly measurement records are handed over in one set at the end.
  5. The date on which agency accounts, automatic reports and widgets are removed after the contract ends.

Handover checklist

  1. Keep, stop or move was agreed with the client and recorded.
  2. There is a list of the AI-facing output: what, from where, on which pages.
  3. The settings are exported, with the list of per-page settings attached.
  4. Your own code was handed over as files or moved to the client's side.
  5. Measurement records and monthly report PDFs were handed over.
  6. You confirmed the client holds top-level permission before removing your accounts.
  7. Automatic monthly reports and your widget on the client's site are switched off.
  8. The client decided whether to keep or cancel the paid plan.
  9. Structured data, llms.txt and robots.txt were checked on the live site and the results recorded.
  10. Your own copy of the exported file was deleted as the contract says.

FAQ

Does the client's AI search optimization stop when the contract ends?

That depends on the setup. Nurevo AEO's free output is built inside the client's site, so removing your connection or the Nurevo account does not stop it. It stops when the plugin is deactivated or deleted. With other setups, check whether anything depends on the agency's servers or accounts.

Can we just delete the plugin?

We advise against it. The address, hours and other properties it added to the SEO plugin's structured data disappear, and the settings and per-page settings are deleted. Decide keep or stop first, and export the settings before removing anything.

What if the next agency uses a different plugin?

Agree a switch-over date and turn the old output off on the day the new setup starts. If both run at once, the same business can appear in two sets of structured data. Check with the Rich Results Test after the switch.

Can the exported settings file be imported into another site?

Yes. Anything not in the file keeps the destination site's current value, and keys, tokens and the plan are not included, so the connection does not carry over. Check the site-specific items (name, address and so on) before importing.

Should the training crawler setting in robots.txt be put back?

There is no rule. Write down the current state, and when and by whom it was decided, so the client can decide. In Nurevo AEO, blocking training crawlers does not affect AI search or the AEO score.

If AI visibility drops after the handover, is the agency responsible?

AI answers change regardless of a handover, so a drop alone does not identify the cause. If the handover document records the output list and the verification results, you can tell whether facts went missing or the AI answers simply changed.

What happens to the referral commission if a referred client cancels?

The commission is based on payments made while the referred client uses a paid plan. For the details, follow what you were told when your partner application was approved, and ask Nurevo if anything is unclear.
