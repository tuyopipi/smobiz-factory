How the Gemini app and Claude pick up your website as a source, and how to check

The Gemini app can ground its answers in pages Google has crawled, and whether yours may be used that way is controlled by the Google-Extended token in robots.txt. Claude reads sites with Claude-SearchBot (search indexing) and Claude-User (fetching pages for a user's question), which you can allow or block separately from ClaudeBot, its training crawler. What you can do on your side: set these four names to match your goals, put answers and facts in the page text, and record whether you appear as a source for unbranded questions. Neither Google nor Anthropic publishes how sources are chosen.

Written November 19, 2026 (JST). We covered Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode in getting cited in AI Overviews, Perplexity in how Perplexity cites websites and ChatGPT in how to appear in ChatGPT. This guide covers the remaining two, the Gemini app and Claude: what their official documentation says and what to do on your site.

AI answers vary by day, wording and plan. These steps make sure Gemini and Claude can read your site and that you record citations correctly; they do not guarantee that you will appear in answers or rank anywhere.

Separate them first: the Gemini app, Google Search AI features and Claude

Some top articles say the Gemini app and AI Overviews are "basically the same". In terms of site settings they are not. AI Overviews and AI Mode are part of Google Search: if Googlebot can read a page and show it in results, it is eligible. Whether pages Google has crawled may be used to ground answers in the Gemini app (gemini.google.com or the mobile app) is managed by a separate name, Google-Extended.

robots.txt by goal: appear in AI search, opt out of training

The most common goal is "appear in AI search and answers, but don't train on my content". For Claude the names are split, so you can allow Claude-SearchBot and Claude-User and block only ClaudeBot. When you create a group for a named crawler, repeat your site-wide rules (for example, keeping /wp-admin/ out) inside it, because a crawler with its own group reads only that group.

# Example: let Claude search and answer from your pages, block only ClaudeBot (training)
User-agent: Claude-SearchBot
Allow: /
Disallow: /wp-admin/

User-agent: Claude-User
Allow: /
Disallow: /wp-admin/

User-agent: ClaudeBot
Disallow: /

# No Google-Extended group (= not blocked).
# Disallowing it stops use for Gemini training AND for grounding
# in Gemini Apps, according to Google.

Gemini is different. Google-Extended covers both training and grounding in the Gemini app, so, as far as Google's documentation goes, you cannot opt out of training while staying available as a source in the Gemini app. If you want to be a source in the Gemini app, leave Google-Extended unblocked. Google also says it intends to support the shared AIPREF training preference signal (user-agent: __usage_ai-training) in early 2027.

Anthropic says that as of October 30, 2026 it has begun recognizing a machine-readable no-AI-training signal based on an IETF aipref draft, but a robots.txt rule that names ClaudeBot takes precedence. State your intent clearly in one place so the two don't conflict.

What you can do on the page

Being readable is not enough if the page doesn't answer the question. Neither Gemini nor Claude publishes its criteria, so these are general practices for any AI. See writing articles AI can cite and the llms.txt guide for detail.

  1. Answer the question in the first one or two sentences under each heading (prices, hours, service area, who it is for)
  2. Date your numbers and cite other people's numbers
  3. Keep name, address and phone the same on your site and outside listings such as Google Business Profile
  4. Put facts that only appear in images or PDFs into the page text as well
  5. Keep structured data identical to the visible text

Choosing block also blocks Google-Extended, which according to Google means your pages are no longer used for grounding in the Gemini app. If you want to be a Gemini app source, think before blocking all training crawlers. # 1) What robots.txt says for Claude's three bots and Google-Extended
curl -s https://example.com/robots.txt | grep -i -E -A3 "claude|google-extended"

# 2) Does a request with the Claude-SearchBot user agent get a 200? (403, 503 or a challenge page is a problem)

How to check whether you appear as a source

In the Gemini app, a Sources button appears below the answer or inline when sources exist, but Google says "not all responses include related links or sources" (Gemini Apps Help). No button means that answer gave no links. Google also says Gemini can present inaccurate information as fact (about Gemini responses).

In Claude, answers that use web search include citations (Claude Help; in the API, citations are always enabled for web search). In the new Claude experience there is no web search toggle; Claude searches when it helps. An answer without search has no citations, so "no citation" does not mean your site wasn't read.

Checklist

  1. You treat the Gemini app and Google Search AI features separately
  2. You decided on Google-Extended knowing its effect on the Gemini app
  3. robots.txt doesn't block Claude-SearchBot or Claude-User
  4. You decided on ClaudeBot (training) separately from the two search crawlers
  5. Named groups repeat rules such as /wp-admin/
  6. Your WAF or security plugin doesn't return 403 to Claude's crawlers
  7. Answers and facts are in the page text
  8. You have fixed unbranded questions and record them under the same conditions

FAQ

If I block Google-Extended, will I disappear from Google Search or AI Overviews?

Google says Google-Extended does not affect inclusion or ranking in Google Search. It affects Gemini model training and grounding in the Gemini app and Vertex AI.

Can I opt out of Gemini training but still be a source in the Gemini app?

According to Google's documentation, Google-Extended covers training and grounding together, so there is no setting that separates them. Google plans to support the shared AIPREF training signal in early 2027; how that changes things has yet to be published.

If I block ClaudeBot, will I drop out of Claude's answers?

Anthropic says ClaudeBot is for training, while search and answers use Claude-SearchBot and Claude-User. If those two are allowed, blocking ClaudeBot does not stop reading for search and answers.

Which search engine does Claude use?

Anthropic's help page says image search is powered by Bing but does not name the web search provider. Brave Search was reported to be on Anthropic's subprocessor list in March 2025, but this has not been published as how the system works.

Google-Extended never shows up in my access logs. Is my setting broken?

Google-Extended has no user agent of its own; existing Google crawlers fetch pages, so it never appears in logs. Check the setting by reading your robots.txt.

Do robots.txt changes show up in Gemini or Claude answers immediately?

Neither publishes how long changes take. Note the date of the change and check a few days later under the same conditions. Whether you are cited is decided by the AI, separately from your settings.

Gemini or Claude says something wrong about us. What can we do?

If sources are shown, trace them. Fix outdated text on your site and ask other publishers to update theirs. You cannot edit AI answers directly, and there is no set time for corrections to show.
