AI search for plumbers, locksmiths and other call-out repair businesses: getting AI to read your fees, estimates and licence numbers correctly

For call-out repair businesses such as plumbers, locksmiths and pest control, AI search optimization is not about advertising the lowest "from" price. It is about answering, in the page text and with conditions, whether there is a call-out fee or an estimate fee, what cancelling costs, whether you give a written total including tax before starting work, and which official designation you hold (number and expiry). In Japan, consumer agencies and local governments keep warning about surprise bills in this trade, and AI reads those warnings too. A page that shows with facts that the warnings do not apply to you is easier for both AI and people to choose.

Written 21 November 2026 (JST). The pages of Japan's National Consumer Affairs Center, the Consumer Commission, Saitama and Osaka prefectures, Chiba City and several city water bureaus, and the top-ranking articles, were read that day. The schema.org types were checked the same day with schema-dts 2.1.0.

AI answers change by day and by wording. This guide helps AI read a repair business's facts correctly; it does not guarantee that you will appear in AI answers or rank higher. It is not legal advice: check the rules for your area with a consumer affairs office or a lawyer. The examples are from Japan; the page structure applies anywhere.

What people in a hurry ask AI

Call-out repair questions arrive when someone is in a hurry and also afraid of being overcharged: "My toilet is blocked at 10 pm. Who can come now in [city]?", "I'm locked out. How much does a locksmith cost?", "How do I avoid being ripped off by a plumber?", "There's a wasp nest on my balcony. Is removal expensive?"

AI often answers with names and with warnings. The warnings come from consumer agencies and local governments. If your site does not state the call-out fee, the estimate fee, cancellation charges, a total before work, payment methods and your designation number, AI can only repeat the general warnings and has no grounds to recommend you.

Why the way you show prices matters most in this trade

On 7 October 2021, Japan's National Consumer Affairs Center reported rising complaints about "household rescue services": toilet repair, leaks, locks, pest control, water heaters and similar. Complaints rose from 2,437 in fiscal 2016 to 5,882 in fiscal 2020, and the share involving online and other electronic ads rose from 19.2% to 41.1%. One case: an ad said "from 950 yen" and the bill was several hundred thousand yen. National Consumer Affairs Center (7 October 2021, Japanese)

The Consumer Commission's opinion of 4 August 2025 puts the electronic-ad share at about 15% in fiscal 2014 and about 55% in fiscal 2024. Its survey of 700 people (March 2025) found that the deciding reasons for choosing a business were a low price in the ad (about 39%), "free estimate" in the ad (36%) and being at the top of the search results (33%). The most common problems were a high bill after the repair (37%) and being charged for an estimate advertised as free (24%). Consumer Commission opinion (PDF, Japanese)

In other words, the low price and the "free estimate" that people see first have been the entry point to trouble. AI reads these public documents, so a page that shows only a big minimum price looks like a business AI would hesitate to recommend. A page that explains how the total is set and that you can decline after the estimate is an answer to the warnings.

Claims regulators acted against, and what to write instead

Saitama Prefecture has issued orders under Japan's Act against Unjustifiable Premiums and Misleading Representations to plumbing repair businesses (dated 8 February 2024 and 5 March 2025, each with an action under the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions the same day). The problems were prices that looked lower than reality and track records with no basis. Saitama (9 February 2024) · Saitama (6 March 2025)

Seven things to put on your prices page

  1. Call-out fee: whether there is one, how much, and whether it changes by area or time. Name the places: "free within the city, ¥X to Y town".
  2. Estimate fee: the cost of finding the cause on site and writing an estimate. If declining after the estimate is free, say so.
  3. Cancellation charge: after you set off, after arrival, and after the estimate.
  4. Total before work: you give a written estimate with the work and the total including tax, and start only after the customer agrees. If extra work turns out to be needed, you stop and give a new price.
  5. Worked totals per job: for example "blocked toilet (cleared with a plunger or similar, about 30 minutes): ¥X incl. tax", with the work, time and parts as conditions and the month written. Build examples from real invoices; never invent figures.
  6. Night and holiday surcharges: hours and amount or percentage.
  7. Payment and paperwork: payment methods other than cash, and that you give a receipt and a work report.

Osaka Prefecture advises people to say clearly that they want someone to come "for an estimate" and to check beforehand whether the estimate and cancelling are charged. Osaka Prefecture FAQ (updated 1 April 2026, Japanese). If your page answers those checks first, AI can say things like "this business states that estimates are free if you decline". Door-to-door sales rules and cooling-off

In Japan, a business visiting a consumer's home and signing a contract there counts as door-to-door sales under the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions, and the consumer can cancel within 8 days of receiving the legally required document. It may not apply when the consumer called the business, but Osaka Prefecture explains that cooling-off may still be possible when the advertised price and the bill differ greatly, or when the customer was pushed into a different contract from the repair requested. Osaka Prefecture

Your site does not need a long legal explanation. State briefly what you do: "we give you the document required by law when you sign" and "the document explains cooling-off". Avoid deciding on your site which cases qualify, and point to the consumer hotline (188 in Japan, or your local equivalent). It gives AI something to read: the business itself names where to get advice.

Publish your designation number and its expiry

In Japan, work on water supply pipes and fittings is done by "designated water supply contractors" approved by each city's water bureau. Shizuoka City says work by non-designated contractors is prohibited by ordinance, and Kashiwa City warns that using one can lead to high bills and poor work. Shizuoka City (updated 10 March 2026) · Kashiwa City (updated 17 December 2025). Requirements include having a chief engineer for water supply work, and cities publish lists of designated contractors. Fukuoka City (updated 1 November 2026)

Designations expire. Under the revised Water Supply Act in force from 1 October 2019, they are renewed every five years. Chiba Prefecture · Japan Water Works Association (PDF)

AI can compare the city's public list with your site. On your about and prices pages, write per city: "Designated water supply contractor of [city], No. X (valid until [month year])". For towns where you are not designated, say so and state how you handle work there. Which jobs need a designated contractor varies by city, so write only what the city's water bureau has confirmed. Locksmiths and pest control businesses can do the same with any registration or association membership they hold (name, number, expiry), and should not mention ones they do not hold. Outside Japan, the equivalent is your licence number and the authority that issued it.

Service area and arrival times

List your service area by city name in text, not only as a map image. Do not give only your best arrival time ("as fast as 15 minutes"); give a range with conditions and a basis, such as "within the city in the daytime, usually 30 to 60 minutes (our records, [months])", and say that rush hour or weather can cause delays.

Taking calls 24 hours is not the same as attending 24 hours. Separate the hours you answer the phone from the hours someone can actually come. For hours in structured data, see Getting AI to read your opening hours, holidays and closures.

Align listing sites, search ads and your Google Business Profile

AI reads listing sites, comparison articles and your Google Business Profile as well as your site. If your name, address, phone, service area, hours or price wording differ between them, AI cannot tell which is right. Keep a list of where you are listed and align them with your site. Ads or listings that still say only "from ¥X" or "free estimate" contradict a site that states conditions carefully.

The Consumer Commission notes that much of the harm starts from search ads and advises consumers not to trust a business "just because it appears at the top of the search results" and to get several estimates where possible. Consumer Commission (PDF). Writing in a form that is easy to compare is the shortest route.

Checklist before you publish

  1. Call-out fee, estimate fee and cancellation charge are each given as an amount or "free", with conditions.
  2. The page says you give a written total including tax before work and start only after the customer agrees.
  3. Worked totals per job are shown with the work, time and month written, and no minimum price is made to stand out alone.
  4. Discounts have start and end dates, and finished ones are removed.
  5. There are no figures you cannot back up: job counts, satisfaction rates, comparisons with competitors.
  6. Designation numbers and expiry dates are written per city in text (none you do not hold).
  7. The service area is listed by city name, arrival times have conditions and a basis, and call-taking hours are separate from attending hours.
  8. The page says you give the legally required document and names a consumer advice line.
  9. The business type is Plumber or similar, and name, address, phone and hours match across the site, structured data, Google Business Profile and listings.
  10. llms.txt lists the prices, service area, about and guarantee pages. What is llms.txt

FAQ

Where should a plumber or locksmith start with AI search?

On your prices page, state the call-out fee, estimate fee and cancellation charge, and that you give a total including tax before work. Then write your designation or licence number and expiry and your service area in text, publish structured data with a type such as Plumber, and align your Google Business Profile and listing sites.

Is it wrong to write "from ¥X"?

Giving a minimum is not banned in itself, but in Saitama Prefecture's orders the problem was showing a minimum price while added work led to bills of tens to hundreds of thousands of yen. If you give a minimum, describe the job that ends at that price and show worked totals per job.

Won't detailed prices put us at a disadvantage against competitors?

It is safer to assume you will be compared. In the Consumer Commission's survey, many people who ran into trouble chose a business because of a low advertised price or "free estimate". Prices with conditions give AI grounds to describe you alongside the warnings.

Where should we write the designated contractor number?

On your about and prices pages, per city: "Designated water supply contractor of [city], No. X (valid until [month year])". Since the revised Water Supply Act in force from 1 October 2019, designations are renewed every five years, so update it when you renew.

What structured data type should a pest control business use?

schema.org has no pest control type, so use HomeAndConstructionBusiness or LocalBusiness and describe each treatment as a Service. Keep the values identical to the page text.
