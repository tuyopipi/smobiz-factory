AI search for hair and beauty salons: how to get AI to read your menu, prices and booking correctly

AI search optimization (AEO) for a hair or beauty salon comes down to publishing the facts clients ask about (your menu, prices, how long each service takes, opening hours, days closed, address and how to book) accurately and consistently, both in your page text and in schema.org HairSalon or BeautySalon structured data. That gives ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI what they need to answer "a salon near the station open on Sunday evening" or "who does keratin straightening near me" without guessing. Posting your own reviews on your site is not a shortcut to better AI or search results.

Written November 1, 2026 (JST). Information from Google, schema.org and Japanese government agencies was checked on each official page on the dates given in the text.

AI answers change from day to day and with how a question is phrased. The steps here make your salon's facts readable to AI. They do not guarantee that an AI will mention your salon or rank it anywhere.

What clients ask AI, and what AI reads

When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews to find a salon, the AI reads what it can find on the web and builds an answer. Salon questions mix place, day, time, service and price: "a hair salon near the station that is good at straightening", "a salon I can book on Sunday after 7pm", "a hairdresser that does kids' cuts", "a place that does nails and lashes on the same day".

To answer them, an AI has to be able to read, as text, where your salon is, which days and until what time it is open, which services you offer at what price and for how long, and where to book. If your menu exists only as an image, every price is "from" with no basis, your days closed differ between pages, or there is no link to booking, your salon is harder to use in an answer.

Google says there are no additional requirements and no special schema.org structured data needed to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, and that the usual basics still matter, such as making structured data match the visible text and keeping your Business Profile up to date (checked November 1, 2026). Google: AI features and your website. The work is not an AI trick: it is publishing your salon's facts correctly and consistently.

The facts a salon should publish

In structured data, a hair salon is described as a schema.org HairSalon. HairSalon sits under HealthAndBeautyBusiness, a kind of LocalBusiness, alongside BeautySalon, NailSalon and DaySpa. Google's local business documentation lists name and address as required, recommends openingHoursSpecification, telephone, url and others, and asks you to use the most specific LocalBusiness sub-type possible (checked October 29, 2026). Show prices the way local rules require. In Japan, for example, the National Tax Agency requires prices shown to consumers in advance to include consumption tax, whatever the medium (for example "11,000円（税込）"). National Tax Agency: total price display (Japanese). Where a price is a range ("from" a base price), say in a few words what it depends on (hair length, senior stylist), so neither AI nor people misread it.

What matters most is that the same fact has the same value everywhere: your site, structured data, Google Business Profile, booking sites and llms.txt. When prices or hours change, update all of them together.

Reviews: what not to add for AI

Marking up client reviews or star ratings on your own salon site will not get you review stars in Google. In 2019 Google said that self-serving reviews, meaning reviews about a business placed on that business's own website, aren't allowed for LocalBusiness and Organization, and that review snippets are not displayed for them. Google Search Central blog, September 16, 2019

Rules on undisclosed advertising differ by country, so check the ones that apply to you. In Japan, stealth marketing has been a violation of the Act against Unjustifiable Premiums and Misleading Representations since October 1, 2023, and the Consumer Affairs Agency treats reviews and posts that a business asks or instructs third parties to make as that business's advertising. Consumer Affairs Agency: stealth marketing (Japanese). Do not grow undisclosed sponsored reviews in the hope that AI will speak well of you.

What AI needs from you is not praise but facts it can check: services, prices, times and place. Claims you cannot back up, such as "No. 1 in the area", are safest left out of both your text and your markup.

Checklist before you publish

  1. Your menu is written as text, not only shown as an image.
  2. Prices are shown as local rules require, and ranges say what they depend on.
  3. Hours, days closed and last booking times match across your site, Google Business Profile and booking sites.
  4. The structured data type fits the business, such as HairSalon, BeautySalon or NailSalon, not a generic Organization.
  5. Prices and hours in the structured data match the values visible on the page.
  6. Reviews on your own site are not marked up as ratings, and no undisclosed sponsored reviews are used.
  7. llms.txt lists your salon overview, menu and prices, directions and booking pages. What is llms.txt?

Checking whether you appear in AI answers

Once the facts are in place, ask AI the questions your clients would ask (area plus service plus day or time) and see whether your salon comes up by name or URL. Answers change between runs, so ask the same questions a few times, spread over time. Booking-site pages are sometimes used as sources too, so keep the information there current as well.

FAQ

Where should a hair salon start with AI search?

Write your menu, prices, durations, opening hours, days closed, address and how to book correctly as text on your pages. Then publish the same facts as HairSalon structured data and in llms.txt, and make sure they match your Google Business Profile and booking sites.

HairSalon or BeautySalon: which should I use?

In schema.org both are kinds of HealthAndBeautyBusiness. Choose the type closest to what you do: HairSalon for cuts and color, BeautySalon for beauty treatments, NailSalon for a nail studio. Google asks you to use the most specific type possible.

If I am on booking sites, do I still need to fix my own site?

Booking-site pages can be used as sources by AI, but your own site is where you can publish your salon's information in the most detail and most accurately: service descriptions, directions and FAQ. Keeping both in agreement is the safest approach.

Will this guarantee my salon appears in ChatGPT?

No. AI answers change, and no tool or technique can guarantee a mention or a position. What you can do is make your facts easy for AI to read and keep checking whether you appear.
