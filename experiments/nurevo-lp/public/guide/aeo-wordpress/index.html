AEO for WordPress: How to Make Your Site Readable by AI

WordPress powers a huge share of the web, which makes it the easiest place to get AEO right — if you know what AI answer engines actually need. Here are the exact pieces, and how to do them on WordPress without touching code.

What AI needs from a WordPress site

AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini) read your site as raw, server-rendered data. On WordPress, three things decide whether they can read and cite you:

  1. Structured data (schema.org JSON-LD) — your business, services, products and FAQs, in a format AI parses.
  2. llms.txt — a file at your domain root telling AI what your site is and which pages matter. What is llms.txt?
  3. AI crawler access — robots.txt must allow GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and others.

The problem with doing it by hand

Each can be done manually, but it drifts: schema breaks when you change themes, llms.txt goes stale, crawler lists change. For an agency managing many client sites, per-site by hand doesn't scale.

The WordPress way: a plugin that outputs it server-side

A plugin can emit correct schema.org data, generate llms.txt, and set AI-crawler access automatically — server-side, so AI reads it even without running JavaScript. That is what Nurevo AEO does: install, connect, the plumbing is handled, with a score that shows where you stand.

FAQ

Does my theme or page builder matter for AEO?

What matters is server-rendered HTML and structured data, not the visual builder. A plugin that outputs schema server-side works regardless of theme.

Will AEO slow my site down?

No. Structured data and llms.txt are tiny text files; they don't meaningfully affect page speed.

Do I still need SEO on WordPress?
