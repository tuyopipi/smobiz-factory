AI search for dental clinics: how to get AI to read your practice's facts correctly

AI search optimization (AEO) for a dental clinic comes down to publishing the facts patients ask about (opening hours, days closed, treatments, address, phone number and how to book) accurately and consistently, both in your page text and in schema.org Dentist structured data. That gives ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI what they need to answer "which dentist near me is open on Saturday?" without guessing. Healthcare advertising rules still apply to your website, so do not add testimonials or bold claims just because the audience is a machine.

Written October 29, 2026 (JST). Information from Google, schema.org and Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare was checked on each official page that day.

AI answers change from day to day and with how a question is phrased. The steps here make your practice's facts readable to AI. They do not guarantee that an AI will mention your clinic or rank it anywhere.

What patients ask AI, and what AI reads

When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews to find a dentist, the AI reads what it can find on the web and builds an answer. The questions are usually about place, day, time and treatment: "a dentist near the station that is open on Saturday", "a family dentist that sees children", "a dental clinic with evening appointments after 7pm".

To answer them, an AI has to be able to read where your practice is, which days and hours it is open, and what it treats. If your hours exist only as an image, your days closed differ between pages, or your treatments are never written out as text, your clinic is harder to use in an answer.

Google says there are no additional requirements and no special schema.org markup needed to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, and that normal SEO basics still matter, such as making structured data match the visible text and keeping your Business Profile up to date. Google: AI features and your website. The work is not an AI trick: it is publishing your practice's facts correctly and consistently.

The facts a dental practice should publish

In structured data, a dental practice is described as a schema.org Dentist, a kind of LocalBusiness. Google's local business documentation lists name and address as required, recommends openingHoursSpecification, telephone, url and others, and asks you to use the most specific LocalBusiness sub-type possible. What matters most is that the same fact has the same value everywhere: page text, structured data, your Google Business Profile and llms.txt. When holiday or temporary closures change your hours, update all of them together.

Healthcare advertising rules: what not to add for AI

Rules differ by country, so check the ones that apply to you. In Japan, an amendment to the Medical Care Act that took effect on June 1, 2018 brought medical institutions' websites under the medical advertising rules. The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's case book on websites treats false and exaggerated claims, claims of being better than other clinics, patient testimonials and before-and-after treatment photos as examples of prohibited advertising. MHLW case book (committee material, Japanese) / 5th edition notice, March 11, 2025 (Japanese)

So do not add phrases like "No. 1 in the area" or "painless treatment", or patient reviews and testimonials, to your structured data or FAQ for the sake of AI. Structured data is your page's content written for machines: if you cannot say it on the page, do not say it in the markup.

On the other hand, the case book sets out conditions under which a website patients look up themselves may explain more, such as the content, cost and main risks and side effects of self-paid treatment, with contact details for questions. For whether a particular wording is allowed, check the official guidance or your local health authority.

Checklist before you publish

  1. Opening hours and days closed are written as text, not shown as an image.
  2. Name, address and phone match across your site and your Google Business Profile.
  3. The structured data type is Dentist, not a generic Organization.
  4. Hours in the structured data match the hours visible on the page.
  5. FAQ structured data appears only on the page where that FAQ is visible.
  6. No testimonials, before-and-after photos or comparative or exaggerated claims, in the text or in the markup.
  7. llms.txt lists your practice overview, treatments, directions and booking pages. What is llms.txt?

FAQ

Where should a dental clinic start with AI search?

Write your opening hours, days closed, address, phone and treatments correctly as text on your pages. Then publish the same facts as Dentist structured data and in llms.txt, and make sure they match your Google Business Profile.

Is Organization or LocalBusiness structured data wrong for a dentist?

Not wrong, but Google asks you to use the most specific type possible. For a dental practice that is schema.org Dentist.

Should I add patient reviews or testimonials for AI?

Check your local rules first. In Japan, clinic websites are covered by medical advertising rules, and patient testimonials are treated as prohibited advertising. Do not add them to structured data or FAQ for AI either.

Will this guarantee my practice appears in ChatGPT?

No. AI answers change, and no tool or technique can guarantee a mention or a position. What you can do is make your facts easy for AI to read and keep checking whether you appear.
