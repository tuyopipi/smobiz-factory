How to see AI Overviews and AI Mode in Search Console: reading the Generative AI performance report

Search Console's "Generative AI performance report (Search)" shows how often links to your site appeared in Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode. It has been available to all websites worldwide since August 31, 2026. It reports impressions, broken down by page, country, device and date. It does not show clicks, so read it side by side with the normal Performance report.

Written November 10, 2026. Google's behavior described here was checked that day in Search Console Help and on Google Search Central. Screens and rules can change, so check the current help pages too.

This report only covers AI features inside Google Search. How you appear in ChatGPT or Perplexity has to be checked another way (see below). And nothing you do can guarantee that an AI answer will include you or rank you anywhere. AI answers change from day to day.

What the report shows and what it does not

Google announced the report on June 3, 2026 (Search Central Blog) and first rolled it out to a subset of sites. According to the help page, it reached all websites worldwide as of August 31, 2026 (Search Console Help: Generative AI performance report (Search)). The essentials:

The help page does not describe clicks, positions or queries in this report.

Be clear about the gaps. This report does not tell you which queries triggered the AI feature, where in the answer your link appeared, or how many clicks the AI answer sent you. Clicks and impressions from Google's AI features are counted inside the normal Performance report under the Web search type (Google: AI features and your website). The Discover report is described in this help article.

Opening the report, and what to check when it is missing

  1. In Search Console, choose your property and open the Generative AI performance report (Search). The direct link is https://search.google.com/search-console/performance/search-analytics/ai (you need to be signed in).
  2. Widen the date range, for example to the last three months, and look at the impressions trend in the chart. Leave the dotted, preliminary days out of any month-to-month comparison.
  3. In the table's Pages tab, see which pages were shown in AI features. Pages are reported by their final URL after redirects.
  4. Use the Countries and Devices tabs to see where and on what devices you appear. For a local business, check the share coming from your own country.
  5. Switch the search type filter to "Web: multimodal" to see whether you appear in image-based searches. This matters most for sites with many product, food or building photos.

If the report does not appear or is empty, check two things. The common reason, according to Google, is that your site has not yet had enough impressions in generative AI features. The other is that the site has been excluded with the Search generative AI control described below (help page).

Also note that from August 13 to 17, 2026, a logging problem at Google reduced the impressions in this report. Google says the missing data was later restored (Search Console data anomalies). When an old dip looks odd, check that page first.

How impressions are counted, and why the totals differ

The report counts differently depending on where you look. Compare numbers without knowing this and they will not add up.

Based on Search Console Help, Generative AI performance report (Search).
Where you lookCounted byExample: one AI answer shows links to two of your pages
Chart totalProperty (the whole site)1
Table, Pages tabPage1 for page A and 1 for page B (2 in total)

So the sum of the Pages table can be higher than the chart total. In a report, write the site-wide impressions (chart) and the per-page impressions (table) separately and never add them together. As in the normal report, the table is limited to 1,000 rows.

Read it next to the normal Performance report

Because the report only has impressions, on its own it can tell you little more than "more impressions than last month". Put it next to the normal Performance report (search type: Web) for the same pages and the same dates, and you can read much more.

The numbers cannot prove a cause. Use these as hypotheses to test.
AI feature impressionsClicks in the normal reportWhat it may mean, and what to do next
UpUp or flatYou are being found both in AI features and in normal results. Keep the facts on that page (prices, hours) current
UpDownThe AI answer may be satisfying the search, so fewer people click. Check whether the page offers what a summary cannot: booking, quotes, photos, case studies
Low or noneHighStrong in normal results but rarely linked in AI features. Check for text that answers the question directly, and structured data that matches the visible page

Google says there are no additional requirements or special optimizations for appearing in AI features, and that a page must be indexed and eligible to be shown in Google Search with a snippet (Google: AI features and your website). For a page with few AI impressions, checking its state in normal search is the quickest start.

Monthly checklist

  1. Fix the date range to last month (Pacific Time dates) and record the chart's impressions. Leave out preliminary days
  2. Record the top ten pages in the Pages table and their change from the previous month
  3. For the same ten pages, record clicks and impressions from the normal Performance report (Web) next to them
  4. Look for large shifts in the country and device split
  5. Check the data anomalies page for any logging issue Google reported that month
  6. Confirm the Search generative AI control is set as intended (normally Include, or inherited from the parent)
  7. Record how you appear in AI outside Google, such as ChatGPT and Perplexity, another way (see below)

Five columns are enough for the log: month, AI feature impressions (site), top pages, those pages' Web clicks and impressions, and notes (site changes, known issues). Writing down site changes such as new pages, structured data fixes or a theme change makes it much easier to explain a rise or a fall later.

The Search generative AI control: what excluding does

Alongside the report, Google added a setting that stops your site from appearing in its AI features. It is under Settings > Search generative AI in Search Console, and it was available to all websites worldwide as of August 31, 2026 (Search Console Help: Search generative AI control).

Based on Search Console Help (Search generative AI control) and Google Search Central (AI features and your website).
Option or methodWhat happens
Include (default)Your links and content can be used in AI Overviews, AI Mode and generative AI features in Discover
ExcludeYour links and content no longer appear in those features and are not used as input to generate a response. You also get no impressions or traffic from them
Inherit control from parentFollows the setting of a parent property such as a domain property (the default when a parent exists)
Google-Extended (robots.txt)Limits use of your content for AI training. The Search generative AI control does not affect training
noindexRemoves the page from Google Search altogether
nosnippet, data-nosnippet, max-snippetLimit how much of the page can be used as a preview in results and AI features

Google says the control is not used as a ranking or inclusion signal for other parts of Search. Exclusion takes effect within one to two days, and some content can take longer because of caching.

For most businesses, AI features are one more way for customers to find them. Exclude only for a clear reason, such as a site built mainly around members-only content or text that misleads when summarized. If only some passages should stay out of summaries, data-nosnippet on those passages is an alternative to excluding the whole site.

Steps in WordPress

  1. Register a domain property: verify ownership with a DNS TXT record. A domain property's Search generative AI setting is inherited by URL-prefix properties under it, so www and non-www, http and https are managed in one place.
  2. Make sure indexing is not blocked: in WordPress, Settings > Reading has a "Search engine visibility" option. When it is ticked, WordPress asks search engines not to index the site. Check it after a migration or a launch.
  3. Make sure robots.txt does not block Googlebot: open /robots.txt in a browser and look for Disallow: / under Googlebot or under User-agent: *.
  4. Keep a passage out of previews with data-nosnippet: in the block editor, choose "Edit as HTML" on that block and add the data-nosnippet attribute to the element around it. To stop a whole page, use your SEO plugin's per-page setting, such as noindex.
  5. Keep structured data and visible text in agreement: hours, prices and addresses should have the same values in the page text and in the structured data. A mismatch is a common source of wrong AI answers.

FAQ

Where is the Generative AI performance report?

Search Console Help gives a direct link to it (search.google.com/search-console/performance/search-analytics/ai). It was available to all websites worldwide as of August 31, 2026. If you cannot see it, your site may not have enough impressions in AI features yet, or it may be excluded with the Search generative AI control.

Can I see clicks from AI Overviews?

The Generative AI performance report shows impressions. Clicks and impressions from Google's AI features are included in the normal Performance report under the Web search type, but you cannot isolate the clicks that came from AI features. The practical approach is to compare AI feature impressions with normal clicks page by page.

Why does the chart total differ from the Pages table?

The chart counts by property and the Pages table counts by page. If one AI answer shows two of your pages, the chart counts one impression and the table counts one for each page. Do not add the two together in a report.

Will excluding my site with the Search generative AI control hurt my rankings?

Google says the control is not used as a ranking or inclusion signal for other parts of Search. But once excluded, you get no impressions or traffic from AI Overviews, AI Mode or generative AI features in Discover. To limit AI training, use Google-Extended rather than this control.

Does Search Console show ChatGPT or Perplexity?

No. Search Console only has Google's data. Check ChatGPT and Perplexity separately with your analytics referrers, by asking fixed questions regularly, or with a measurement tool.

Is there a special setting that increases AI impressions?

Google says there are no additional requirements or special optimizations for AI features. Getting the basics right is the quickest route: pages that are indexed and can show a snippet, text that answers the question directly, and structured data that matches the visible page. No method guarantees that you will be shown.
