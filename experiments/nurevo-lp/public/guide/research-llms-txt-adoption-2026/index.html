llms.txt adoption on 15,838 web design agency websites in 25 countries (November 2026)

We checked /llms.txt on 15,838 websites of web design agencies. 13.4% of agency sites in Japan (333 of 2,486) and 26.4% of those outside Japan (3,522 of 13,352) had an llms.txt. Among the 25 countries and territories with at least 30 reachable sites, Japan had the lowest share.

Published 2026-11-12. Data aggregated 2026-11-12. Research by Nurevo AEO (Bestie.合同会社). Nurevo sells AI search optimization (AEO) tools, including one that publishes an llms.txt, and so has an interest in this topic.

Key findings

  1. 26.4% of web design agency websites outside Japan (3,522 of 13,352) and 13.4% in Japan (333 of 2,486) have an llms.txt. On WordPress sites the shares are 33.1% and 14.0%.
  2. Among 25 countries and territories with 30+ reachable sites, Singapore (38.8%), India (36.6%) and Brazil (34.1%) lead; the United States is at 23.1%, the United Kingdom at 21.5% and Japan at 13.4%.
  3. Outside Japan, WordPress agency sites running AIOSEO have an llms.txt in 51.1% of cases, Rank Math 40.8% and Yoast SEO 30.8%, against 24.2% where no SEO plugin is detected. The full-text llms-full.txt is rare: 1.8% outside Japan, 0.6% in Japan.

About the survey

  1. Population: web design agencies' own websites. 3,352 in Japan that we could confirm are agencies, and 20,335 from our lists of agencies elsewhere (23,687 in total). This is not a sample of all business websites.
  2. Reached: the home page could be fetched for 15,838 sites (2,486 in Japan, 13,352 elsewhere). All results refer to these sites.
  3. Fetched: each site's home page, /robots.txt, /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt, once each (User-Agent: NurevoSurvey/1.0), between early November and 2026-11-12, aggregated on 2026-11-12.
  4. How things were judged: an llms.txt counts if /llms.txt returns status 200 with non-HTML text longer than 20 characters (the same for llms-full.txt). If the home page redirects, the root of the final domain is checked. WordPress and SEO plugins are identified from the home page HTML. The country is the country of the list the site came from.
  5. This continues the run behind our overview report published 2026-11-10 (aggregated 2026-11-05, 2,357 Japanese sites), with more sites added since. The small difference for Japan (13.6% → 13.4%) comes from the larger population, not from changes on the same sites.

Results: Japan and elsewhere

WordPress sites are much more likely to have an llms.txt; on other platforms the share is under 10% both in Japan and elsewhere. The gap between Japan and other countries is mostly on WordPress sites (14.0% vs 33.1%).

Relationship with other items (Japan)

18.4% of sites with JSON-LD on the home page have an llms.txt, against 2.1% of those without. Among the 96 sites with FAQPage structured data, 42.7% do. Sites that add one AI-oriented item tend to add others. This is co-occurrence only and says nothing about cause.

How long the files are

The median llms.txt among the 333 Japanese sites is 93 lines; none is 10 lines or shorter, and 72 (21.6%) run to 301 lines or more. Outside Japan the median is 65 lines. We did not assess content quality or format.

Limitations

  1. Only the home page and /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt at the domain root were checked. An llms.txt placed elsewhere counts as missing.
  2. We checked only whether an llms.txt exists, not its quality, format, or whether AI services actually read it.
  3. The population is web design agencies' own websites, not businesses in general. The lists are our own; how they were built and their size differ by country, and they do not cover every agency. Treat country comparisons as indicative.
  4. One fetch per site: sites whose home page could not be reached (including temporary errors and bot protection) are excluded.

Our reading (opinion)

What follows is our opinion, not a finding. llms.txt is a fairly new proposal for telling AI systems what a site is about, and how the major AI services use it is not yet clear. Having one does not guarantee appearing in AI answers (in another of our studies, on-site signals showed no relationship with appearance). Still, it is useful to know that agencies outside Japan already put it on their own sites at about twice the rate of Japanese agencies.

FAQ

How widely is llms.txt adopted?

In this survey of web design agencies' own websites, 26.4% outside Japan (3,522 of 13,352) and 13.4% in Japan (333 of 2,486) had an llms.txt (November 2026).

Which countries have the highest llms.txt adoption?

Do sites with an SEO plugin have an llms.txt more often?
