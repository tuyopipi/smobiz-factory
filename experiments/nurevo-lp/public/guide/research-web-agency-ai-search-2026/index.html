AI search readiness of 2,357 Japanese web agency websites (November 2026 survey)

We fetched the home page, robots.txt and llms.txt of 2,357 websites belonging to web design and development agencies in Japan. 13.6% have an llms.txt file, 1.3% block AI crawlers in robots.txt, and 68.9% have JSON-LD structured data. AI crawlers are rarely blocked, but files and markup written for AI (llms.txt, FAQ structured data) are still uncommon.

Published 2026-11-05. Data aggregated 2026-11-05. Research and writing: Nurevo AEO (Bestie. LLC). Nurevo makes AI-search tools, so we have an interest in this topic.

Key findings

  1. 13.6% of Japanese web agency websites have an llms.txt file (321 of 2,357).
  2. 1.3% block at least one major AI crawler in robots.txt (28 of the 2,158 sites with a robots.txt). 1.3% block GPTBot; none block OAI-SearchBot.
  3. 68.9% have JSON-LD structured data (1,624 sites); 1.7% of those (27) contain JSON-LD that does not parse. Only 3.9% have FAQPage markup.

Methodology

  1. Population: the company websites of web agencies in Japan: 2,756 Japanese companies from a list of web agencies we compiled (one domain counted once).
  2. Reached: 2,357 sites (home page returned HTML successfully; the rest failed to connect or returned errors). Percentages are of these 2,357 unless stated otherwise.
  3. Aggregated: 2026-11-05 (pages fetched within the week before).
  4. Fetched: each site's home page, /robots.txt, /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt, once each (User-Agent: NurevoSurvey/1.0).
  5. Rules: llms.txt and llms-full.txt count as present when they return status 200, are not HTML and have more than 20 characters. Structured data: JSON-LD (application/ld+json) on the home page was parsed and its @type values counted. AI crawler blocking: robots.txt was parsed and each bot checked for whether it may fetch the home page (/) (falling back to User-agent: * when the bot is not named; longest match wins). WordPress: detected from wp-content, wp-includes or the generator tag.
  6. Limits: home pages only, so structured data on inner pages is not counted. robots.txt is judged for / only; blocks on some directories are not counted. Blocking at the firewall or server level is not visible. These are web agencies' own websites, not Japanese business websites in general.

Finding 5: distribution of the AI readiness score

We counted how many of six items each site meets, as an "AI readiness score" from 0 to 6: (1) has llms.txt, (2) has JSON-LD, (3) has Organization or LocalBusiness markup, (4) has a robots.txt that blocks none of GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and OAI-SearchBot, (5) has a meta description, (6) has a canonical link. The average is 4.0, and 10.5% of sites score 6. This is a simple measure we defined for this survey; it does not predict whether a site appears in AI answers.

For reference: web agencies outside Japan (WordPress sites)

Measured the same way, 10,033 WordPress websites of web agencies outside Japan show llms.txt on 33.1%, JSON-LD on 92.4%, at least one AI crawler blocked on 4.3% (of sites with robots.txt), and an average AI readiness score of 4.9. Compared with Japanese WordPress agency sites (14.3%, 72.5%, 1.0%, 4.2), agencies elsewhere use llms.txt and structured data more, and block AI crawlers slightly more often. The non-Japanese lists were collected differently from country to country, so country comparisons will be covered in separate reports.

What this suggests (our opinion from here on)

The facts: Japanese web agency websites rarely block AI crawlers and have their classic SEO settings in place, while items that organise information for AI, such as llms.txt and FAQPage markup, remain uncommon.

In our view, this means agencies have plenty of room to try AI-search measures on their own sites before offering them to clients. Having llms.txt or structured data does not guarantee appearing in AI answers, though. In a separate study we found no relationship between on-site measures and appearing in Perplexity's answers (AEO signals and AI answers study). We see on-site work as the groundwork that lets AI read a company's facts correctly.

FAQ

How many Japanese web agency websites use llms.txt?

In our November 2026 survey, 13.6% (321) of 2,357 Japanese web agency websites had llms.txt. 0.6% had llms-full.txt.

Do many agency websites block AI crawlers?

Very few. Of the 2,158 sites with a robots.txt, 1.3% (28) blocked at least one major AI crawler. None blocked the OAI-SearchBot search crawler.

Do these figures apply to all Japanese business websites?

Not necessarily. The survey covers web agencies' own websites and checks only the home page, robots.txt and llms.txt. Other business websites may differ.
